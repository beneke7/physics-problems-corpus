**3. Water Droplet Touching a Glass Rod in Space**

Inside the cabin of a freely orbiting spacecraft, a water sphere approximately 4 cm in diameter floats motionless. Nearby, a thin glass rod approximately 8 cm long with a circular cross-section and rounded ends floats freely. We bring one end of the rod into very gentle contact with the „water droplet". Sketch the shape that the water takes on!

(Károlyházy Frigyes)
