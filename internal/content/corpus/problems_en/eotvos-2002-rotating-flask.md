**2. Closed Flask**

A closed flask contains a small amount of water. With the flask's mouth pointing downward, the water stands at approximately 5 cm height in the flask's neck. (Figure 4 shows the internal dimensions.)

We then set the flask into uniform rotation about its vertical axis such that it makes three complete revolutions per second. We ensure that the temperature of the flask walls is the same everywhere. After a sufficiently long time, equilibrium is established.

Draw schematically how the water is distributed in the flask at this point!
