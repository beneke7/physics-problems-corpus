**1. Problem. Temperature of Compressed Gases.**

A thermally insulated cylindrical vessel is divided by a stationary, thermally conducting wall into two identical cylindrical sections. One section contains helium gas, the other contains oxygen gas of the same amount (number of moles). Both gases have initial temperature $T_{0}$ and initial volume $V_{0}$. One end of the vessel is sealed with an easily movable, thermally insulating piston, which can change the volume of the helium-filled section. Determine the final temperature of the gases in the cylinder after the piston is slowly moved to reduce the helium gas volume to $V_{0}/2$!
