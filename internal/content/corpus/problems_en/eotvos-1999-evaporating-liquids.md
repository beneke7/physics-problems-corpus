**2. Two differently shaped flasks.**

Two otherwise identical flasks differ in that one has a straight neck, and the other has a bent neck that goes downward. Identical quantities of

a) water and
b) ether

are poured into the flasks, ensuring that the temperature of the liquid is

100 °C in case a) and
34.6 °C in case b).

From which flask does the liquid evaporate first in case a) and in case b)?
