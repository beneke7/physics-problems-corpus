In a vacuum chamber, a long, straight wire with very good electrical conductivity carries a current of 10 A. Electrons with initial speed $v_0$ are launched from a point at distance $r_0$ from the wire, directed toward the wire and perpendicular to it. However, these electrons are only able to approach the wire to a distance of $r_0 / 2$. What could be the value of $v_0$? (The effect of Earth's magnetic field can be neglected.)
