There is a hole in the middle of a 1-meter-high table top. Near the hole on the table surface, we place a thin 1-meter-long gold chain loosely. We pull one end of the chain slightly through the hole and then release it. The chain slides through the hole with ever-increasing speed. (We can assume that the chain does not tangle. Friction and air resistance are negligible.)

In how much time does each end of the chain reach the ground?
