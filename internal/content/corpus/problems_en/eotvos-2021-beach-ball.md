**3. Problem. Submerged Beach Ball.**

An inflatable beach ball is made of light, thin, very flexible but non-stretchable plastic. When fully inflated, the ball is almost precisely spherical in shape, with radius 20 cm. In an experiment, we inflate the ball to half its volume with air, then gradually push it underwater using a horizontally held large flat plate, until it completely submerges in the water. Sketch what shape the underwater-pressed ball takes! If possible, determine the numerical values of the relevant dimensions of the shape!
