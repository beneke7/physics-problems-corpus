## Problem 1

An easily moving piston initially divides a thermally insulated horizontal axis cylinder into two parts of equal volume, $V_{0}$. In both parts of the cylinder, there exists an ideal monatomic gas with pressure $p_{0}$. The cylinder's initial temperature is $2 T_{0}$ in the left-hand section of the piston and $T_{0}$ in the right-hand section of the piston. The piston is moderately conductive and its heat parameter is characterized by $\alpha$, i.e., in the case of a temperature difference $\Delta T$, a heat flux $\alpha \Delta T$ is flowing through the cylinder per unit time.

(a) What will be the volume, temperature, and pressure in each section after a long period of time?

(b) Give as a function of time, the volume of the gases $V_{1}(t)$ and $V_{2}(t)$ in each section!
