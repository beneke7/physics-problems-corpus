**Problem 1. Cooling Cup**

We wash a 400 g cup with a 6 cm diameter in 45°C water for a long time, then remove it from the water, place it upside down (mouth-down) on a horizontal, flat surface, and let it cool. (The room temperature is 20°C.)

With what force can we move the cup perpendicular to the surface?
