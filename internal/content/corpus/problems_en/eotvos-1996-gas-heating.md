**Problem 2. Gas Heating and Cooling in a Cylinder**

A closed cylindrical vessel of height 3 dm contains air at temperature 300 K and pressure $10^{5} \mathrm{~Pa}$. By cooling and heating from outside, we lower the temperature of the base to 270 K and raise the temperature of the top to 330 K, and subsequently maintain them continuously at these temperatures. (The sidewall of the vessel is thermally insulated.)

a) Does the pressure of the gas change compared to the original state?
b) Estimate by how much the center of mass of the enclosed gas shifts!
