**1. Problem. Air Bubble in a Rotating Container**

A large, sealed cylindrical container is filled with water at room temperature and pressure. It is in zero gravity and contains an air bubble with a volume of $V = 1 \mathrm{~cm}^{3}$. The container is rotated gently around its axis of symmetry until it reaches a constant angular velocity of $\omega = 300 \mathrm{~s}^{-1}$. What shape does the air bubble take then? Find the characteristic dimensions of the bubble. The surface tension of water is $\alpha = 0.07 \mathrm{~N/m}$.
