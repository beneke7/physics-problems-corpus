**Problem 2. Lander with Legs on Inclined Plane**

A two-legged spacecraft shown in the figure has landed on an inclined plane with inclination angle $\alpha$. The wide legs of the spacecraft contact the ground along two parallel segments perpendicular to both the line of steepest descent of the incline and the plane of the figure. The coefficient of friction between the legs and the incline is $\mu$. The center of mass of the entire spacecraft is at distance $h$ from the ground, and the legs are initially separated by distance $a$. Using an internal mechanism, the spacecraft begins to push its legs apart as shown in the figure (while the legs remain perpendicular to the ground at all times).

Which leg will slip first on the incline?
