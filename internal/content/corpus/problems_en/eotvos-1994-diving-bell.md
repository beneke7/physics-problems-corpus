**1. Problem. Diving Bell.**

A diving bell with a volume of $1 \mathrm{~m}^{3}$ is submerged to a depth of 20 m in a lake and fills with water. From a boat floating on the surface, we pump air into the space below the bell through a thin tube. (The bell is heavy, so it does not rise even then.) There is no significant temperature difference between the air and the water.

| What is the minimum work performed by the compressor to displace the $1 \mathrm{~m}^{3}$ of water?

*Frigyes Károlyházy*
