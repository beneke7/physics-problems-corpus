On the evening of September 16, 1997, a lunar eclipse could be observed in Hungary. The total phase of the lunar eclipse lasted from 9:15 to 10:15, that is, during this time the Moon was completely within Earth's umbral shadow.

a) Why does a lunar eclipse generally last longer than a solar eclipse?

b) Assuming that the Moon orbits Earth and Earth orbits the Sun in circular orbits, and neglecting the optical effects of Earth's atmosphere, determine the maximum duration of a total lunar eclipse. From Earth, both the Moon and the Sun subtend an angular size of 0.5°; from the Moon, Earth subtends an angular size of 1.83°. (How would the result be affected if we took into account that the mentioned orbits are ellipses rather than circles?)

c) In reality, during a total lunar eclipse, the Moon does not completely disappear from the sky but glows faintly in a reddish color. Why is this?
