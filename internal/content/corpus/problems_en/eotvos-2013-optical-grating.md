**2. A peculiar optical grating.**

The slits in a peculiar optical grating are not equally spaced: the distances
between neighbouring slits alternate between $30\ \mu\mathrm{m}$ and
$90\ \mu\mathrm{m}$. What diffraction pattern forms on a screen placed $2\ \mathrm{m}$
away when the grating is illuminated normally by laser light of wavelength
$660\ \mathrm{nm}$? Sketch the intensity distribution on the screen. (All slits have
the same width, which is much smaller than the distances between them.)
