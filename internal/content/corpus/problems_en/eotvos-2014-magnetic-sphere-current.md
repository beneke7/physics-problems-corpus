A thin-walled copper sphere of radius $R$ is placed on an insulating stand. A long, radial, straight conductor brings current of strength $I$ into the sphere at one point, and a perpendicular radial conductor extracts it at another point.

What magnetic field is formed inside the sphere and outside the sphere? In particular, what is the magnitude of the magnetic induction vector at point $P$, located halfway between the current entry and exit points, at a distance just outside the sphere's surface?
