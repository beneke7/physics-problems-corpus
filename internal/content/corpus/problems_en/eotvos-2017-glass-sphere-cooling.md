**3. Glass sphere cooling**

A solid, homogeneous glass sphere with radius 30 mm has been immersed in boiling water for a very long time. We suddenly immerse the sphere in a vessel filled with ice water for 30 seconds, then remove it and place it in a thermally insulated container. (The water drops are quickly wiped off.) Estimate what the equilibrium temperature of the glass sphere will be after a long time.

Additional data: The density of glass is $2500 \mathrm{~kg}/\mathrm{m}^{3}$, its specific heat capacity is $830 \mathrm{~J}/(\mathrm{kg} \mathrm{~K})$, and its thermal conductivity is $0.95 \mathrm{~W}/(\mathrm{m} \mathrm{~K})$.
