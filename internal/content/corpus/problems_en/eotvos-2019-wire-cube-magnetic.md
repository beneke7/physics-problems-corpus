## Problem 2

Each edge of a cube is made of the same wire which has resistance $R$. The cube is immersed into a homogeneous induced magnetic field $B_{0}$ which is reduced at a constant rate to zero in a time $\tau$. What is the Joule heat generated during the process if the magnetic induction vector forms an acute angle $\alpha, \beta$, and $\gamma$ respectively with the edges of the cube meeting at the vertex? (Note: $\cos ^{2} \alpha+\cos ^{2} \beta+\cos ^{2} \gamma=1$.)
