**3. Optical grating.**

Monochromatic light is incident perpendicular to a diffraction grating. The grating, with adjacent slits spaced a distance $d$ apart, is somewhat unconventional: wider and narrower slits alternate. (For example, the width of the odd-numbered slits is $a$, that of the even-numbered ones is $b$, where $b < a$ and both are much smaller than $d$.) This property of the grating manifests itself in the diffraction pattern in a characteristic, easily noticeable way. How?

Sketch the diffraction pattern if $b \ll a$ and if $b \approx a$!
