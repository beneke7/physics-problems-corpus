**3. Two Charged Hemispherical Shells**

Two insulating hemispherical shells (for example, two halves of a ping-pong ball) are placed in close proximity to each other as shown in figure 8.a, concentrically. One is charged with charge $Q$, the other with charge $q$, uniformly distributed.

a) What force do the two bodies exert on each other?

b) Does the result change if one of the hemispherical shells has only half the radius?

(Problem based on: Gagik Grigorjan [Armenia])
