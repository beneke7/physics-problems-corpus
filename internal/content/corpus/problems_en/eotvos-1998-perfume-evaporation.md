**2. Perfume evaporation**

There are 1 and 2 cubic centimetres of perfume in two straight, vertically test
tubes of identical cross-section, 20 cm and 40 cm long, respectively, and open
at the top. Approximately how many times longer does it take the perfume to
completely evaporate from the second test tube than from the first? Is the
answer modified if the tops of the test tubes are closed, only leaving a small
(identical) hole on each cover?
