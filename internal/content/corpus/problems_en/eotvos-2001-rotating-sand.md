A cylindrical closed container containing 100 kg of sand is rotating uniformly (in a horizontal plane) about its (horizontal) long axis, at a rotation rate of 0.5 s$^{-1}$. The internal diameter and length of the container are both 1 m, and the walls are rough.

Estimate how much the temperature of the sand increases during a 10 minute period, neglecting the heat escaping through the walls!
