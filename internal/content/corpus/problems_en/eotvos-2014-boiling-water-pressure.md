András, Bence, and Csaba are participating in a week-long cycling trip. Every morning for breakfast they drink tea; they boil the tea water in their own metal mugs over a (pressure-relief valve-free) butane gas canister flame. As the tour comes to an end, they make an interesting observation: boiling the tea water takes noticeably more time than at the beginning of the tour.

According to András, there is nothing special about this: as the amount of gas in the canister decreases, so does the pressure, and thus the gas flame burns more weakly. Bence points out that there is also liquid in the canister, so the gas pressure always equals the saturation vapor pressure regardless of the amount of gas. According to him, the pressure decreased because the liquid has already completely run out from the canister. Csaba then shakes the canister and is surprised to find that there is still some liquid inside.

What could be the cause of the increased boiling time?
