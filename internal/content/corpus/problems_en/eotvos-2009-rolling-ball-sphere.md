**1. Problem. Rolling Ball in a Plexiglass Sphere.**

A solid rubber ball of radius $r$ can roll without slipping inside a thin-walled plexiglass sphere of radius $R$ with a rough interior surface. The sphere can be rotated about a horizontal fixed axis passing through its center.

a) What is the period of small-amplitude oscillations of the ball inside the sphere, if the sphere is stationary? Or if the sphere rotates uniformly? How will a ball that starts at rest move in an experiment where the sphere is spun up with a constant angular acceleration that is small compared to $g/R$?

b) If the sphere is spun to high speed and then suddenly stopped, the ball, which was stationary at the bottom, after a very short time rolls purely again and can even roll up to the top of the sphere. What is the minimum angular velocity to which the sphere must be spun for this to occur?

In all cases, the center of mass of the ball moves in a vertical plane.

*(Gyula Honyek)*
