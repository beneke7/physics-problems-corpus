**1. The Elastic Behavior of a Balloon Characterized by the Elastic Energy of the Material.**

The elastic behavior of a balloon can be characterized by the elastic energy of its wall material. When a well-approximated spherical balloon changes its size to $\lambda$ times the unstretched size, the elastic energy of the balloon grows in proportion to the expression $2 \lambda^{2}+\lambda^{-4}-3$ until, finally, at around $\lambda \approx 3$, the balloon bursts.
