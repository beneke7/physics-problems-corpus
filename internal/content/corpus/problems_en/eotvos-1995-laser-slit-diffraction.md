**Problem 3. Laser Light Diffraction Through a Rotating Slit.**

We illuminate the central region of a vertical, very narrow slit with a narrow, horizontal laser beam from a laser.

a) What do we see on a screen placed behind the slit, perpendicular to the direction of the laser beam?

b) How does the image on the screen change if we rotate the slit by an angle $\varphi$ about its horizontal midline? (Let $\varphi=45^{\circ}$ as an example.)

(The slit can be considered as a series of very closely spaced tiny holes, equally spaced from each other. The screen is far enough from the slit.)

*Gyula Radnai*
