A coil with a large iron core, high self-inductance, and negligible ohmic resistance has its ends connected through a light bulb rated for voltage $U$. When an AC voltage with effective value U/2 is applied between points A and B, the bulb glows very dimly.

![figure](sources/eotvos/figures/eotvos-2007-autotransformer-induction-fig1.jpg)

Since there is also a tap C at the middle of the coil, we attempt to connect the voltage source terminals to points A and C instead. Does the current through the bulb change in magnitude, and if so, how? In the figure we have marked the instantaneous current direction $I(t)$ in the main branch. How does the current flow through the coil at the same time?
