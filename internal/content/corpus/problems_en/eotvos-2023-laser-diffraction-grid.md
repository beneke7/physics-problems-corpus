**3. Problem. Diffraction on a Modified Square Grid**

An opaque plate has identical small holes arranged in a regular square grid. When the plate is illuminated perpendicularly with monochromatic laser light whose wavelength is much larger than the grid constant, we observe bright spots on a distant screen arranged in a regular square grid, each with intensity $I_{0}$.

How does the diffraction pattern change if every second hole in every second row of the plate is covered, as shown in the figure? What will be the intensity of the individual bright spots?
