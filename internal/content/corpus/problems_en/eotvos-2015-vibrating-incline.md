**Problem 1. Vibrating Incline.**

A rigid wooden board of length $L=6 \mathrm{~m}$ makes a constant angle $\alpha=10^{\circ}$ with the horizontal. A small block is placed at the top of the incline. The board is made to oscillate harmoniously in a direction parallel to the slope with amplitude $A=1 \mathrm{~mm}$ and angular frequency $\omega=500 \mathrm{~s}^{-1}$. In how much time does the block reach the bottom of the incline? (The coefficients of sliding and static friction are both $\mu=0.4$; the block does not tip over during motion.)
