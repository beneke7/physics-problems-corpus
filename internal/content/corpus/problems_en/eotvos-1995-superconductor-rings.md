**Problem 2. Superconducting Rings in a Magnetic Field.**

Two thin, concentric superconducting rings are placed in a homogeneous magnetic field perpendicular to their plane. The magnitude of the magnetic induction vector is $B_0$, and its direction is into the page (as shown in the figure). The radius of the inner ring is much smaller than that of the outer ring ($R_1 \ll R_2$). The inductance of each ring is $L_1$ and $L_2$ respectively, and mutual inductance between them is not negligible.

**What magnitude and direction of currents are induced in each ring if the external magnetic field is switched off?**
