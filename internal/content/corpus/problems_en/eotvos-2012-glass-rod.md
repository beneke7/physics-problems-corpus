**2. Cylindrical glass rod as a lens.**

A cylindrical glass rod, 10 cm long and 2 cm in diameter, has both of its convex ends hemispherical. Along the rod's axis, at what distance from one end should we place a point light source in air, if we want the rays leaving from the other end of the rod, making a small angle with the axis, to converge at a distance from that end that is

**a)** equal to the source distance,
**b)** twice as large?

The refractive index of glass with respect to air is 1.5.
