**2. Magnetic Field of Two Parallel Conductors**

Two infinite long, thin parallel straight wires separated by distance $d$ carry equal magnitude currents in opposite directions. The lines of magnetic induction lie in planes perpendicular to the wires. Select an arbitrary point $P$ in one such plane and investigate whether the line of magnetic induction passing through it is circular in shape.

(Radnai Gyula)
