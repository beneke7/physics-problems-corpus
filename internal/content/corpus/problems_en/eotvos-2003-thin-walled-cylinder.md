**1. Thin-Walled Cylinder**

A thin-walled cylinder rolls without slipping down an incline at an angle $\alpha=10^{\circ}$. The mass of the cylinder's lateral surface is $M$, and the mass of the circular end caps is negligible. Inside the cylinder there is mercury of mass $m$. (Friction between the cylinder and the mercury is negligible.)

The surface of the mercury makes an angle $\varphi$ with the horizontal. Determine this angle $\varphi$ for the cases:

a) $M \ll m$;

b) $M=m$.

(Balogh Péter)
