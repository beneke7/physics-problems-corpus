**3. Interlocked toroidal coils.**

Two toroidal coils of identical size, differing only in their number of turns, uniformly wound with $N_1$ and $N_2$ turns ($>N_1$), are interlocked as shown in the figure. (The planes of their middle circles are perpendicular to each other.)

**a)** Between which coil terminals is a larger voltage induced if an alternating current of given effective magnitude and frequency flows through the other coil?

**b)** An alternating network voltage of effective value $U_{\text{eff}}$ is connected to the $N_1$-turn coil, and an ideal voltmeter is connected across the terminals of the other ($N_2$-turn) coil. What effective voltage does the meter read? Let $N_1=100$, $N_2=900$, $U_{\text{eff}}=230$ V, for example.
