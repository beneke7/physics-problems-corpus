**2. Vapor pressure of water.**

Boil water in a test tube with an open top. Just before the last few drops boil away, the test tube is suddenly sealed airtight. Then the temperature at the top of the test tube is slowly raised to 200°C, while ensuring — by cooling if necessary, and by heating if necessary — that the temperature at the bottom of the test tube remains at 100°C.

What will be the vapor pressure in the test tube?

*Frigyes Károlyházy*
