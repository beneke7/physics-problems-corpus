**Problem 3. Charged Particles in a Magnetic Field**

Two point particles with identical mass $m$ and charges $+q$ and $-q$ are initially at rest at distance $d$ from each other. If we release both particles simultaneously, they collide after some time. If we repeat the experiment in the presence of a sufficiently strong uniform magnetic field perpendicular to the line connecting the particles, then the particles do not collide.

a) What is the minimum magnetic induction value required to prevent collision?

b) What is the minimum distance to which the particles approach each other in this case?
