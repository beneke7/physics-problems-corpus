**3. In this task, we examine the motion of electrons in a homogeneous magnetic field $B$, in a plane perpendicular to the magnetic field lines.**

(The electron is considered a classical point mass with mass $m$ and charge $-e$, on which only electric and magnetic forces act.)

**a)** Two electrons are initially at rest at a distance $d$ apart. Under certain conditions, we can give them initial velocities in opposite directions so that their distance does not change during the motion. Find the initial speed $v$ of the electrons, and find the minimum distance $d_{\min}$ for such motion to be possible.

**b)** Show that the distance $d$ can remain constant even if only one of the electrons is given an initial velocity. In this case, what is the minimum distance $d_{\min}$ at which such a motion can still occur? When $d = d_{\min}$, sketch the motion of the electrons, and find when the electron with the initial velocity first comes to rest momentarily.
