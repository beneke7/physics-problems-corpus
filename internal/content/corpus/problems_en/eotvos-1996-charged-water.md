**Problem 3. Charged Plastic Ball Above Salty Water**

A plastic ball of diameter 1 cm hangs from an insulating thread. Its surface carries an electric charge of $10^{-8} \mathrm{C}$, uniformly distributed. We place the ball above a broad, large bowl of salty water such that its lowest point is 1 cm above the water surface. The surface of the water beneath the ball rises slightly. How large is this rise? (The role of surface tension may be neglected; the density of the salty water may be taken as $1000 \mathrm{~kg/m^{3}}$.)
