**Problem 3. Solenoid and Secondary Coil.**

A long, thin, straight coil (solenoid) has a length $\ell=1 \mathrm{~m}$, diameter $D_{1}=2 \mathrm{~cm}$, number of turns $N_{1}=2000$, and negligible resistance. An alternating voltage of 100 V effective (rms) voltage at a frequency of 100 kHz is applied to the terminals of the coil. Beside the solenoid, in its immediate vicinity, in a plane perpendicular to its axis and passing through its midpoint, a flat coil is placed with $N_{2}=200$ turns and diameter $D_{2}=3 \mathrm{~cm}$.

What is the effective (rms) voltage shown by an ideal voltmeter connected across the flat coil?
