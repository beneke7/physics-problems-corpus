**P1. Rotating coin on a drawing board**

A small coin coated with graphite rests on a horizontal, sufficiently large, rectangular drawing board. The drawing board is moved in its own plane such that its center travels on a circle of radius $R$ with angular velocity $\omega$, while its sides remain parallel to their original positions at all times. The coefficient of friction between the coin and the drawing board is $\mu$, which is small enough that the coin slides continuously.

How does the coin move after a long time? What trace does it leave on the drawing board?
