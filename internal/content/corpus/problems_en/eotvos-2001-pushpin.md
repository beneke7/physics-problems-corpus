A tilted pushpin lies on a slightly inclined tabletop. If we nudge it slightly from the side, it rocks back and forth, but does not slip.

a) What is the ratio of the normal components (perpendicular to the table) of the forces exerted by the head and the point of the pushpin in stable equilibrium?

b) At what frequency does the pushpin oscillate (for small angles) about its equilibrium position?

For simplicity, assume that the head of the pushpin is a homogeneous disk, the mass of the needle is negligible compared to the head, and the tip of the needle does not move on the tabletop during oscillation.

Data: The radius of the disk is $R=6~\mathrm{mm}$, the length of the needle is $l=8~\mathrm{mm}$, and the inclination of the table is $\alpha=5^{\circ}$.
