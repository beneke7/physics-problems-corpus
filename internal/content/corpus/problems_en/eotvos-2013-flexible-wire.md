**3. A current-carrying flexible wire.**

In a homogeneous, strong magnetic field of flux density $B$, the endpoints of a
light, thin, flexible conducting wire of length $l$ are fixed at points $P_1$ and
$P_2$, whose separation is $l/2$. A steady current of strength $I$ flows through
the wire. What shape does the wire take if the magnetic-induction vector is

**a)** perpendicular to the segment $P_1P_2$?

**b)** parallel to the segment $P_1P_2$?

With what force does the wire pull on the fixed points in the two cases?
