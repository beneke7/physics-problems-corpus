**2. Problem. Distribution of Ions in an Electric Field**

A rectangular gas container is divided into two parts by a double-layer fine-woven metal screen; the volumes of the two spaces have a ratio of 1:2. The two layers of the metal screen are separated by a very narrow gap and do not touch each other. The container holds a gas of singly positively charged ions. The temperature in both spaces is maintained constant at 1200 K. What polarity and magnitude of direct voltage should be applied between the layers of the metal screen so that after a long time, the number of ions in both spaces becomes equal?

(The gas is dilute enough that interactions between particles can be neglected, and the mean free path is much larger than the distance between the layers of the screen. The charge of the ions is constant.)
