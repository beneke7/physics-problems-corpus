**2. A test tube, fixed vertically with its opening facing downwards, is about to be immersed in a large bowl of water.**

The temperature of the water and the environment is slowly increased from the initial 0 °C. After a while, the heating is stopped and the temperature is allowed to return to the original value. We find that the test tube is half full of water. The external air pressure was $10^{5}$ Pa throughout.

Approximately what temperature (in °C) did we heat the system to?

(Note: for this question, you are expected to have a table of the vapour pressure of water at different temperatures. You can use the one on Wikipedia.)
