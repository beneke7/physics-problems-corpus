**2. Problem. Metal Rod in AC Voltage**

A metal rod of mass $m=50 \text{ g}$ and length $\ell=20 \text{ cm}$ is suspended horizontally by two metal wires, each also of length $\ell$. The rod is placed between the poles of a wide horseshoe magnet, so that the rod is completely immersed in a nearly homogeneous magnetic field with induction $B=0.50 \text{ T}$. The combined electrical resistance of the wires and rod is $r=0.10 \text{ Ω}$. Between the upper ends of the wires, a sinusoidal AC voltage with amplitude $U_{0}=10 \text{ mV}$ and frequency $f=1.0 \text{ Hz}$ is applied.

How does the rod move after a long time? Neglect air resistance.
