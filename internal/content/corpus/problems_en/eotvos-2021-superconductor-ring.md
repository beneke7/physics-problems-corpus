**2. Problem. Superconductor Ring in a Solenoid.**

A cylindrical, length $\ell$, radius $R \ll \ell$, air-core solenoid has $N$ turns. Inside the coil, a superconductor ring with radius $r \ll R$, perpendicular to the solenoid's axis of symmetry, with inductance $L$, is placed (the ring and solenoid centers coincide).

a) Does the inductance of the solenoid increase or decrease due to the placement of the ring?

b) Determine the magnitude of the change in inductance!
