**P1. Nitrogen Cooling with Heat Pump**

A sample of mass $m_0$ with constant specific heat $c$ is at a temperature slightly below the boiling point $T_0$ of nitrogen. We have available liquid nitrogen of mass $m$ at its boiling point, and a heat pump. What is the minimum temperature to which the sample can be cooled before all the nitrogen has evaporated? The latent heat of vaporization of nitrogen is $L$.
