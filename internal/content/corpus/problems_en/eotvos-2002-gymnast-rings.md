**1. Gymnast on Rings**

We wish to estimate the force stretching the arms of a gymnast exercising on rings at the lowest point of the trajectory when swinging from a handstand to a giant circle. We apply the following simple model: a homogeneous rod of length $l$ and mass $m$ is attached to the end of a very long, inextensible rope, and we release it from the position shown in figure 1.a. Calculate the force stretching the rope at the moment shown in figure 1.b! (Neglect the mass of the rope.)
