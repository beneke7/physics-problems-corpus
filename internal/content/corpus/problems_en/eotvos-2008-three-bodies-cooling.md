**P2. Three Bodies Cooling**

Three objects made of the same material with constant specific heat have temperatures of $13°\mathrm{C}$, $27°\mathrm{C}$, and $90°\mathrm{C}$. The mass of each of the two warmer objects is half the mass of the $13°\mathrm{C}$ object. Using appropriate heat engines and energy storage devices, without external energy input, we want to cool the $13°\mathrm{C}$ object as much as possible.

a) How should we proceed? (The objects can only change temperature through heat absorption or release; there is no change of state, and thermal expansion is negligible.)

b) How cold can the originally $13°\mathrm{C}$ object become?

(Radnai Gyula)
