**1. Problem. The Phase of Venus**

On September 12, 2023, Venus rose 2 hours and 20 minutes before the Sun, almost simultaneously with the Moon. At that time, the Moon formed a thin crescent, but Venus was visible to the naked eye as a bright "star," appearing point-like.

a) How would we have seen Venus through a telescope on that morning, given that the next day it rose a few minutes earlier relative to the Sun? What was its phase (what fraction of the disk was visible)?

b) How do the shape and apparent size of Venus change over the following months? When will Venus next rise 2 hours and 20 minutes before the Sun? Sketch to scale the appearance of Venus in September and at that future date.

For simplicity, disregard the eccentricity and inclination of the orbits and the obliquity of Earth's axis. (The given data have been adjusted accordingly from reality, and the answer should be found in this approximation.) Venus's orbital radius is 0.723 times Earth's.
