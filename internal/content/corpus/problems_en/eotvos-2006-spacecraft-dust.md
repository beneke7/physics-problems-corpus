**Problem 2. Space Probe in a Dust Cloud**

A space probe traveling in an interplanetary orbit passes through a cosmic "dust cloud" at some point along its path. All dust particles that collide with the probe stick to it. By the time the probe exits the dust cloud, its mass has increased by 2%.

By what percentage has the time of passage through the dust cloud increased compared to the time it would have taken to traverse the same distance without the braking effect of the dust cloud?

(Consider the dust cloud to be a uniform-density object with well-defined boundaries.)
