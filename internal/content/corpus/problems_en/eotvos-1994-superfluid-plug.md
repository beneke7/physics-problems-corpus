**2. Problem. Superfluid Helium.**

A cylindrical container is filled with superfluid helium. The container is 1 dm tall with an internal base area of $1 \mathrm{dm}^{2}$.

Carefully, we place a cylindrical "plug" on top of the helium that is also 1 dm tall but with a base area of only $0.99 \mathrm{dm}^{2}$, and we release it. The density of the plug equals the density of the helium.

| How does the plug move?
| In how much time does it reach the bottom of the container?

The entire apparatus is at a temperature very close to 0 K, and the internal friction and surface tension of the liquid are negligible.

*Péter Gnädig*
