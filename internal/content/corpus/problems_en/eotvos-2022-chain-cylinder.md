**1. Problem. Chain Falling Off a Cylinder**

A thin, flexible chain of mass $m$ is placed on a fixed horizontal cylinder as shown in the figure and held at rest. Friction between the cylinder and the chain is negligible.

a) What acceleration does the chain start with when released?
b) What is the maximum value of the force that stretches the chain at the moment of release?
