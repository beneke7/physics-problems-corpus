**2. Problem. Image of a Flame in a Cylindrical Mirror.**

In the center of a circular table stands a cylindrical glass vase with very thin walls, inside which a candle burns. The cylinder has a diameter of 12 cm, its axis is vertical, and the center of the flame is 2 cm from the cylinder's axis.

Laci observes the vase from the side at the same height as the flame and notices that beside the flame, he also sees a sharp, distinct mirror image of the flame reflected in the inside of the vase. Walking around the table, he observes that the width of the flame's image and its position relative to the vase change continuously.

a) From which direction does Laci see the image of the flame having the same width as the flame itself?

b) What path does the center of the flame's image trace as Laci walks around the table?

For the imaging by the cylindrical mirror, we can apply the mirror equation that holds for spherical mirrors.

*(Gyula Radnai)*
