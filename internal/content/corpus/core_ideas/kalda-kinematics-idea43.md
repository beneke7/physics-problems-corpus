For some problems, the main difficulty is understanding, what is going on; once you understand, the calculations are typically quite easy. Keep your mind calm: in physics competitions, you are not asked to do something impossible: act as a detective by investigating step-by-step what is going on, and narrowing down possibilities by the exclusion method.
