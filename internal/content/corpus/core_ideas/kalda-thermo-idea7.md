If two reservoirs are connected via a narrow pipe, narrow valve, etc, then a quasi-equilibrium is possible: while the temperatures of the two reservoirs to the other sides of the pipe are different, the pressures are equal. Thus, there is a mechanical equilibrium, but no thermal equilibrium (it takes much longer time to reach the latter).
