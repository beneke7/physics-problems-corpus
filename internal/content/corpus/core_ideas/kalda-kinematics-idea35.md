Since the distance between any two points is fixed in a rigid body, the projections of velocities of both points on the line connecting them are equal. NB! the respective projections of accelerations are not necessarily equal due to the centripetal acceleration; when dealing with accelerations you need to use the fact 13 in one point's frame of reference, instead.
