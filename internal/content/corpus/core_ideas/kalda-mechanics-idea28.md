When solving three-dimensional problems, sometimes calculating coordinates in appropriately chosen axes and applying formulae of spatial rotations can be of use. For the rotation around $z$-axis by angle $\varphi, x^{\prime}=x \cos \varphi-y \sin \varphi$ and $y^{\prime}=y \cos \varphi+x \sin \varphi$.
