Using torque balance conditions is usually more efficient than using force balance conditions because for any force balance condition, we can eliminate only one force ${ }^{21}$ by projecting the condition to the perpendicular of that force; meanwhile, if we choose the pivot point as the intersection point of the two lines along which two unparallel forces are applied, both forces disappear from the equation.
