Idea 46 can be generalized in a way that would hold for open systems (certain amounts of matter enter and leave the system): $\vec{F}=\frac{\mathrm{d} \vec{P}}{d t}+\vec{\Phi}_{P \text { in }}-\vec{\Phi}_{P \text { out }}$, where $\vec{\Phi}_{P \text { in }}$ and $\vec{\Phi}_{P \text { out }}$ are the entering and the outgoing fluxes of momentum (in other words, the net momentum of the matter entering and leaving the system, respectively).
