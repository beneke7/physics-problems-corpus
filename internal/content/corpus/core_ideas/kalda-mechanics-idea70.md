If a body moves in a liquid, the fluid will also move. (A) If the flow is laminar (no eddies), only the liquid adjacent to the body will move; (B) if the flow is turbulent, there will be a turbulent 'tail' behind the body. In either case the characteristic velocity of the moving liquid is the same as the velocity of the body.
