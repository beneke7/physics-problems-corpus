If there is no leakage of magnetic field lines from a transformer, i.e. if $M=\sqrt{L_{1} L_{2}}$ then the total inductive electromotive force in both inductors is defined by the same linear combination of currents $J \equiv I_{1} \sqrt{L_{1}}+I_{2} \sqrt{L_{2}}$ (indeed, $\Phi_{1}=J \sqrt{L_{1}}$ and $\left.\Phi_{2}=J \sqrt{L_{2}}\right)$. This has two important consequences.
