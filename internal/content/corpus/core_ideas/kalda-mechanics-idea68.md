If the motion of a spread collection of particles could be divided into oscillation in a known direction and an oscillation-free motion (so motion perpendicular to the oscillation), then the particles are focussed at certain points: where the oscillation phase of all particles is either zero or is an integer multiple of $2 \pi$.
