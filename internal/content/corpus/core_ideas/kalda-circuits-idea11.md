Sometimes it is convenient to deal with constant current sources - instead of batteries (and sometimes, a current source is already present). A battery with electromotive force $\mathcal{E}$ and internal resistance $r$ is equivalent to a constant current source with $I=\mathcal{E} / r$ which is connected parallel to the shunt resistance $r^{11}$.
