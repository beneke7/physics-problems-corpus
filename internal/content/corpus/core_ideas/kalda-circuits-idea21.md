Edge-splitting method: a resistor between nodes $A$ and $B$ can be represented as a parallel connection of two resistors, and the node $A$ can be split into two nodes, if the potentials of the new nodes $A^{\prime}$ and $A^{\prime \prime}$ will be equal.
