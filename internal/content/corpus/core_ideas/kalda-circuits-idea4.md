Kirchoff's laws are not valid only for the currents and voltages, but also for voltage increments $\Delta V_{i}=V_{i}($ after $)- V_{i}$ (before) and current increments $\Delta I_{i}=I_{i}$ (after) $-I_{i}$ (before).
