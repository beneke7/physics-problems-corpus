Non-ideal diodes which are approximated with an idealized $V-I$ curve with a non-zero opening voltage $V_{c}$ (there is no current for $V<V_{c}$, and for any forward current, $\left.V=V_{c}\right)$ ca be also handled according to the idea 28 ; the only difference is that for forward currents, diode needs to be replaced by a battery with emf. $\mathcal{E}=V_{c}$. Additionally, the power dissipation on the diode is calculated in the same way as the work done by a battery: dissipation power is $V_{c} I$, and the dissipated heat - $V_{c} \Delta Q$, where $\Delta Q$ is the charge passing through the diode.
