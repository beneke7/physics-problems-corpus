The more general and sometimes indispensable form of Newton's $2^{\text {nd }}$ law is $\vec{F}=\frac{\mathrm{d} \vec{P}}{d t}$, where $\vec{P}$ is the net momentum of the system and $\vec{F}$ is the sum of external forces acting on the system. An analogous formula is $\vec{M}=\frac{\mathrm{d} \vec{L}}{d t}$, where $\vec{L}$ is the net angular momentum of the system (with respect to a given point) and $\vec{M}$ is the sum of external torques.
