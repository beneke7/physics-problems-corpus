Wave propagation problems can be often conveniently analysed using the wave vectors: using orthogonal coordinates $(x, y, z)$, and time $t$, a sinusoidal wave $a(x, y, z, t)$ can be represented as $\sin (\vec{k} \vec{r}-\omega t)$, where the scalar product of the wave vector $k$ and radius vector $\vec{r}$ equals to $\vec{k} \vec{r}=k_{x} x+k_{y} y+k_{z} z$. Here $\omega$ is the angular frequency of the wave and the wave's propagation speed $v=\omega / k^{13}$.
