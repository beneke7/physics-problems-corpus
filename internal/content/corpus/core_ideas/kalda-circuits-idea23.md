Sometimes the problem symmetrization can be achieved by introducing fictitious negative resistances: there is no problem with applying Kirchoff's laws to negative resistances. ${ }^{17}$ In particular, $R$ and $-R$ in parallel correspond to an infinite resistance, and in series - to a zero resistance.
