For liquid flow, Bernoulli's (i.e. energy conservation) law is often helpful: $p+\rho g h+\frac{1}{2} \rho v^{2}=$ Const, where $p$ is the static pressure, $h$ is the height of the considered point and $v$ is the velocity of the flow at that point.
