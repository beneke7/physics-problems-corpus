Here are moments of inertia for a few bodies, with respect to the centre of mass. A rod of length of $l$ : $\frac{1}{12} M l^{2}$, solid sphere: $\frac{2}{5} M R^{2}$, spherical shell: $\frac{2}{3} M R^{2}$, cylinder: $\frac{1}{2} M R^{2}$, square with side length $a$, axis perpendicular to its plane: $\frac{1}{6} M a^{2}$.
