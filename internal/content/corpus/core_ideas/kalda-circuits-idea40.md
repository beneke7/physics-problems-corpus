Suppose that a periodic signal is applied to a circuit containing two or more of the following elements: resistances $(R)$, capacitors $(C)$, inductances $L$, nonlinear elements such as diodes. If the system has evolved long enough (much longer than $R C$ and $L / R$, so that the system response has also become periodic), the average voltage on the leads of an inductor is zero, and the average current through each capacitor is zero.
