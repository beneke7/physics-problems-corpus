If the considered time interval is much less than $R C$ or $L / R$, the time dependence of the capacitors' charges and inductors' currents can be linearized: $q=q_{0}+I_{c} t$, where $I_{c}$ is an almost constant current feeding the capacitor, and $I_{L}=I_{0}+V_{L} t / L$, where $V_{L}$ is an almost constant voltage applied to the leads of the inductor.
