Sometimes it is useful to change into a non-inertial frame of reference: velocities are added just in the usual way, $\vec{v}_{\text {lab }}=\vec{v}_{\text {rel }}+\vec{v}_{\text {fr }}$, where $\vec{v}_{\text {lab }}$ is the velocity in the lab frame, $\vec{v}_{\text {rel }}$ velocity in the moving frame, and $\vec{v}_{\text {fr }}$ - the speed of that point of the moving frame where the body is at the given moment. If the frame moves translationally (without rotations) then the accelerations can be added in the same way, $\vec{a}_{\text {lab }}=\vec{a}_{\text {rel }}+\vec{a}_{\text {fr }}{ }^{1}$.
