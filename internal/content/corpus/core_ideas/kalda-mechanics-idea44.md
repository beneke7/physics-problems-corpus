Velocity is maximal (or minimal) when acceleration (and net force) is zero (since $0=\frac{d v}{d t}=a$ ); shift is extremal when velocity is zero. Possible other pairs: electrical charge (capacitor's voltage)-current, current-inductive emf, etc.
