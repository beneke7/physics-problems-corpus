Two vectors $\vec{a}=\left(a_{x}, a_{y}, a_{z}\right)$ and $\vec{b}=\left(b_{x}, b_{y}, b_{z}\right)$ are perpendicular if their scalar product is zero, $a_{x} b_{x}+a_{y} b_{y}+ a_{z} b_{z}=0$. (This assumes that the axes $x, y$ and $z$ are perpendicular to each other.)
