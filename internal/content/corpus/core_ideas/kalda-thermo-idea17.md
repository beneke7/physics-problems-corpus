If the dependence between two physical quantities $y$ and $x$ is given as a graph where $y$ is plotted versus $x[y=f(x)]$, and you are asked to find, which value of $x$ (or $f$ ) is taken, and from theoretical considerations one can derive another functional dependence between $f$ and $x$, for instance $y=a x+b$, then the solution can be found as the intersection point of two graphs, $y=f(x)$ and $y=a x+b$.
