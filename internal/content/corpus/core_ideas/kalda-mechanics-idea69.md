If we draw a straight line of length $\tilde{l}$ such that it passes through the centre of mass and one of its ends is by the axis of rotation, then if we move the rotation axis to the other end of the segment (and let the body reach a stable equilibrium), then the new frequency of oscillation is the same as before. Conclusion: the set of points where the axis of rotation could be placed without changing the frequency of oscillation, consists of two concentric circles around the centre of mass.
