In the case of a homogeneous vector field $\vec{F}(x, y, z) \equiv \vec{F}_{0}$, its flux $\Phi=\int_{S} \vec{F} \cdot \mathrm{~d} \vec{S}$ trhough a (possibly curved) surface $S$ can be found as the dot product of a certain effective flat surface element $\vec{S}_{\text {eff }}=\int_{S} \mathrm{~d} \vec{S}, \Phi=\vec{S}_{\text {eff }} \cdot \vec{F}_{0}$.
