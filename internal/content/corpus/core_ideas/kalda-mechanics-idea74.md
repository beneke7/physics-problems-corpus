If a similar oscillatory motion takes place, for which the parameters of the system change slowly (compared to the period of oscillation), then the so-called adiabatic invariant $I$ is conserved: it is the area enclosed by the closed contour traced by the trajectory of the system on the so-called phase diagram (where the coordinates are the spatial coordinate $x$ and momentum $p_{x}$ ).
