When a body is rotating around the axis $s$, the net torque it experiences is $M=I \varepsilon$ (not to be confused with the body's mass), where $I$ is its moment of inertia with respect to the axis $s, I=\sum m_{i} r_{i}^{2}=\int r^{2} \cdot d m=\int r^{2} \rho \cdot d V$ and $r_{i}$ is the distance of $i$-th particle from the axis $s$ (the sum is evaluated over all particles of the body). Kinetic energy is $K=\frac{1}{2} I \omega^{2}$.
