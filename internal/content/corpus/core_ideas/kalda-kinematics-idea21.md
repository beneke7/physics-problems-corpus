If a force is perpendicular to the direction of motion (normal force when sliding along a curved surface, tension in a rope when a moving body is attached to an unstretchable rope fixed at the other end, force on a charge in magnetic field) then the velocity vector can only turn, its modulus will not change. ${ }^{4}$
