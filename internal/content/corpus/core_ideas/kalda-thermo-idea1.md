The body temperature $T$ and the net heating power $P$ are related via equality $P \equiv \frac{d Q}{d t}=C \frac{d T}{d t}$. Therefore, the tangent to the $T(t)$ curve is proportional to the net power and inversely proportional to the heat capacitance; many problems can be solved by using this observation.
