For problems involving addition of vectors (velocities, forces), the problems can be often reduced to the application of simple geometrical facts, such as (a) the shortest path from a point to a line (or plane) is perpendicular to the line (plane); (b) among such triangles $A B C$ which have two fixed side lengths $|B C|=a$ and $|A C|=b<a$, the triangle of largest $\angle A B C$ has $\angle B A C=90^{\circ}$.
