Consider (infinitesimally) small volumes (line segments, etc.) and write down applicable force balances and/or conservation laws for these volumes to derive either a relationship between physical quantities, or differential equations describing a physical quantity as a function of other quantities. Make use of the smallness of these quantities: drop anything which has higher order of smallness (e.g. squared small quantities).
