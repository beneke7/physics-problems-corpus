If an AC-circuit problem turns out to be essentially a geometrical problem, it is better to use vector diagrams instead of complex amplitudes, i.e. to draw the vectors corresponding to the complex amplitudes, and to study the problem geometrically; keep in mind that using scalar product and rms. amplitudes, $P=\vec{U} \cdot \vec{I}$. Geometrical knowledge which can be useful: Thales theorem, inscribed angle theorem, laws of sines and cosines
