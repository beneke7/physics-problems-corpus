For some problems, optimal choice of parametrization can simplify mathematical calculations significantly. An incomplete list of options: Cartesian, polar, cylindrical, and spherical coordinates; travel distance; Lagrangian coordinates (i.e. for fluids flow using the initial coordinate of a fluid particle instead of its current coordinate); relative position of a particle according to a certain ranking scheme, etc.
