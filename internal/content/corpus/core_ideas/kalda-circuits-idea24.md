If there are nonlinear resistors included into a circuit which are characterized with a nonlinear current-voltage dependence $I(V)$ then the current through the nonlinear element can be found graphically: $I-V$-dependence can be also expressed using the Kirchoff's laws, in simpler cases this will be a linear law $V=U_{0}-I r$. Then, the solution will be the intersection point of the two curves, $U_{0}-I r$ and $I(V)$.
