If the task is to find the resistance of a circuit between two leads, it is often useful to assume that either a voltage $V$ is applied to the leads, or a current $I$ is driven through these leads. Then we need to find the missing quantity ( $I$ or $V$, respectively), and calculate $R=V / I$.
