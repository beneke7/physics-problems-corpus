If the task is to find a temporal dependence of a voltage or current, and the circuit contains one or more batteries or constant current sources, the solution can be found as a superposition of a stationary solution (when all the voltages and currents are constant), and a solution obtained for a simplified circuit, where all the ideal batteries are substituted with wires, and the current sources are "cut off".
