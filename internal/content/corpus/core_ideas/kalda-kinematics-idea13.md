For an elastic bouncing of a ball from a wall which moves with a velocity $\vec{u}$ in the direction of the surface normal, the normal component $\vec{v}_{n}$ of the ball's velocity $\vec{v}$ is increased by $2 \vec{u}$, i.e. $\vec{v}_{n}^{\prime}=-\vec{v}_{n}+2 \vec{u}$.
