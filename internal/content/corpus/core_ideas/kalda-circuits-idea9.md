Any circuit which consists only of resistors and has three ports is equivalent to a $\Delta$ - or a $Y$-connection of three appropriately chosen resistors ${ }^{7}$ In particular, a $Y$-connection can be substituted by a $\Delta$-connection and vice versa ${ }^{8}$.
