Extremal currents and voltages can be often found from the energy conservation law by noting that (a) at the moment of an inductor's current extremum, $\frac{d I}{d t}=0$, hence the voltage on the inductor $V=L \frac{d I}{d t}=0$; (b) at the moment of a capacitor's voltage extremum, $\frac{d V}{d t}=C^{-1} \frac{\mathrm{~d} q}{\mathrm{~d} t}=0$, hence the current through capacitor's leads $I=\frac{\mathrm{d} q}{\mathrm{~d} t}=0$.
