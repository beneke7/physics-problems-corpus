If more fixing elements (rods, strings, etc) than the necessary minimum have been used to keep a body in static equilibrium (i.e. more than the number of degrees of freedom) and fixing elements are absolutely rigid, then tensions in the elements cannot be determined. In order to make it possible, the elements have to be considered elastic (able to deform); recall the fact 13 .
