The relation $I \varepsilon=M$ is clearly valid only if the centre of rotation is motionless; however, it turns out that it also holds when the instantaneous axis of rotation is moving translationally such that the distance of the body's centre of mass from the axis does not change (eg when rolling a cylindrical or spherical object).
