Question 4
A glass-walled elevator is moving upwards with constant acceleration. At some point in the elevator's motion, a bolt breaks loose and drops from the ceiling. What is the motion of the bolt as seen by an external observer (i.e. one located outside the elevator)?

a. The bolt moves upwards at a constant speed.
b. The bold initially moves upwards, then slows, reverses direction and moves downwards.
c. The bolt appears to remain stationary.
d. The bolt immediately moves downwards, accelerating as it goes.
e. The bolt immediately moves downwards, at constant speed.

Solution: $b$. Immediately after breaking loose the bolt will be moving at the same speed as the elevator, which is upwards. Since the bolt is no longer attached to the elevator, the only force it experiences is the gravitational force downwards. This means that the bolt moves upwards initially, slows and then reverses direction.
