Question 9
Assuming $g = 10 \mathrm {~m} \mathrm {~s} ^ { - 2 }$, what is the magnitude of the normal force exerted by the surface on the block in Question 8?

a. 20 N
b. 30 N
c. 50 N
d. 70 N
e. 5 kg - 20 N

Solution: $b$. The normal force will prevent the block accelerating downwards. The other forces acting on the block are 50 N down due to gravity plus 20 N up due to the applied force, so, in total, 30 N down. This means that the normal force is 30 N upwards.
