Question 2
Which of the following is the best estimate of the speed of a parrot flying?

a. $3 \times 10 ^ { - 3 } \mathrm {~m} \mathrm {~s} ^ { - 1 }$
b. $1 \times 10 ^ { - 1 } \mathrm {~ms} ^ { - 1 }$
c. $3 \times 10 ^ { 0 } \mathrm {~m} \mathrm {~s} ^ { - 1 }$
d. $1 \times 10 ^ { 2 } \mathrm {~m} \mathrm {~s} ^ { - 1 }$
e. $3 \times 10 ^ { 3 } \mathrm {~m} \mathrm {~s} ^ { - 1 }$

Solution: c. parrots fly much faster than people walk, which rules out a. and b. which are both less than $1 \mathrm {~ms} ^ { - 1 }$. However, they do not fly as fast as 100 m, which is a typical length of an oval, in 1 s, which rules out d. and e.
