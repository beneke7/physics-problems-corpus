Question 8
A block of mass 5 kg lies at rest on a horizontal surface. An upwards force of 20 N is applied to the block, as shown. Assuming $g = 10 \mathrm {~m} \mathrm {~s} ^ { - 2 }$, what is the weight of the block?
![](../../../figures/solution-ocr/c35030eab2572bdfc30aaf91.jpg)

a. 3 kg
b. 5 kg
c. 30 N
d. 50 N
e. $5 \mathrm {~kg} - 20 \mathrm {~N}$

Solution: d. The weight is the gravitational force on the block. This is $W = m g = 5 \mathrm {~kg} \times 10 \mathrm {~m} \mathrm {~s} ^ { - 2 } = 50 \mathrm {~N}$.
