The containers of  A and B are connected by a short and thin tube. The volume of container  A is three times as much as that of container  B . Initially in both containers there is air at a temperature of 20 $^\circ$C and at a pressure of 10$^{5}$ Pa. Then container  A is heated to a temperature of 100 $^\circ$C, and is kept at this temperature while the temperature of  B is still 20 $^\circ$C. By what percent does the mass of the gas in each container change, and what will the pressure be in them? 
 (4 pont)
