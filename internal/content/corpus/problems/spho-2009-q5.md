5. The workfunction of potassium is 2.25 eV. A beam of light of wavelength 400 nm has an intensity of $3 \times 10^{-9} \mathrm{Wm}^{-2}$. Find
    (i) the maximum kinetic energy of the photoelectrons, and
    (ii) the number of electrons emitted per meter squared per second from the surface, assuming that only 3\% of the incident photons are effective in ejecting electrons.
