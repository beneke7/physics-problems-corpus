A steel ball has a diameter of $4.160\,\mathrm{cm}$ at $0\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$, and a circular hole in an aluminium sheet, made by laser cutting technology, has a diameter of $4.150\,\mathrm{cm}$ at $0\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. The coefficients of linear expansion are: $\alpha_{\textrm{Al}}=2.4\cdot 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{\tfrac{1}{K}}$ and $\alpha_{\textrm{steel}}=1.2\cdot 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{\tfrac{1}{K}}$. (Ignore the temperature dependence of the coefficients of thermal expansion.)
 a)  What is the temperature of the steel ball when it is just passing through the hole in the $0\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ aluminium plate?
 b)  What is the temperature of the aluminium sheet when a steel ball of temperature $0\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ can just fit through the hole?
 c ) What is the common temperature at which the hole and the ball have the same diameter?
 (4 pont)
