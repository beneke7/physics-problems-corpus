The circuit below makes it possible to charge a rechargeable battery of voltage $\mathcal{E}=12 \mathrm{~V}$ with a direct voltage source of a voltage lower than $\mathcal{E}, V_{0}=5 \mathrm{~V}$. To that end, the key $K$ is periodically switched on and off - the open and closed periods have equal length of $\tau=10 \mathrm{~ms}$. Find the average charging current assuming that $L=1 \mathrm{H}$. The diode can be considered to be ideal; neglect the ohmic resistance of the inductor.
