A wedge has been made out of a very light and slippery material. Its upper surface consists of two slopes making an angle $\alpha$ with the horizontal and inclined towards one another. The block is situated on a horizontal plane; a ball with mass $m$ lies at the bottom of the hole on its upper surface. Another ball with mass $M$ is placed higher than the first ball and the system is released. On what condition will the small ball with mass $m$ start slipping upwards along the slope? Friction can be neglected.
