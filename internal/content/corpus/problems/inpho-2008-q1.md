We define three quantities as follow:
$$
A=m_{e} c^{2}, B=h / m_{e} c, C=e^{2} / 2 \epsilon_{0} c h
$$
Where $m_{e}$ is electron mass and other symbols have their usual meanings. For the hydrogen atom, express the radius of the $n^{\text {th }}$ Bohr orbit $r_{n}$, the energy level $E_{n}$, and the Rydberg constant $R$ in terms of any two of $\{A, B, C\}$
