In our car both the head and the rear lights are connected to a 12 V rechargeable battery through a single 30 A fuse. The two headlights are each rated 55 W, and the two deadlights are each rated 30 W, and one of them goes wrong, while the fuse melts. We have spare bulbs, but the spare fuse that we have is rated 20 A. Can we use it?
