5. The amount of chocolate of chocolate Kate consumes in a day is given by
$$
C=0.05 \mathrm{~kg}+\sum_{i} T_{i} A_{i}^{2}
$$
Where $i$ is the number of annoying people Kate has to deal with in the day, $\sum_{i}$ means that you add a copy of $T_{i} A_{i}{ }^{2}$ for each of the people. $T_{i}$ is the amount of time measured in hours that Kate spends with the $i^{\text {th }}$ annoying person and $A_{i}$ is the "annoyance factor" of the $i^{\text {th }}$ annoying person. What are the units of the annoyance factor, A?
    A. kg
    B. kg/s
    C. kg/h
    D. $\mathrm{kg}^{2} / \mathrm{h}^{2}$
    E. $\quad \mathrm{kg}^{1 / 2} / \mathrm{h}^{1 / 2}$
