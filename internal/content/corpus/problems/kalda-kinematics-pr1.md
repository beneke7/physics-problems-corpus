On a river coast, there is a port; when a barge passed the port, a motor boat departed from the port to a village at the distance $s_{1}=15 \mathrm{~km}$ downstream. It reached its destination after $t=45 \mathrm{~min}$, turned around, and started immediately moving back towards the starting point. At the distance $s_{2}=9 \mathrm{~km}$ from the village, it met the barge. What is the speed of the river water, and what is the speed of the boat with respect to the water? Note that the barge did not move with respect to the water.
