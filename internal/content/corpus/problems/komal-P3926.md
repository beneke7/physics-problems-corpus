The two ends of a rubber string of negligible mass are fixed at the same height, such that the string is un-stretched. A small object is attached to the string in its midpoint, thus the angle of depression of the string is =30$^\circ$. Then the small object is charged and vertical electric field is applied. Initially the depression angle of the string increases to $_{1}$=45$^\circ$, then the electric field is changed and the depression angle increases to $_{2}$=60$^\circ$. By what factor is the electric field in the second case more than that of in the first case? 

 (4 pont)
