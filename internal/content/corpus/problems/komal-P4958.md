In a piece of uranium ore there are 200 million ${}^{233}$U atoms. The half-life of the ${}^{233}$U isotope is $1.6\cdot10^5$ years, and it decays to ${}^{229}$Th, the half-life of which is $7.{}8\cdot10^3$ years. This decays to ${}^{225}$Ra, which has a half-life of 15 days. Estimate the number of ${}^{225}$Ra nuclei in the uranium ore. 
 (5 pont)
