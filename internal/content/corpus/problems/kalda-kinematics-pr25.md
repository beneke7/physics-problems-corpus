A hinged structure consists of two links of length $2 l$. One of its ends is attached to a wall, the other is moving at distance $3 l$ from the wall at constant vertical velocity $v_{0}$. Find the acceleration of the hinge connecting the links when a) the link closer to the wall is horizontal b) the velocity of the connection point is zero.
