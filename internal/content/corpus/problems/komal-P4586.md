A square shaped, uniform density carpet is rotated by 360$^\circ$ about one of its vertices, such that it slides on the floor with its whole area. The coefficient of kinetic friction is the same at any point of the floor. With performing the same work how many times can this carpet be rotated, sliding on the floor, about its centre?
 (5 pont)
