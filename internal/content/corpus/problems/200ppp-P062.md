Two soap bubbles of radii $R_{1}$ and $R_{2}$ are joined by a straw. Air goes from one bubble to the other (which one?) and a single bubble of radius $R_{3}$ is formed. What is the surface tension of the soap solution if the atmospheric pressure is $p_{0}$ ? Is measuring three such radii a suitable method for determining the surface tension of liquids?
