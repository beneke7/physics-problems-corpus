A long slipway, inclined at an angle $\alpha$ to the horizontal, is fitted with many identical rollers, consecutive ones being a distance $d$ apart. The rollers have horizontal axles and consist of rubber-covered solid steel cylinders each of mass $m$ and radius $r$. Planks of mass $M$, and length much greater than $d$, are released at the top of the slipway.
![](../../sources/200ppp/figures/200ppp-039.jpg)

Find the terminal speed $v_{\text {max }}$ of the planks. Ignore air resistance and friction at the pivots of the rollers.
