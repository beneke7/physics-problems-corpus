There are two discs of radii $2r$ and $r$ at rest on a horizontal, not perfectly flat tabletop such that they almost touch each other. In the plane, a third disk of radius $3r$ slides without rotation so that its velocity vector is parallel to the common tangent of the three disks. The top view of the three disks are shown in the figure . All three disks are made of the same material and have the same height. After the collision, which is considered to be elastic, the disc with radius $3r$ slows down due to friction and stops after travelling $d=5~\mathrm{cm}$. In what direction and how far will the smaller discs travel on the table? Friction between the discs is negligible. Hint: See problem
