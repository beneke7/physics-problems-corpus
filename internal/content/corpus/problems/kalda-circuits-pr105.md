A rectangular voltage waveform of period $\tau$ and amplitude $V_{0}$ is applied to the input ports (this means that during half of the period, the input voltage is $V_{0}$, and during the other half-period, the voltage is $-V_{0}$ ). Find the shape and amplitude of the current flowing through the input ports.
