A billiard ball hits another stationary billiard ball. At which collection of points could the stationary ball be positioned such that it would be possible to achieve the situation where both balls will fall into two (different) pockets on the table? The collisions are perfectly elastic, the balls are perfectly slippery (hence the rotation of the balls is negligible).
