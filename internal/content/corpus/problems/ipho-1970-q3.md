## Problem 3.

Inside a thin-walled metal sphere with radius $R=20 \mathrm{~cm}$ there is a metal ball with the radius $r=10 \mathrm{~cm}$ which has a common centre with the sphere. The ball is connected with a very long wire to the Earth via an opening in the sphere (Fig. 3). A charge $Q=10^{-8} \mathrm{C}$ is placed onto the outside sphere. Calculate the potential of this sphere, electrical capacity of the obtained system of conducting bodies and draw out an equivalent electric scheme.
