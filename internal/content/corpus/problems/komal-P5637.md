The heat capacity of the interior of a freezer is $4\cdot 10^5\mathrm{\tfrac{J}{K}}$, and we want to keep the temperature inside the freezer at $(-18\pm 1)~{}^\circ\mathrm{C}$. The freezer is in a room of temperature $20~{}^\circ\mathrm{C}$. The refrigerator motor will start when the temperature inside reaches $-17~{}^\circ\mathrm{C}$. After the motor has been running for 15 minutes, the temperature in the interior of the refrigerator reaches $-19~{}^\circ\mathrm{C}$ again. At least what is the power of the freezer's motor? (Assume that the refrigerator is operating as an ideal Carnot heat pump, which is a reversed Carnot cycle.) 
 (4 pont)
