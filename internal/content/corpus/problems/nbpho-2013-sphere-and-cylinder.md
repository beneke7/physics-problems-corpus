**6. SPHERE AND CYLINDER (7 points)**

A sphere and a cylinder are lying on an inclined surface with inclination angle $\alpha$. Both have mass $m$ and radius $r$. The bodies are released from equal initial heights $H$. The moments of inertia of the sphere and the cylinder are, respectively, $I_{\text{sph}}=\frac{2}{5} m r^{2}$ and $I_{\text{cyl}}=\frac{1}{2} m r^{2}$. The coefficient of friction between the surface and the bodies is $\mu$.

i) (2 points) Which of the bodies comes down faster? What was the relative lag of the slower body $\gamma=\left(t_{2}-t_{1}\right) / t_{1}$? The times $t_{1}$ and $t_{2}$, respectively, denote the traveling times of the faster or the slower body. Assume that the rolling occurs without slipping.

ii) (2.5 points) Find the minimal angle of inclination $\alpha_{0}$ for which the cylinder starts to slide in addition to rolling.

iii) (2.5 points) If $\alpha \rightarrow 90^{\circ}$, the bodies obviously lose contact with the surface and fall down in free fall with equal times. What is the minimal angle of inclination $\alpha_{m}$, for which both the sphere and the cylinder come down with equal times?
