Fish soup is prepared in a hemispherical copper bowl of diameter 40 cm . The bowl is placed into the water of a lake to cool down and floats with 10 cm of its depth immersed.
![](../../../../../sources/200ppp/figures/200ppp-007.jpg)

A point on the rim of the bowl is pulled upwards through 10 cm , by a chain fastened to it. Does water flow into the bowl?
