A pencil is placed vertically on a table with its point downwards. It is then released and tumbles over. How does the direction in which the point moves, relative to that in which the pencil falls, depend upon the coefficient of friction? Will the pencil point lose contact with the table (other than when the 'shoulder' of the pencil ultimately comes into contact with the table)?
