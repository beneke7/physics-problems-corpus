Balloons with constant ascending velocity can be used to investigate wind velocities at various heights. The given graph of elevation angle against time was obtained by observing a such balloon. The balloon was released at distance $L=1 \mathrm{~km}$ from the point of observation and it seemed to be rising directly upwards. Knowing that wind velocity near the ground was zero, find the balloon's height at time $t=7$ min after its start and wind velocity at this height.
