A disc of mass $m$ can move on a horizontal frictionless surface of a table. The disc is connected to a body of mass $M$ by an inextensible rope that passes through a hole in the table (the friction between the rope and the tabletop is also negligible). The disc initially moves on a circular path of radius $R$ when it is slightly pushed radially. What is the period of the oscillation of the body of mass $M$?
