A hoop of mass $M$ and radius $r$ stands on a slippery horizontal surface. There is a thin slippery tunnel inside the hoop, along which a tiny block of mass $m$ can slide. Initially all the bodies are at rest and the block is at the hoop's uppermost point. Find the velocity and the acceleration of the hoop's central point at the moment when the angle between the imaginary line connecting the hoop's central point and the block's position and the vertical is $\varphi$.
