Small liquid drops of various sizes are in a closed container, to whose walls the liquid does not adhere. Over a sufficiently long time, the size of the smallest drops is found to decrease whilst that of the larger ones increases, until finally only one large drop remains in the container. What is the explanation for this phenomenon?
