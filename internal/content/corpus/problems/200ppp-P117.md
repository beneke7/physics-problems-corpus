Electrical charges are evenly distributed along a long, thin insulating $\operatorname{rod} A B$.
![](../../../../../sources/200ppp/figures/200ppp-055.jpg)

Show that at an arbitrary point $C$ (see figure), the electric field due to the rod points in the direction of the bisector of angle $A C B$.
