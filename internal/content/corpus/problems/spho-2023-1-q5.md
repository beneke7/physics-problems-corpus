5. (a) A beam of monochromatic UV light with wavelength 50 nm is incident onto a blackened flat plate of total area $200 \mathrm{~cm}^{2}$. The direction of the beam is normal to the surface of the plate. The beam has an intensity of $24 \mathrm{~W} \mathrm{~m}^{-2}$. Calculate the force exerted by the light beam on the surface of the plate. State any assumption you make in your calculation.

5. (b) Radioactive nucleus A with half-life of 1 hour decays into another radioactive nucleus B which has a half-life of 6 hours. Nucleus B decays into nucleus C which is stable. At time $t=0$, a specimen contains $10^{-3}$ mole of A but none of B and C. Nucleus B in the specimen reaches a maximum quantity of $X$ nuclei at time $\mathrm{t}=T$ hours. Find $X$ and $T$.
[For the first order differential equation, $\frac{d y}{d x}+c_{1} y=c_{2} e^{-k x}$, where $c_{1}, c_{2} \& k$ are constants, the solution is
$$
\left.y=\frac{c_{2}}{c_{1}-k}\left(e^{-k x}-e^{-c_{1} x}\right)\right]
$$
