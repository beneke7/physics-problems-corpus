The length of a weightless rod is $2 l$. A small sphere of mass $m$ is fixed at a distance $x=l$ from its upper end. The rod rests with its one end against the wall and the other against the floor. The end that rests on the floor is being moved with a constant velocity $v$ away from the wall. a) Find the force with which the sphere affects the rod at the moment, when the angle between the wall and the rod is $\alpha=45^{\circ}$; (b) what is the answer if $x \neq l$ ?
