10. In these three circuits all the batteries are identical and have negligible internal resistance, and all the light bulbs are identical. Rank all 5 light bulbs $(A, B, C, D, E)$
in order of brightness from brightest to dimmest.
![](inpho-2010-a10-fig01.png)
(a) $A=B=C>D=E$
(b) $A>B=C>D=E$
(c) $A=D=E>B=C$
(d) $A=D=E>B>C$
(e) $D=E>A>B=C$
