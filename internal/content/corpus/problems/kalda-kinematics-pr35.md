A boy is swimming in a fast-flowing river of width $L$; the water speed is $u=2 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$, and the boy can swim with speed $v=1 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$. While being at point $A$ near one coast, he wants to reach such a point $B$ near the other coast which is directly across the river $(\overrightarrow{A B} \perp \vec{u})$. Since the river is too fast, he cannot avoid being carried downstream to a certain distance $a$ from point $B$; what is the smallest possible value of $a$ which he can achieve for the optimally chosen swimming direction?
