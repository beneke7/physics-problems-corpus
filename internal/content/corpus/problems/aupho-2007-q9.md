The speed of light in vacuum, $c$, depends on two fundamental constants, the permeability of free space, $\mu_{0}$, and the permittivity of free space, $\varepsilon_{0}$. The speed of light is given by $c=\frac{1}{\sqrt{\mu_{0} \varepsilon_{0}}}$. The units of $\varepsilon_{\mathrm{o}}$ are $\mathrm{N}^{-1} . \mathrm{C}^{2} . \mathrm{m}^{-2}$.
The units for $\mu_{\mathrm{o}}$ are:

(A) $\mathrm{kg}^{-1} \mathrm{~m}^{-1} \mathrm{C}^{2}$
(B) $\mathrm{kg} . \mathrm{m} . \mathrm{C}^{-2}$
(C) $\mathrm{kg} . \mathrm{m} . \mathrm{s}^{-4} . \mathrm{C}^{-2}$
(D) $\mathrm{kg}^{-1} \mathrm{~s}^{-3} \mathrm{C}^{-2}$
(E) $\mathrm{kg}^{2} . \mathrm{m}^{2} . \mathrm{C}^{-4}$
