## Question 8

A big dog weighing 60 kg runs into a cat weighing 8 kg . The dog was moving at $1.5 \mathrm{~ms}^{-1}$, and the cat was sitting in the sun. Assume that the dog and cat continue moving together. What is the final speed of the combined cat/dog furball?
a. $0.18 \mathrm{~ms}^{-1}$
b. $0.75 \mathrm{~m} \mathrm{~s}^{-1}$
c. $1.3 \mathrm{~m} \mathrm{~s}^{-1}$
d. $1.7 \mathrm{~ms}^{-1}$
e. $11.3 \mathrm{~ms}^{-1}$
