A dog is chasing a fox running at constant velocity $v_{1}$ along a straight line. The modulus of the dog's velocity is constant and equal to $v_{2}$, but the vector $\vec{v}$ is always directed towards the fox. The distance between the animals was $l$ at the moment when their velocity vectors were perpendicular. What was the acceleration of the dog at that moment?
