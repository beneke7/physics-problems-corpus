Two identical sound sources at a distance of $d$ from each other emit sound waves of wavelength $\lambda$ in the same phase. The waves are detected by a receiver at a point on the circumference of a circle of radius $R$. The two sound sources are symmetrically positioned with respect to the centre of the circle. Where are the points at which the waves from the two sound sources reinforce each other if $d=2\lambda$ and $R\gg d$?
