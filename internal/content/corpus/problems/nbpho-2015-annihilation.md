**1. ANNIHILATION (6 points)** — *L. Franti*

An electron with kinetic energy 1 MeV travels along the $z$-axis and collides with a positron at rest. The particles annihilate producing a pair of two photons A and B with equal energies.

i) *(1.5 points)* Find the speed of the electron $v_{e}$ in the lab frame.

ii) *(0.5 points)* Find the energy $E_{\gamma}$ of photon A in the lab frame.

iii) *(1 point)* Find the momentum $p_{\gamma}$ of photon A in the lab frame.

iv) *(2 points)* Find the angle $\alpha$ between the $z$-axis and the momentum of photon A in the lab frame.

v) *(1 point)* Why is it not possible for a collision process to produce just one single photon?

The rest mass of electron $m_{e}=511 \mathrm{keV} / c^{2}$. You can express your results in electron volts and related units.
