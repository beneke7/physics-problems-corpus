Room is being heated by an electrical radiator of power $P=1 \mathrm{~kW}$. Outside temperature is $t_{0}=0^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$, the room temperature has been stabilized at $t_{1}=20^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$. A ventilation window is opened, supplying now a fresh air at the volume rate $v=20 \mathrm{l} / \mathrm{s}$. What will be the new room temperature? Air can be considered to be an ideal bimolecular gas.
