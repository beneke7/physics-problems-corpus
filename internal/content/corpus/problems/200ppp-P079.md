A heavy body of mass $m$ hangs on a flexible thread in a railway carriage which moves at speed $v_{0}$ on a train-safety test track, as shown in the figure.
![](../../../../../sources/200ppp/figures/200ppp-033.jpg)

The carriage is brought to rest by a strong but uniform braking. Can the pendulum travel through $180^{\circ}$, so that the taut thread reaches the vertical?
