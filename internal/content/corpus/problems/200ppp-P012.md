A small, elastic ball is dropped vertically onto a long plane inclined at an angle $\alpha$ to the horizontal. Is it true that the distances between consecutive bouncing points grow as in an arithmetic progression? Assume that collisions are perfectly elastic and that air resistance can be neglected.
