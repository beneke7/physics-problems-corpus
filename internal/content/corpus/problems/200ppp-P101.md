A light, inelastic thread is stretched round one-half of the circumference of a fixed cylinder as shown in the figure.
![](../../sources/200ppp/figures/200ppp-043.jpg)

As a result of friction, the thread does not slip on the cylinder when the magnitudes of the forces acting on its ends fulfil the inequality

$$
\frac{1}{2} F_{A} \leq F_{B} \leq 2 F_{A} .
$$

Determine the coefficient of friction between the thread and the cylinder.
