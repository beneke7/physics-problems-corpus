The titanium-devouring little green people of the previous problem continued their excavating. As a result of their environmentally destructive activity, half of the asteroid was soon used up and, as shown in the figure, only a regular hemisphere remained. The excavated material was carried away from the asteroid.
![](../../../../../sources/200ppp/figures/200ppp-053.jpg)

What is the gravitational acceleration at the centre of the circular face of the remaining hemisphere if the gravitational acceleration at the surface of the original (spherical) asteroid was $g_{0}=9.81 \mathrm{~cm} \mathrm{~s}^{-2}$ ?
