The fuel consumption meter of a car, moving uniformly along a level road, reads 5 litres/100 km. If the same car is moving along the same road, and accelerates uniformly, then the reading on the fuel consumption meter at the moment when the car reaches the speed of the uniform motion is 10 litres/100 km. When the car goes at the same speed on a hill the fuel consumption is 12 litres/100 km. What does the meter read when the car is driving up the same hill at the acceleration as described before at the moment when its speed also reaches the value described above? 
 (3 pont)
