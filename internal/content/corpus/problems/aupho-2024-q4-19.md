4. The speed of a wave v in a string depends on the tension force, T, and the linear mass density $\mu$ (mass per unit length) of the string. It is given by $\mathrm{v}=\mathrm{T}^{\mathrm{a}} \mu^{\mathrm{b}}$ where a and b are constants. Using dimensional analysis, determine the exponents a and b. (3 marks)

One technique that violinists can use is known as 'harmonics'. To play a 'harmonic', the violinist will place their finger lightly on the string in certain points. Instead of shortening the effective length of the string, the finger forces that point to be a node (zero displacement), while still allowing the entire string to oscillate.
