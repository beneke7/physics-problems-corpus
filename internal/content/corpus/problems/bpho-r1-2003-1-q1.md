e) Van der Waals proposed modifications to ' $P V=R T^{\prime}$, the equation of state for an ideal gas. The modified equation is

$$
\left(P+\frac{a}{V^{2}}\right)(V-b)=R T

(i) What are the units, or dimensions, of $a$ and $b$ ?
(ii) What is the physical meaning of $b$ and $a$ ?
(iii) Calculate the pressure of 2.00 mol of nitrogen at 200 K contained in a volume of $6.00 \times 10^{-3} \mathrm{~m}^{3}$ if $a=0.140 \mathrm{SI}$ units and $b=3.90 \times 10^{-5} \mathrm{SI}$ units.
(iv) What would be the pressure of the gas if it were ideal?
f) The experimental values of two quantities, $X$ and $Y$, are thought to satisfy one of the following two theoretical equations:
(i) $X=a Y^{b}$
(ii) $X^{3}=(c Y+d)^{2}$
