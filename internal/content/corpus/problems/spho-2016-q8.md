8. Consider a spaceship initially at rest in the lab frame. At a given instant, it starts to accelerate with constant proper acceleration $a$. Assume that the lab clock $(t)$ and spaceship clock $\left(t^{\prime}\right)$ are synchronized such that this happens when $t=t^{\prime}=0$.
[Note: Proper acceleration is defined such that: at $t^{\prime}+d t^{\prime}$, the spaceship is moving at speed $a d t^{\prime}$ relative to the frame it was in at time $t^{\prime}$.]
(a) Determine the relative speed of the spaceship and the lab frame in terms of $t^{\prime}$. Also, check the limit of the relative speed found when $a t^{\prime} \ll c$ and comment.
[5 marks]
(b) Find the relation between $t$ and $t^{\prime}$ at later stage. Also, explore the relation for condition $a t^{\prime} \gg c$. Comment.
[5 marks]
