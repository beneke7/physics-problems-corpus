One end of a chord is attached to a small body which undergoes circular motion on a horizontal frictionless table. There is a hole on the table at the centre of the circle, the chord goes through the hole and the other end of the chord is held by our hand. Pulling this end the body moves from the path of radius r $_{1}$ to another path of radius r $_{2}$. By what factor does the kinetic energy of the body change? By what factor does the tension change?
 (4 pont)
