A ball which was dropped from a height of $h$ in a stationary elevator is bouncing for a time of $t$.
 $a)$ What is the coefficient of restitution $k$, which characterises the elasticity of the impact? (The constant $k$ is the ratio of the linear momentum of the ball before the impact to that of after the impact.)
 $b)$ The elevator is moving upwards at a speed of $v$. How long does the ball bounce when it is dropped from a height of $h$?
