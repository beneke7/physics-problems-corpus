3. A thick cylindrical steel plate of diameter $D=1 \mathrm{~m}$ has a hole (of diameter $\delta=1 \mathrm{~cm}$ ) drilled through the middle. A rigid narrow tube (of matching inner diameter $\delta$ ), open at both ends, is attached and sealed to the top of the hole in the plate. The resulting column through the plate and tube has a height $h=10 \mathrm{~m}$. The plate and tube form a piston assembly of mass $M=10^{3} \mathrm{~kg}$.
The piston assembly is housed in a close-fitting cylindrical cavity, sealed with a frictionless seal that allows the piston to move up and down. The piston is initially held in a position such that the cavity underneath it is $d=10 \mathrm{~cm}$ deep. With the piston held in this position, water (of density $\rho=10^{3} \mathrm{~kg} \mathrm{~m}^{-3}$ ) is poured in slowly from the top of the tube, such that the entire volume of air is gradually and completely replaced by water. The tube is fully filled with water, and the system is released from rest.
![](spot-2020-q2-fig01.png)
    (a) Calculate the equilibrium height of the water in the tube (measured from the bottom surface of the piston).
    (b) Which direction did the piston move?
