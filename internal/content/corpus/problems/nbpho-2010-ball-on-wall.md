**4. BALL (6 points)**

Massive spherical ball has a mass $M = 100$ kg; an attempt is made to roll the ball upwards, along a vertical wall, by applying a force $F$ to some point $P$ on the ball. The coefficient of friction between the wall and the ball is $\mu = 0.7$.

**i)** *(5 pt)* What is the minimal force $F_{\mathrm{min}}$ required to achieve this goal?

**ii)** *(1 pt)* On a side view of the ball and the wall, construct geometrically the point $P$, where the force has to be applied to, together with the direction of the applied force.
