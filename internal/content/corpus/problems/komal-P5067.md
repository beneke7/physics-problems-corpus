Two light springs, whose spring constants are $D_1$ and $D_2$ are put onto a frictionless rod. One end of each spring is fixed and the distance between their other ends is $d$. There is also a small object of mass $m$ on the rod, and with this small object one of the springs is compressed by $x$, and then the object is released. How much time elapses until the object of mass $m$ reaches its initial position? Does this time depend on the spring it was started from?
