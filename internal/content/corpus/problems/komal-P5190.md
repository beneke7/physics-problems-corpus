The bottom end of a vertical, thin-walled glass tube has a shape of a hemisphere. The diameter of the tube is 10 cm. The tube is filled with water up to a height of 20 cm. 30 cm above the water level and along the axis of the tube there is a small light source. 
 $a)$ Where should we place a screen in order to gain a sharp image of the light source? 
 $b)$ What is the magnification? 
 (The refractive index of water is $n= 4/3$.) 
 (5 pont)
