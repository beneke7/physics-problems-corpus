The work function of the cathode of a photocell in a circuit is $6\cdot 10^{-19}$ J. Ultraviolet wave of wavelength 250 nm hits the cathode perpendicularly. 
 $a)$ What is the stopping potential at which the photo-current becomes zero? 
 $b)$ What is the linear momentum given to the cathode, during one elementary process, if the emitted electron is ejected oppositely to the direction of the radiation? 
 $c)$ Let us change the frequency of the radiation. At most by what factor can the linear momentum of the emitted electron be greater than that of the entering photon?
