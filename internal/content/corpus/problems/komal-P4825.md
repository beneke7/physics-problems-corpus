A thermally insulated container is divided into two parts of volumes $V$ and $2V$ by a thermally insulated piston. Initially both parts contain air at the temperature of $T_0=300~\rm K$, and at the pressure of $p_0=10^5~\rm Pa$. An electric heater of resistance $R=100\,\Omega$, rated at $U=230$ V, is built into that part which has a volume of $V=2$ litres. The heater is operated until the values of the volume of the gas at the two parts ``swap over'', that is, in accordance with the figure the volume of the gas at the left hand-side of the container becomes $V$ , and the volume of the gas at the right hand-side becomes $2V$ . 

 $a)$ What are the temperature values at the two parts of the container at the end? 
 $b)$ How long did the process take?
