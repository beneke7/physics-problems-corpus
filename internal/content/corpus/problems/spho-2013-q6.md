6. On a smooth and insulating large ring of radius $R$, there is a small ring of mass $m$ and carrying charge $q$. The large ring is placed horizontally and in a uniform magnetic field of strength $B_{0}$ and perpendicular to the ring plane. Starting at $t=0$, the magnetic field is changed to $B(t)=B_{0}+\alpha t$. Find the force of the small ring acting on the big ring afterwards and describe the motion of the small ring.
