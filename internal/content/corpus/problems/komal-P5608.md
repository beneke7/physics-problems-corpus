An explosive projectile of mass $8~\mathrm{kg}$ is fired at an angle of $60^\circ$ with respect to the horizontal, with an initial speed of $120~\mathrm{m}/\mathrm{s}$. It explodes at the top of its trajectory into two pieces of masses $3~\mathrm{kg}$ and $5~\mathrm{kg}$ such that the relative velocities of the two pieces are perpendicular to the plane of the trajectory of the projectile. 80% of the $12~\mathrm{kJ}$ energy released in the explosion is used to increase the kinetic energy of the pieces. From the position of the launch 
 a) how far and 
 b) at what speed will the two pieces reach the ground? 
 (Neglect air resistance.) 
 (4 pont)
