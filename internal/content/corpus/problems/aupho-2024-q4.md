4. Assuming that the depth of the hole is 1.50m, the density of compacted dirt is $1600 \mathrm{~kg} / \mathrm{m}^{3}$ and the density of uncompacted dirt is $1200 \mathrm{~kg} / \mathrm{m}^{3}$. How much work is done (in joules) against gravity to lift the dirt? Please assume that the acceleration due to gravity is $9.81 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}^{2}$ for this question. (1 mark)
