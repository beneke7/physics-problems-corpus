The most famous geyser in Yellowstone National Park is Old Faithful. This geyser can be considered as a large underground cavity with a narrow flue leading to the surface.
![](../../../../../sources/200ppp/figures/200ppp-062.jpg)

The surrounding earth is warm as a result of residual volcanic activity and boils the water in the cavity. After coming to the boil, the water in the flue is expelled, and approximately 44 tons of steam leave the geyser in 4 minutes. After the eruption, underground springs refill the cavity and the flue to ground level in 20-30 minutes, and the process then repeats itself. An eruption occurs every 90 minutes.

Geological experiments show that the underground temperature in this area increases by $1^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ for each metre of depth. Determine the minimum distance below the surface at which the cavity is situated. If the cavity is assumed to be located at this minimum depth, what is its volume?
