volume of the envelope filled with hot air is 650 m3 . The molar mass of air is 29 g/mol. Treat the
    temperature of the air in the balloon as uniform.

      (a) The balloon can either be tightly sealed, so that none of its air mixes with the outside air,
          or have a hole, so that its pressure equalizes with that of the outside air. For the purpose of
          generating lift, which is better?

      (b) Assuming the better option has been taken, to what temperature must the air inside the
          balloon be heated to make the balloon begin to rise?
