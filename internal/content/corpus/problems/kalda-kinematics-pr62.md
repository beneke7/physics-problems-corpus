On a wide river, a motorboat moves with a constant speed $v=7 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$ from village $A$ to village $B$ over the river. When answering the following questions you may take measurements from the figure below depicting waves behind the boat. What is the water speed in the river, and what is the water depth $h$ ? Note: wave speed in shallow water ${ }^{17}$ is $w=\sqrt{g h}$, where $g=9.81 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}^{2}$.
