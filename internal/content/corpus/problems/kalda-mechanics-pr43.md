A boy is dragging a sled by the rope behind him as he slowly ascends a hill. What is the work that the boy does to transport the sled to the tip of the hill if its height is $h$ and the horizontal distance from the foot of the hill to its tip is $a$ ? Assume that the rope is always parallel to the tangent of the hill's slope, and that the coefficient of friction between the sled and the snow is $\mu$.
