The goal-keeper is just about to kick off the ball. The kick lasts for $\Delta
t=0.01$ s, during which the goalkeeper exerts an average force of $F=850$ N on the ball lying on the ground. If there is no air drag, the kicked ball would reach the ground in $t=3$ s and it would be $\ell=45$ m further.
 $a)$ What is the greatest height that the ball could reach?
 $b)$ According to the official rules of association football the mass of the ball must be between 410 g and 450 g. Is the match played according to the rules?
 (4 pont)
