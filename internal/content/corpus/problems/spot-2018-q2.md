2. The cross-section of a glass prism is an isoceles triangle with its pointy top chopped off as shown, and has its base immersed in water. Let the refractive indices of glass and water be $n_{g}$ and $n_{w}$ respectively, with $n_{g}>n_{w}$.
![](spot-2018-q2-fig1.png)

(a) Find the constraints on the angle $\theta$ such that a ray of light parallel to the top and bottom surfaces of the prism is totally reflected at the base of the prism.
(b) The prism is now lifted completely out of the water and dried.
    i. Discuss if and how the constraint on $\theta$ found in the earlier part changes.
ii. What is the orientation of the transmitted image of the arrow AB?
iii. What is the orientation of the transmitted image of the arrow AB, when the prism is rotated an angle $\phi$ around the axis defined by the incident beam, for $\phi=\pi / 2$ and $\phi=\pi$ respectively?
