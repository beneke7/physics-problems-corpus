On a boat trip we bought vanilla ice cream, which is sold in a thin-walled, roughly cuboid shape ice cream container. The product's label states that it contains 1250 g ice cream, which has a net volume of 2500 ml. The empty box weighs 81 g and when filled to the top with water, it weighs 2738 g.
 The sealed, unopened ice cream container is dropped into the water with the top facing up. Estimate what fraction of its height is submerged in the water, and up to what fraction of its height it is filled with ice cream.
