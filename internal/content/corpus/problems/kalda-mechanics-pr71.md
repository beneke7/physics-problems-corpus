A cylindrical ice hockey puck with a uniform thickness and density is given an angular velocity $\omega$ and a translational velocity $u$. What trajectory will the puck follow if the ice is equally slippery everywhere? In which case will it slide farther: when $\omega=0$ or when $\omega \neq 0$, assuming that in both cases $u$ is the same?
