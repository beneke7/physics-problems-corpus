**3. MAGNETS (6 points)**

To explore the force between two small magnets, the following experiment is performed. One of the magnets is hanged from a thread with length $l=1 \mathrm{~m}$. Other magnet is moved slowly closer while keeping the axes of the magnets always on the same horizontal line. At the moment when the distance between the magnets is $d_{1}=4 \mathrm{~cm}$ and the hanged magnet has moved $x_{1}=1 \mathrm{~cm}$ from initial position, balance is lost and the magnets pull together. By making the assumption that the pulling force between the magnets $F_{\mathrm{m}}$ depends on the distance $d$ according to the relation $F_{\mathrm{m}} \propto d^{-n}$, find the value of the exponent $n$.
