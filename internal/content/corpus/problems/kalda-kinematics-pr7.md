After being kicked by a footballer, a ball started to fly straight towards the goal at velocity $v=25 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$ making an angle $\alpha=\arccos 0.8$ with the horizontal. Due to side wind blowing at $u=10 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$ perpendicular the initial velocity of the ball, the ball had deviated from its initial course by $s=2 \mathrm{~m}$ by the time it reached the plane of the goal. Find the time that it took the ball to reach the plane of the goal, if the goal was situated at distance $L=32 \mathrm{~m}$ from the footballer.
