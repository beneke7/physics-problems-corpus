A rocket is intended to leave the Earth's gravitational field. The fuel in its main engine is a little less than the amount that is necessary, and an auxiliary engine, only capable of operating for a short time, has to be used as well. When is it best to switch on the auxiliary engine: at take-off, or when the rocket has nearly stopped with respect to the Earth, or does it not matter?
