An explosive projectile of mass $M$ was fired at an angle of $\alpha$ and at an initial speed of $v_0$. At the top of its trajectory, the projectile exploded into two parts of masses $m_1$ and $m_2$, and the part with mass $m_2$ started to fall freely at this moment.
 $a)$ How far apart will the two parts hit the ground?
 $b)$ What are the velocities of the two parts at their impact with the ground? (Determine both the speed and the direction of he velocity.)
 ( Data: $M = 0.6$ kg, $m_1 = 0.2$ kg, $\alpha=60^\circ$, $v_0 = 100$ m/s. Air drag is negligible.)
 (4 pont)
