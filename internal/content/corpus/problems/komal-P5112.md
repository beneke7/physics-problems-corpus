From a wall of height $H$ a snowball was thrown at an initial speed of $v_0$ and at an angle of $\alpha$ with respect to the horizontal. A child, who was at a distance of $s$ from the wall, began to run at the same moment when the snowball was thrown. What was the initial direction and speed of the child, if he ran at a constant speed along a straight line and the snowball hit him? (Air drag is negligible.) Both the motions of the child and the snowball are in a vertical plane, which is perpendicular to the wall. 
 Data: $H=45$ m, $s=21$ m, $v_0=5$ m/s, $\alpha=30^\circ$. 
 (4 pont)
