(a) Find the period of small oscillations, assuming no friction. Can you give an intuitive explana-
          tion of the simplicity of your answer?

      (b) What is the period if the block is replaced with a small uniform ball, with sufficient friction
          to roll without slipping? Neglect the ball’s radius.

      (c) Now suppose the block moves in a circle, staying a constant height h ≪ R above the bottom
          of the bowl. Find the period of the motion, assuming no friction.

      (d) Again, find the period if the block is replaced with a small uniform ball, with sufficient friction
          to roll without slipping.

      (e) Now consider part (c) again. Suppose that at some moment, the speed of the block is
          instantaneously increased by a small amount. Qualitatively describe the subsequent motion,
          e.g. sketch what a top-down view would look like. What if h is not small?
