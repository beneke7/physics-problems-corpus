Show that for three parallel slits of equal size and with neighbour-to-neighbour distances being equal to $a$ and $b$, respectively, the intensity at the diffraction minima is non-zero unless $\frac{a}{b}=\frac{n}{m}$, where $n$ and $m$ are integers and $n-m$ is a multiple of three.
