A plane mirror is placed horizontally on a horizontal floor, and above it another plane mirror, facing towards the first one is placed as well. There is a small black spot in the middle of the top mirror. The mirror at the top is released and begins to fall at an acceleration of $g$. What are the magnitudes and the directions of the acceleration of the images of the spot? 
 (4 pont)
