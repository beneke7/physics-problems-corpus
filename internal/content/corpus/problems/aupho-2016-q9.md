Question 9
A roller-coaster car is full of water, and is moving at constant velocity along a frictionless horizontal track. Someone removes a plug in the bottom of the floor, allowing the water to drain out. Ignoring air resistance, as the water drains out the car will:

a. slow down.
b. continue to move at constant velocity.
c. speed up.
d. initially slow down, then speed up.
e. initially speed up, then slow down.
