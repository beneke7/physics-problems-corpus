A1. An empty 4.2-g balloon is filled with air to a pressure of 2.0 atm at the ambient temperature of $20.0^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$. When filled it is spherical with a radius of 15.0 cm . The mean molecular mass of air is $28.8 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{mole}$ and the atmospheric pressure is $1.01 \times 10^{5} \mathrm{~Pa}$.
(15) a. The filled balloon is weighed by placing it on a scale. What is the scale reading?
(5) b. The filled balloon is now gently heated until its radius increases by $10 \%$. What is the scale reading now?
