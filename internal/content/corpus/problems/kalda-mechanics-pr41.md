Sand is transported to the construction site using a conveyor belt. The length of the belt is $l$, the angle with respect to the horizontal is $\alpha$; the belt is driven by the lower pulley with radius $R$, powered externally. The sand is put onto the belt at a constant rate $\mu(\mathrm{kg} / \mathrm{s})$. What is the minimal required torque needed to transport the sand? What is the velocity of the belt at that torque? The coefficient of friction is large enough for the sand grains to stop moving immediately after hitting the belt; take the initial velocity of the sand grains to be zero.
