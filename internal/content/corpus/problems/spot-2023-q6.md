6. This question is about deriving thermodynamic identities.
(a) Consider a mole of ideal gas at pressure $P$, volume $V$, and temperature $T$. Denote the heat capacity at constant volume as $C_{V}$. Show that the heat capacity $C$ is given by
$$
C=C_{V}+\frac{R}{1+\frac{V}{P} \frac{d P}{d V}} .
$$
(b) Consider two ideal gases $A$ and $B$ which are mixed. There are $n_{1}$ moles of gas $A$ and $n_{2}$ moles of gas $B$, and the molar heat capacities at constant volume of gases $A$ and $B$ are $c_{v 1}$ and $c_{v 2}$ respectively. Find the adiabatic constant of the mixed gas.
