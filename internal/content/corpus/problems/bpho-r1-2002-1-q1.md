c) A rocket, of constant mass $2.00 \times 10^{3} \mathrm{~kg}$, is fired vertically upwards with constant acceleration. After one minute it reaches a height of 36.0 km and its engine is switched off. Assuming the rocket's mass and $g$ remain constant, calculate:

Determine for the ${ }^{12} \mathrm{C}_{6}$ nucleus:
(i) its mass in atomic mass units

Sketch a graph of the height of the centre of gravity, $y$, against the height, $h$, of liquid, giving an explanation for the behavior of the graph.
