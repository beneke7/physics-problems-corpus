We want to make a ping-pong ball bounce uniformly with the help of a motor in the following way: a piston is moved along a vertical axis with an amplitude of 3 cm, and the ball always hits the piston in the equilibrium position once in a period. What should the frequency of the motor be in order to achieve this process? The coefficient of restitution is $k=0.8$.
 (4 pont)
