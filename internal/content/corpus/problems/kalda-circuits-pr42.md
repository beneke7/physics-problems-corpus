The figure below depicts a cube, the edges of which (blue lines) are made of a resistive wire, so that the resistance of each edge is $R=1 \mathrm{k} \Omega$. The ammeters are connected with copper wires of negligible resistance to the vertices of the cube. The battery voltage is $\mathcal{E}=9 \mathrm{~V}$; the wires make electrical contact only at the vertices of the cube. Find the readings of the ammeters.
