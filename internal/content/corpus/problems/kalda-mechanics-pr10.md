A rod with length $l$ is hinged to a ceiling with height $h<l$. Underneath, a board is being dragged on the floor. The rod is meant to block the movement the board in one direction while allowing it move in the opposite direction. What condition should be fulfilled for it to do its job? The coefficient of friction is $\mu_{1}$ between the board and the rod, and $\mu_{2}$ between the board and the floor.
