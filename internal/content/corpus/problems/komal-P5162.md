The faces of an equilateral-triangle-based right prism are plane mirrors. The prism executes uniform rotational motion of period $T$ about a vertical axis which goes through the centroid of the horizontal base of the prism. A horizontal beam of laser light aimed at the axis of rotation is incident on one of the faces. At the instant $t=0$ the beam is perpendicular to one of the mirrors. Determine and graph the angle between the incident and the reflected laser beam as a function of the elapsed time over the interval $0\le t\le T$.
 (4 pont)
