Through a given point $P$ of a vertical plane, slopes with different angles of inclination are laid (perpendicularly to the plane), and point-like objects, which were released from rest, slide down the planes. What is the locus of points which are reached by the sliding objects in a given time of $t$? The coefficient of friction between the slopes and the objects is $\mu$. 
 (6 pont)
