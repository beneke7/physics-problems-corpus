Two thermally insulated containers hold identical masses of water. The water is at temperature $T_{1}$ in one of them, but at temperature $T_{2}$, ( $T_{2}>T_{1}$ ), in the other. What is the maximum work that this system can do if it is used as a heat engine? Take the specific heat of water as constant over the working range.
