Q9
    (a) The energy levels, $E_{n}$, of the hydrogen atom are given by $E_{n}=-13.6 / n^{2} \mathrm{eV}, \quad$ where $n=1,2,3, \ldots$.
What is the wavelength of the photon that results from a transition from the second excited state to the ground state?
    [3]
    (b) A 0.50 mW laser, of wavelength 590 nm producing a beam with a divergence angle of $1.5 \times 10^{-3}$ radians, is pointing at the Moon. What is the maximum number of photons arriving per second per square metre on the Moon?
    [5] The Earth - Moon distance $R_{E M}=3.84 \times 10^{8} \mathrm{~m}$.
    (c) A photon of frequency $f_{1}$ and wavelength $\lambda_{1}$, momentum $h / \lambda_{1}$, is scattered by a stationary electron. A photon of frequency $f_{2}$ and wavelength $\lambda_{2}$ results. It travels in the opposite direction to the initial photon and the electron gains energy of 5.00 keV, with velocity $v$ in the same direction as the incident photon. Determine numerically the value of $\lambda_{1}$.
    [8]
    (d) Monochromatic light of wavelength 380 nm is incident on a metal surface. A potential of 1.32 V is required to cut off the flow of photoelectrons. What is the work function of the metal?
    [4]
