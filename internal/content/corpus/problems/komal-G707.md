Sam and Sarah are jogging along a straight path at constant speed. Sam's speed is 3 m/s and Sara's speed is 2 m/s. Pluto, their dog, is running back and forth between them. Initially Sarah is 20 m ahead Sam. Pluto is a ``miraculous'' dog, because he can run between them at a constant speed of 4 m/s such that all his turns are immediate. Considering Pluto's arbitrary initial location and direction of running, determine the smallest and greatest values of both the distance covered and the displacement of the dog until Sam and Sarah meet.
 (4 pont)
