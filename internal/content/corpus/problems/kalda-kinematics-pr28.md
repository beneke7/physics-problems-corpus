A single-lane road is full of cars which move at the speed $v=90 \mathrm{~km} / \mathrm{h}$. The average distance between neighbouring cars is such that a standing observer would measure a time lapse $\tau=2 \mathrm{~s}$ from the head of the first car until the head of the second car. One car has to stop due to malfunctioning and a queue of standing cars starts forming behind it. Find the speed $u$ with which the queue length is growing if the average distance between the centres of neighbouring cars in the queue is $l=6 \mathrm{~m}$ ?
