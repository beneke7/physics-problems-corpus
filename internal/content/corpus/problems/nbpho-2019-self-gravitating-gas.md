**6. SELF-GRAVITATING GAS (10 points)** — *Eero Vaher (v: Jaan Kalda).*

Consider a ball of monoatomic ideal gas at temperature $T$ which keeps a spherically symmetric mechanically stable stationary shape due to its own gravity field. Let the total mass of the gas be $M_{0}$, and its molar mass $\mu$. We shall describe the radial mass distribution in terms of the total mass $M=M(r)$ inside a sphere of radius $r$, and the pressure $p=p(r)$ as a function of distance $r$ from the centre of the spherical gas cloud.

**i)** *(2 points)* State the mechanical equilibrium condition for a small parcel of gas of mass $m$ and volume $v$ at distance $r$ from the centre of the cloud in terms of the local pressure gradient $p^{\prime}(r)=\frac{\mathrm{d} p}{\mathrm{~d} r}$, the local value of $M(r)$, and the appropriate constants of nature. Simplify your expression by noticing that $m / v=\rho$ is the local density of the gas.

**ii)** *(2 points)* Show that the total thermal energy of the gas can be expressed as

$$U=-\alpha \int V \mathrm{~d} p,$$

where $V=\frac{4}{3} \pi r^{3}$, and $\alpha$ is a numerical factor, and the integral is taken from the centre of the cloud, to very far distances from the centre, where the pressure is negligibly small. Find the value of $\alpha$.

**iii)** *(3 points)* Based on your previous results, show that $U=-\beta E_{G}$, where $E_{G}$ is the gravitational potential energy of the gas, and $\beta<1$ is a positive numerical factor. Find the value of $\beta$.

**iv)** *(1 point)* How will the temperature and characteristic radius of the cloud change in time due to heat radiation? Give a qualitative motivated answer. (The characteristic radius $R_{c}$ can be defined as such a radius that half of the mass of the cloud is inside the sphere of radius $R_{c}$.)

**v)** *(2 points)* Consider now a similar fully ionized plasma cloud. Assume that plasma is a macroscopically neutral mixture of electrons and protons. What is the proportionality factor between the total gravitational energy and total thermal energy for the plasma cloud?
