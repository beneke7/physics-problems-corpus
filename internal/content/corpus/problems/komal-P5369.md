There are two parallel metal rails in a horizontal plane, at a distance of $\ell$. The left ends of the rails are connected with a resistor of resistance $R$ and a switch as shown in the figure. The arrangement is in vertical uniform magnetic field which varies with time according to the following relation: $B(t)=B_0+kt$, where $B_0$ and $k$ are known constants. 
 Perpendicular to the rails, a metal rod is placed at a distance of $d$ from the switch, to the right side of the switch. The resistance of the rails and the metal rod is negligible. At time $t=0$ the switch is closed and we begin to move the rod in the horizontal plane perpendicularly to the rod, at a constant speed of $v_0$. Determine the current flowing in the rod at time $t$.
