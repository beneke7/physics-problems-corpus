Three identical cylinders of radius $R$ and mass $m$ are made from ice and they are all released without initial velocity from the position shown in the figure. The surface of the ice is smooth, so friction is negligible everywhere. 

 $a)$ Determine and sketch the kinetic energy of one of the lower ice cylinders as a function of the displacement of the upper cylinder. 
 $b)$ What is the speed at which the top ice cylinder hits the ground, and to what speed do the other two ice cylinders accelerate?
