A point mass of 0.5 kg moving with a constant speed of $5 \mathrm{~m} \mathrm{~s}^{-1}$ on an elliptical track experiences an outward force of 10 N when at either endpoint of the major axis and a similar force of 1.25 N at each end of the minor axis. How long are the axes of the ellipse?
