Two resistors of resistances 1 k and 4 k are connected in series to a 220 V voltage supply of negligible internal resistance. Across the 4 k resistor a moving coil voltmeter reads 147 V. What would be the reading on this voltmeter if it was connected to measure the voltage across the other resistor?
