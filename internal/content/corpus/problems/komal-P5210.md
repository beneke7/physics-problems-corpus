The crew of the spacecraft Apollo 11 (Neil Armstrong, Edwin Aldrin és Michael Collins) performed the first landing on the Moon. The spacecraft was launched from the Kennedy Space Center on July 16, 1969.
 $a)$ Apollo 11 was launched by a Saturn V rocket, which had a thrust of $34\,000$ kN. The total mass of the spacecraft and the rocket after a few seconds of the launch was 2.8 millions of kilograms. What was the acceleration of the spacecraft at that time?
 $b)$ The spacecraft left the Earth orbit on July 16 at 16:22, and after travelling around $380\,000$ km, it entered lunar orbit on July 19 at 17:21. What was the approximate average speed of the spacecraft while it travelled from the Earth to the Moon?
 (4 pont)
