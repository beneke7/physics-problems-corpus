## Problem 2. Thermodynamic cycle (5 points)

Calculate the thermal efficiency of an ideal-gas cycle consisting of two isotherms at temperatures $T_{1}$ and $T_{2}$, and two isochores joining them. (An isochore is a constant-volume process.) The engine is constructed so that the heat released during the cooling isochore is used for feeding the heating isochore.
