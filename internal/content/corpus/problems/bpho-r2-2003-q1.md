Q1
In this question you are asked to make reasoned estimates and assumptions. These must be clearly stated.

a)
    (i) On a really dark moonless night the planet Venus is sufficiently bright to produce shadows of objects on the Earth. Explain why the shadows are different to those produced by the Sun and the Moon.
    (ii) It is important not to under inflate car tyres - they could overheat and explode. Why is this?
(iii) It is difficult to open small plastic bags for vegetables in the supermarket. A sharp blow along the edge in the plane of the bag usually works. Why?

    (iv) How long will it take to hard boil an ostrich egg? Explain how you reached your answer.
