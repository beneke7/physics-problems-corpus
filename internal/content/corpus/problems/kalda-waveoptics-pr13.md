A detector of radiowaves in a radioastronomical observatory is placed on the sea beach at height $h=2 \mathrm{~m}$ above the sea level. After the rise of a star, radiating electromagnetic waves of wavelength $\lambda=21 \mathrm{~cm}$, above the horizon the detector registers series of alternating maxima and minima. The registered signal is proportional to the intensity of the detected waves. The detector registers waves with electric vector, vibrating in a direction parallel to the sea surface.
(i) Determine the angle between the star and the horizon in the moment when the detector registers maxima and minima (in general form).
(ii) Does the signal decrease or increase just after the rise of the star?
