5. In Young's double slit experiment, one of the slits is wider than the other, so that the amplitude of the light from one slit is double that from the other slit. If $I_{m}$ be the maximum intensity, the resultant intensity when they interfere at phase difference $\phi$ is given by
    (a) $\frac{I_{m}}{3}\left(1+2 \cos ^{2} \frac{\phi}{2}\right)$
    (b) $\frac{I_{m}}{5}\left(1+4 \cos ^{2} \frac{\phi}{2}\right)$
    (c) $\frac{I_{m}}{9}\left(1+8 \cos ^{2} \frac{\phi}{2}\right)$
    (d) $\frac{I_{m}}{9}\left(8+\cos ^{2} \frac{\phi}{2}\right)$
