Where is gravitational acceleration greater, on the surface of the Earth, or 100 km underground? Take the Earth as spherically symmetrical. The average density of the Earth is $5500 \mathrm{~kg} \mathrm{~m}^{-3}$, and that of its crust is $3000 \mathrm{~kg} \mathrm{~m}^{-3}$. (The depth of the crust may be assumed to be at least 100 km .)
