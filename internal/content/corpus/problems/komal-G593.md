The driver of a car travelling at a speed of 18 km/h catches the sight of a van, 250 m ahead of the car, travelling at a constant speed of 54 km/h. Then the car starts to accelerate at a constant rate, and it reaches the van in 20 seconds.
 $a)$ What is the acceleration of the car?
 $b)$ By what distance does the car overtake the van during the next 20 seconds, if it stops accelerating at the moment when it reaches the van?
 (3 pont)
