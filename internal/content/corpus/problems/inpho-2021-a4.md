4. [12 marks] Three thin convex lenses $\mathrm{L}_{1}, \mathrm{~L}_{2}$, and $\mathrm{L}_{3}$ with focal lengths $f_{1}, f_{2}$, and $f_{3}$, respectively, are arranged in order ( $\mathrm{L}_{1}$ followed by $\mathrm{L}_{2}$, followed by $\mathrm{L}_{3}$ from left to right) with their principal axes coincident. The distance $d_{12}$ between $\mathrm{L}_{1}$ and $\mathrm{L}_{2}$, and the distance $d_{23}$ between $\mathrm{L}_{2}$ and $\mathrm{L}_{3}$ are such that $d_{12}+d_{23} \geq f_{1}+4 f_{2}+f_{3}$. If a parallel beam of light incident on $\mathrm{L}_{1}$ at a small angle to the principal axis remains parallel to itself when leaving the system after passing through $\mathrm{L}_{2}$ and $\mathrm{L}_{3}$, draw the appropriate ray diagram and determine $d_{12}$ and $d_{23}$ in terms of $f_{1}, f_{2}$, and $f_{3}$.
