A meteorite of mass $m$, of specific heat capacity $c$ and of specific latent heat of fusion $L$, consists of some material which is extremely good heat conductor. When it reaches the atmosphere of the Earth the temperature of the meteorite is $\Delta T$ below its melting point. In the atmosphere due to friction heat is generated in it at a rate of $P$. How long does it take for the meteorite to be melted totally?
 (3 pont)
