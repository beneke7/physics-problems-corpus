A stick with uniform density rests with one end against the ground and with the other against the wall. Initially it was vertical and began sliding from rest such that all of the subsequent motion takes place in a plane that is perpendicular to the intersection line of the floor and the wall. What was the angle between the stick and the wall at the moment when the stick lost contact with the wall? Ignore friction.
