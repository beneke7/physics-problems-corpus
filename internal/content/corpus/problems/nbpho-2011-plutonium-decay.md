**5. PLUTONIUM DECAY (3 points)**

Plutonium is an unstable element, a $\text{Pu}^{239}$ atom decays with a half-life of $\tau_{1/2} = 24000$ years by creating smaller nuclei, including an $\alpha$-particle. Find the $\alpha$ particle flux density (i.e. the number of passing nuclei per unit time and per unit cross-sectional area) near the surface of a plate of $\text{Pu}^{239}$. The plate has thickness $d = 1 \, \text{mm}$; its width and length are much larger than that. The density of plutonium $\rho = 19800 \, \text{kg/m}^3$. Remark: half-life is the period of time it takes for a substance undergoing decay to decrease in size (in the number of particles) by half. The mass of an atom of $\text{Pu}^{239}$ is $m_0 = 3.84 \times 10^{-25} \, \text{kg}$.
