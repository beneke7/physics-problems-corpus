Q2.
A small sound transmitter T radiates uniformly in all directions and at four times the power of each of two similar transmitters, $S_{1}$ and $S_{2}$, which are situated a distance $l=0.25 \mathrm{~m}$ on either side of T , along a north-south line. T is wired to be at $180^{\circ}$ out of phase with $\mathrm{S}_{1}$ and $\mathrm{S}_{2}$. All three transmitters emit a 200 kHz signal. A small receiver, R , is placed a distance $d=10.0 \mathrm{~m}$ due east of T and slowly moved eastwards; being, in general, a distance $x$ from T, $x \geq 10.0 \mathrm{~m}$. The speed of sound is $330 \mathrm{~ms}^{-1}$.
(a)
(i) How does the intensity, $I$, of a simple point source of sound vary with distance $r$ from the source?
(ii) Show that the path difference $p=\mathrm{S}_{1} \mathrm{R}$ - TR is given approximately by

$$
p=\frac{1}{32 x}, \quad x \geq 10
$$

(b) When all transmitters are on, determine the condition for the intensity of the signal at R to be:
(i) a maximum
(ii) a minimum
(iii) Where do these maxima and minima occur?
(iv) Sketch the variation of the intensity, $I$, against $x$, for $x \geq 10$, when all transmitters are on.
[9]
(c) At the position/s of maximum signal intensity the following transmitters are switched off:
(i) $\quad S_{1}$ and $S_{2}$
(ii) T .

Determine, with an explanation, by what factor the power received falls in each case.
[4]
(d) Indicate graphically how the intensity, $I$, of the signal received at R varies with $x$ when:
(i) $\quad \mathrm{T}$ is switched off
(ii) $\quad \mathrm{S}_{2}$ is switched off

Note: $(1+y)^{1 / 2} \approx 1+\frac{1}{2} y \quad$ for $y \ll 1$
