Consider a black cube which is made from a perfectly heat-conducting material. A parallel beam of light with intensity $I\left(\mathrm{~W} / \mathrm{m}^{2}\right)$ falls onto this cube. The equilibrium temperature $T$ of the cube depends on its orientation; find the minimal and maximal values of $T$ ( $T_{\text {min }}$ and $T_{\text {max }}$, respectively).
