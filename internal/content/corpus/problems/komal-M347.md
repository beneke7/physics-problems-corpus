Fix a wooden skewer to the rim of the table. Gradually load its free end, or hang a spring balance to it. Measure the torque $M$ needed to break the skewer, and the work $W$ done by the load. Will the measured data change if the length $\ell$ of that part of the skewer which sticks out of the table is halved? 

 (6 pont)
