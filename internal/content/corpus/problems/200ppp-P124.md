A point-like electric dipole is placed between the earthed plates of the plane capacitor discussed in the previous problem. Its dipole momentum vector $\mathbf{p}$ is perpendicular to the plates and the distances of the dipole from the plates are $x$ and $d-x$, respectively.

How does the charge which accumulates on each of the plates depend on $x$ ? (Ignore edge effects.)
