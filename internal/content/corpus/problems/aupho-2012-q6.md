## Question 6

Twins Anna and Tom measure each other's heights each birthday. When they were 12 Anna's height was measured as 151.5 cm and Tom's was measured as 151 cm . On their 16th birthday Anna's height is measured as 167.5 cm and Tom's is measured as 168.5 cm . The uncertainty in the height measurements is 5 mm . Between the ages of 12 and 16 Anna grew:
a. $16 \mathrm{~cm} \pm 5 \mathrm{~cm}$.
b. $16 \mathrm{~cm} \pm 1 \mathrm{~cm}$.
c. $16 \mathrm{~cm} \pm 0.5 \mathrm{~cm}$.
d. $16.0 \mathrm{~cm} \pm 0.5 \mathrm{~cm}$.
e. $16.0 \mathrm{~cm} \pm 0.25 \mathrm{~cm}$.
