In Victor Hugo's novel les Misérables, the main character Jean Valjean, an escaped prisoner, was noted for his ability to climb up the corner formed by the intersection of two vertical perpendicular walls. Find the minimum force with which he had to push on the walls whilst climbing. What is the minimum coefficient of static friction required for him to be able to perform such a feat?
