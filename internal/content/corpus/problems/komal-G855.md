Newton did not write the mirror and lens equation in the usual form $\left(\tfrac{1}{t}+\tfrac{1}{k}=\tfrac{1}{f}\right)$. He measured the object distance $x_\mathrm{t}$ from the focus on the side of the object, and the image distance $x_\mathrm{k}$ from the focus on the image side as shown in the figure. 

 $a)$ Determine the lens equation with these parameters in its simplest form. 
 $b)$ How can this formula be used for a diverging lens?
