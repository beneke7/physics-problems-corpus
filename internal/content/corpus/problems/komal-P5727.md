A 30 cm high air column is enclosed by an 8 kg piston in a vertical, thermally insulated cylinder with a cross-sectional area of $2\,\mathrm{dm^2}$. The external air pressure is 100 kPa. The enclosed gas is kept in stable equilibrium by a constant downward force acting on the piston. At a certain moment the piston is released and allowed to move freely upward until it reaches a bumper located 10 cm above its initial position. A heating coil ensures that the gas inside the cylinder drives the piston upward with constant acceleration. 
 a) What is the initial pressure of the gas if the piston moves frictionlessly and the duration of its motion is 0.2 s? 
 b) Give the power of the heating coil as a function of time. 
 (4 pont)
