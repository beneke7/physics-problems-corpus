Question 3
A roller-coaster cart full of water is moving at a constant speed along a horizontal, frictionless length of track. Suddenly, a plug in the bottom of the cart is removed, and the water starts to flow downwards out of the cart. What happens to the speed of the cart while the water is flowing? Ignore air resistance in your answer.

a. The cart speeds up.
b. The cart slows down.
c. The cart speeds up until half the water is gone, then it slows down.
d. The cart slows down until half the water is gone, then it speeds up.
e. The cart's speed does not change.
