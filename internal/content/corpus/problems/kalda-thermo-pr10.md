Natural uranium consists of mainly two isotopes, $\mathrm{U}^{238}$ and $\mathrm{U}^{235}$, whereas the relative concentration of the latter is $0,7 \%$. Uranium is "enriched" (i.e. the concentration of $\mathrm{U}^{235}$ is increased) by implementing a multi-stage process, where at each stage, evaporated chemical compound $\mathrm{UF}_{6}$ is led through a porous wall. The porous wall can be considered to be a thin film having microscopic holes in it (the size of the holes is smaller than the mean free path of the molecules, but larger than the dimension of the molecules How many stages is needed to increase the $\mathrm{U}^{235}$ content to $1,4 \%$ ? The molar mass of fluoride (F) is $19 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{mol}$.
