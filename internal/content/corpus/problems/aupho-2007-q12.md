Molly the ant falls from a height $h$ above a conveyor belt which is moving at a constant, very high, velocity $v_{\mathrm{c}}$, and she bounces off at an angle $\theta$ to the horizontal. Molly has a mass $m$ and the coefficient of kinetic friction between Molly and the belt is $\mu$.

a. Find an expression for the velocity with which Molly hits the conveyor belt. (1 mark)
b. Draw a diagram showing all the forces acting on Molly during her collision with the conveyor belt. Indicate on your diagram the relative sizes of the forces in the vertical direction. (2 marks)

Impulse, $I$, is the change in momentum, $\Delta \mathbf{p}$, of an object caused by a force, $\mathbf{F}$, and is equal to the average force multiplied by the time, $\Delta t$, over which it acts. Hence $I=\Delta \mathbf{p}=\mathbf{F} . \Delta t$.

c. Write down an expression for the change in momentum of Molly in the horizontal and vertical directions in terms of the forces on your diagram and the time the collision takes, $\Delta t$. (2 marks)
d. Find expressions for Molly's final velocity in the horizontal and vertical directions. (2 marks)
e. If the average size of the normal force that Molly experiences during the collision is equal to 2 mg , find an expression for the time the collision takes, $\Delta t$. (3 marks)
