In three different containers there are three samples of air at three different temperature values of 300 K, 500 K and 720 K. Allowing heat transfer between the 300 K and the 500 K samples of gas, the common temperature would be 420 K, and allowing heat transfer between the 300 K and the 720 K samples of gas the common temperature would be 600 K. What would be the common temperature of the samples of air of initial temperatures of 500 K and 720 K? What is the ratio of the initial internal energy values of the three samples of gas?
 (4 pont)
