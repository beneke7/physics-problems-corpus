5. Explain why the centre of the model snake does not move forward after each cycle. (2 marks)

After doing some research, Student B finds that the kinetic friction coefficient between the snake and the ground is $\mu_{\mathrm{f}}=0.15$ when the snake moves forwards, but $\mu_{\mathrm{b}}=0.25$ when the snake moves backwards. This allows the model snake to move forward after each closing-opening cycle.
