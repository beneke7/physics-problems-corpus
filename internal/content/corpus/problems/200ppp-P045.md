Two elastic balls of masses $m_{1}$ and $m_{2}$ are placed on top of each other (with a small gap between them) and then dropped onto the ground. What is the ratio $m_{1} / m_{2}$, for which the upper ball ultimately receives the largest possible fraction of the total energy? What ratio of masses is necessary if the upper ball is to bounce as high as possible?
