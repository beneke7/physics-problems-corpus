## Question 11

Suggested Time: 25 min
The following parts of this question are unrelated and so may be completed in any order. Note that the data provided on the next page may be used for any part, as required. You must explain your reasoning for each part.

a) Calculate the amount of energy per gram of fat.
b) The typical office computer is only used one third of the time during business hours. Today a typical office computer consumes around 130 W of power.
Estimate the cost savings and reduction in greenhouse gas emissions over one year if everyone in an office of 60 people turns off their computers when they are not using them instead of leaving them on at all times, even over the weekend.
c) Estimate the energy efficiency of the conversion of sunlight into sugarcane.
d) "Kilogram per kilogram humans produce more heat than the Sun." Is this claim true?
