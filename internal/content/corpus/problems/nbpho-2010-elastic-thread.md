**5. ELASTIC THREAD (10 points)** — *Equipment: ruler, tape, an elastic thread, a wooden rod, marker, a known weight.*

The purpose of this problem is to study the elastic properties of an elastic thread for large relative deformations $\varepsilon = (l - l_0) / l_0$, where $l_0$ and $l$ are the lengths in initial and stretched states, respectively. If Hooke's law were valid, the ratio $F / \varepsilon$ of the elastic force $F$ and $\varepsilon$ would be constant: $F / \varepsilon = SE$, where $S$ is the cross-section area of the thread and $E$ is the Young modulus of the thread material.

**i)** Collect the data needed to plot the ratio $F / \varepsilon$ as a function of $\varepsilon$, up to $\varepsilon \approx 4$. Plot the appropriate graph, and indicate the uncertainties.

**ii)** By making the assumption that the Young modulus $E = F / (S \varepsilon)$ remains constant, study how the volume of the thread depends on $\varepsilon$. Plot the appropriate graph.
