The figure shows the sketch of the structure of an amusement park ride. The big cylinder-shaped pole at the centre is rotating uniformly. By four horizontal struts circular ``gondolas'' are attached to the pole and move around it. At the centre of each gondola a horizontal disc is fixed to the gondola such that the symmetry axis of the disc coincides with the vertical shaft of the gondola about which it can be rotated. These discs on the gondolas are connected by transmission belts to disc $K$, which is attached to the pole at the centre of the structure. Disc $K$ is fixed, and not rotating at all. (For clarity reasons only one of the transmission belts was drawn in the figure.) 

 At points $A$, $B$, $C$ and $D$ there is a passenger in each gondola. What is the path of each passenger? How does the distance between them change during the rotation? (The structure is rotating in the horizontal plane and all the rotational axes are vertical.) 
 (4 pont)
