The area of the plates of a parallel plate capacitor is 2 dm${}^2$, and their distance is 1 cm. The condenser is charged to a potential difference of 1000 V and then it is disconnected from the voltage source.
 $a)$ What is the surface density of the charge on the plates?
 $b)$ What is the magnitude of the force between the plates?
 $c)$ By what amount does the energy of the capacitor increase if the plates are displaced to a distance of 1.5 cm?
