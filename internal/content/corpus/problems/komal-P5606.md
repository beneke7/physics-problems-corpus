A spacecraft with a total mass $m_0$ is travelling in interplanetary space at a speed of $v$ without the presence of external forces. To change the direction of its motion, it suddenly turns on its propulsion system, from which propellant flows out at a constant (relative) speed $u$, all the time in a direction perpendicular to the instantaneous speed of the spacecraft. How much does the mass of the spacecraft decrease until its velocity vector turns $90^\circ$ relative to the original direction?
 (6 pont)
