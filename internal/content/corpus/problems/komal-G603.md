A finely wrought cogwheel is rotated, its teeth cease a thin laser beam travelling through them. The beam is reflected in a mirror at a distance of $d=1000$ m from the wheel such that it arrives at its starting point through the same gap between the teeth. The speed of the wheel is increasing uniformly and 1 minute after its start it reaches the value of 600 rotations per second. How long does it take after starting the wheel, until the reflected beam on the side of the light source disappears? There are 300 teeth on the wheel, and the size of a tooth is the same as the size of a gap. 

 (3 pont)
