8. A solenoid of length $2 \ell$ has an inner radius $R_{1}$ and an-outer radius $R_{2}$. The current through the solenoid is $I$.
(i) Show that the magnetic flux density, $B$ at the center of the solenoid is

$$
B=\kappa n I \ell \ln \frac{\alpha+\left(\alpha^{2}+\beta^{2}\right)^{1 / 2}}{1+\left(1+\beta^{2}\right)^{1 / 2}}
$$

where $n$ is the number of turns per square meter and $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are functions of $R_{1}, R_{2}$ and $L$. Write down the expression for $\alpha$ and $\beta$. State the value of $\kappa$.
(ii) Show that the length of the wire is

$$
\ell=n V=2 \pi n\left(\alpha^{m_{1}}-1\right) \beta^{m_{2}} R_{1}^{m_{3}}
$$

where $V$ is the volume of the winding and $m_{1}, m_{2}$ and $m_{3}$ are exponents that need to be determined. State the value of $m_{1}, m_{2}$ and $m_{3}$.
(iii) Show that the $B$ field at the center of the solenoid can be written as

$$
B=G\left(\frac{P \lambda \sigma}{R_{1}}\right)^{1 / 2}
$$

where $G$ depends on the geometry, $P$ is the dissipated power, $\lambda=n \pi r^{2}$ is the filling factor or fraction of the coil cross section occupied by the conductor, $r$ is the radius of the wire and $\sigma$ is the conductivity.
