Smugglers set off in a ship in a direction perpendicular to a straight shore and move at constant speed $v$. The coastguard's cutter is a distance $a$ from the smugglers' ship and leaves the shore at the same time. The cutter always moves at a constant speed in the direction of the smugglers' ship and catches up with the criminals when at a distance $a$ from the shore. How many times greater is the speed of the coastguard's cutter than that of the smugglers' ship?
