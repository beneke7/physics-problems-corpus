Three turtles are initially situated in the corners of an equilateral triangle at distances 1 m from one another. They move at constant velocity $10 \mathrm{~cm} / \mathrm{s}$ in such a way that the first always heading towards the second, the second towards the third and the third towards the first. After what time will they meet?
