In a long straight coaxial cable the centre conductor has a radius of  r $_{1}$, and the outer metallic shield has an inner radius of  r $_{2}$, and an outer radius of  r $_{3}$. (Between the two conductors there is a dielectric insulator.) In the two conductors the current of  I flows in the opposite direction. Determine the magnetic induction vector  B around the cable as a function of the distance  r measured from the symmetry axis of the cable. 
 (4 pont)
