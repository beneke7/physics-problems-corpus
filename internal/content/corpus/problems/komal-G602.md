An initially empty water tank of height 3 meters can be filled with the water of a well in two different ways. Once through tap $\rm Cs_2$ the water is pumped into the bottom of the tank, and the other is through tap $\rm Cs_1$ the water is made to flow into the tank from above.

 $a)$ Which one is cheaper?
 $b)$ Somebody calculated that if the losses at the pump and the friction of the flowing water in the tubes are neglected, then the tank can be filled with 20% less energy when the tank is filled in the less energy consuming way. At what depth is the water in the well? (The well is rich in water and the water level in it is constant.)
 (3 pont)
