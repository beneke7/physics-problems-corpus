An open-topped vertical tube of diameter $D$ is filled with water up to a height $h$. The narrow bottom-end of the tube, of diameter $d$, is closed by a stop as shown in the figure.
![](../../../../../sources/200ppp/figures/200ppp-029.jpg)

When the stop is removed, the water starts flowing out through the bottom orifice with approximate speed $v=\sqrt{2 g h}$. However, this speed is reached by the liquid only after a certain time $\tau$. Obtain an estimate of the order of magnitude of $\tau$. What is the acceleration of the lowest layer of water at the moment when the stop is removed? Ignore viscous effects.
