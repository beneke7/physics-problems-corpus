Juice bottle is being pasteurised at temperature $t_{1}=80^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ so that a light cap lies freely on the bottle rim (excess gas can exit freely from the bottle, but outside air cannot come in). Then the cap is tightly fixed so that air can no longer enter and exit from the bottle; the cap is so rigid that its deformation due to excess pressure can be neglected. The bottle is cooled down to the room temperature $t_{2}=20^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$. What is the pressure under the bottle cap? Assume that the physical properties of the juice are identical to that of water; the pressure of saturated water vapours is depicted as a function of temperature in a graph. Atmospheric pressure $P_{0}=1.01 \times 10^{5} \mathrm{~Pa}$.
