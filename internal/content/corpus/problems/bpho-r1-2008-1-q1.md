## Q1

(a) A 10 W immersion heater is placed in 0.25 kg of a liquid which is contained in a calorimeter of heat capacity $50 \mathrm{JK}^{-1}$. It is switched on, and after a time the temperature of the liquid reaches a constant value. The heater is now switched off and the rate of fall of temperature is $15 \mathrm{mKs}^{-1}$. What is the specific heat capacity of the liquid ?
(b) Comment on the following:
(i) The planets move with constant velocity in circles around the Sun.
(ii) The horizontal component of the energy of a tennis ball remains constant after being hit by a racket.
(iii) The speed of a light spot produced on a distant wall, by a rotating laser, can be greater than $c$, the speed of light.
(iv) The north magnetic pole is near the geographic north pole and the north pointing end of the needle of a compass always points to the north magnetic pole.
(v) The specific heat capacity of a gas at constant volume differs from that at constant pressure .
(vi) When gases expand rapidly they are cooled.
(c) A viola string 0.50 m long is tuned to A ; a frequency of 440.0 Hz ..
(i) What change in length will raise its frequency to 550.0 Hz ?
(ii) If it goes out of tune and vibrates at 435.6 Hz , by what fraction, and in what sense, must the tension in the string be changed to retune it ?
(d) A man cycles to work, with the wind behind him , at $8.0 \mathrm{~ms}^{-1}$ and returns home, Against the wind, at $4.0 \mathrm{~ms}^{-1}$. What is his average speed ?
(e) A machine gun fires bullets of mass 10 g at a speed of $12 \mathrm{kms}^{-1}$. If the gunner can exert an average force of up to 80 N against the gun, calculate the maximum number of bullets that he can fire per minute.

Sketch a force - time graph of the recoil force of the gun on the shoulder of the gunner, and show, graphically, what is understood by the average force.
(f) An oil drop is observed between the plates of a capacitor.
(i) In an electric field of $0.30 \mathrm{MVm}^{-1}$ a stationary drop, with one excess electron, is observed. What is the weight of the drop ?
(ii) This drop is later observed to fall with a terminal speed of $0.20 \mathrm{mms}^{-1}$, which is independent of the pd between the plates. Explain this observation.
(g) Explain qualitatively how Newton's interference rings are formed using an optical plate and a convex lens.
(h) A body , mass $m$, rests on a scale pan which is supported by a spring. The period of oscillation of the scale pan is 0.50 s . It is observed that when the amplitude of the oscillations is increased, and exceeds a certain value, the mass leaves the pan. Explain.
At what point in the motion does the mass initially leave the pan ?
(i) A uniform capillary tube, closed at one end, contains air trapped by a thread of mercury 85 mm long. When the tube is held horizontally, the length of the air column is 50 mm . When it is held vertically, with the closed end downwards, the length is 45 mm . Determine the atmospheric pressure.

(k) Answer the following questions with an example where possible:
(i) Can a body to be accelerating while travelling at constant speed ?
(ii) Can a body have a constant velocity and a varying speed ?
(iii) If a body has zero velocity, can it be accelerating ?
(iv) Can a body be accelerating in a direction opposite to its velocity?
(l) The equation

$$
y=A \sin (\omega t-k x)
$$

represents a plane wave travelling along the x -direction in a medium, $y$ is the displacement of a point at position $x$ at time $t . A, k$ and $\omega$ are constants. Deduce the direction and speed of the wave.

If $A=1.0 \times 10^{-7} \mathrm{~m}, \boldsymbol{\omega}=6.6 \times 10^{3} \mathrm{~s}^{-1}$ and $k=20 \mathrm{~m}^{-1}$, calculate:
(i) the speed of the wave
(ii) the maximum speed of a particle of the medium due to the wave.
(m) 20 identical electric lamps, each of 12 V , are connected in series to a 240 V mains supply. The total power consumed is 24 W .
(i) What is the resistance of each lamp ?
(ii) If one lamp is short circuited, what is the power consumed ?
(iii) When one of the lamps is tested by applying a pd of 0.10 V , a 10 mA current is measured. Explain.
(n) The maximum kinetic energy of photoelectrons ejected from a tungsten surface, by light of wavelength 248 nm , is $8.6 \times 10^{-20} \mathrm{~J}$. What is the work function, in eV , of tungsten?
(o) An electron and a positron annihilate each other to produce two $\gamma$-rays. Calculate the minimum energy of the photons?
(p) Explain qualitatively using sound waves as an example, what is understood by:
(i) the Doppler effect
(ii) the phenomenon of beats
(iii) stationary waves
(q) What is a magnetic field line ?

Draw the magnetic field lines in a plane perpendicular to: (i) a current $I$ in an infinite straight wire and (ii) the axis of a bar magnet at one end.
