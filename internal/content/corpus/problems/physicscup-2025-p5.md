By Jaan Kalda (TalTech).

A satellite orbits a planet. At point $A$, its speed is $v_1$. At point $B$, its speed is $v_2$ and its velocity vector forms a right angle with the velocity vector at point $A$. At point $C$, the velocity is exactly opposite to the velocity at point $A$, with a magnitude of $v_3$. Find the eccentricity of the orbit. Also determine the exact numerical value of the eccentricity when $v_1 = 1$km/s, $v_2 = 2$km/s, and $v_3 = 3$km/s.
