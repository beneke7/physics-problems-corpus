6. Jason is going to make a blouse for his mother for Mother's day. The pattern requires $2.5 \mathrm{~m}^{2}$ of fabric so Jason asks for 1.7 m of fabric that is 1.5 m wide. When he gets home the measures this piece to be 1.50 m wide but only 1.66 m long. His measuring tape has an uncertainty of 1 cm. What are of fabric does Jason have?
    A. $\quad 2.49 \mathrm{~m}^{2} \pm 0.01 \mathrm{~m}^{2}$
    B. $2.49 \mathrm{~m}^{2} \pm 0.02 \mathrm{~m}^{2}$
    C. $2.49 \mathrm{~m}^{2} \pm 0.03 \mathrm{~m}^{2}$
    D. $2.55 \mathrm{~m}^{2} \pm 0.01 \mathrm{~m}^{2}$
    E. $\quad 2.55 \mathrm{~m}^{2} \pm 0.02 \mathrm{~m}^{2}$
