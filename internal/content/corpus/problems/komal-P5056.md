A spring with negligibly small mass and of spring constant 40 N/m is standing on the tabletop in a vertical position, and on its top there is a sheet, which also has negligible mass. A 0.2 kg small object is dropped to the sheet from a height of 0.4 m, measured from the level of the sheet. For how long will the small object be on the sheet if it does not stick to it?
 (5 pont)
