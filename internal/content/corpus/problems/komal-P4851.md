In aerodynamics the Mach number is the ratio of the speed of the object moving in air and the local speed of sound. 
 During a fighter aircraft training one of the fighters ascends from a region of $0~{}^\circ$C air at a height of some kilometres, while its speed increases from 1.5 Mach to 1.75 Mach. Due to the jet powered engine the fighter's kinetic energy increased by 20%, and its mass decreased by 1%. 
 $a)$ What is the temperature of the air (in ${}^\circ$C) at the height to which the fighter moved? 
 $b)$ How much did the fighter ascend, and what is its final speed if the temperature of the air decreases $0.65~{}^\circ$ in each 100 m increase in height? 
 (5 pont)
