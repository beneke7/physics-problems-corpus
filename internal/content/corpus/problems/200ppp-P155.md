A battery consists of $N$ identical cells, each of e.m.f. $\mathscr{E}$. Is it true that the energy wasted when using the battery to charge a capacitor through a resistor can be reduced by charging it in $N$ stages? That is by connecting it first across a single cell, and then across two cells, and so on, rather than across the whole battery in a single step.
