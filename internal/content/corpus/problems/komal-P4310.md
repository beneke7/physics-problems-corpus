An air gun is modelled such that initially there is some compressed air in a container of volume  V $_{1}$, at a pressure of  p $_{1}$. In the barrel there is a small projectile of mass  m right in front of the container's opening. a ) How long should the barrel be in order to accelerate the projectile to the greatest speed? b ) What can the maximum speed of the projectile be? (Neglect friction and the leaking of the high-pressure air.) Data: m =0.51 g, the inner diameter of the barrel is 4.5 mm, p $_{1}$=2 MPa, V $_{1}$=1 cm$^{3}$.
 (5 pont)
