Investigate how the time of falling of different rectangular shaped sheets of paper depends on the ratio of the sides of the rectangle, if the height from which they are dropped is kept constant. Also keep the area of the rectangles constant and make sure that the plane of each sheet is horizontal when it is dropped.
 (6 pont)
