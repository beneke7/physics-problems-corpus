During a lecture given by Baintner, Géza (1892–1980) there was an experiment shown, that when three rubber strings are tied in a Y shape, and the two strings at the two symmetrical ends of the Y are vibrated totally out of phase, then the two waves cancel each other, and the third piece of the Y stayed at rest. Question: What happens to the energy of the two waves? 
 (4 pont)
