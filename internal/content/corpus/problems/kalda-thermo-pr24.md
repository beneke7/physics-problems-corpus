Consider two liquids $A$ and $B$ insoluble in each other. The pressures $p_{i}(i=A, B)$ of their saturated vapours obey, to a good approximation, the formula $\ln \left(p_{i} / p_{0}\right)=a_{i} / T+b_{i}$, where $p_{0}$ denotes the normal atmospheric pressure, $T$ - the absolute temperature of the vapour, and $a_{i}$ and $b_{i}(i=A, B)$ - certain constants depending on the liquid.
