A boy with mass $m$ wants to push another boy standing on the ice, whose mass $M$ is bigger that his own. To that end, he speeds up, runs toward the other boy and pushed him for as long as they can stand up. What is the maximal distance by which it is possible to push in this fashion? The maximal velocity of a run is $v$, the coefficient of friction between both boys and the ice is $\mu$.
