Both solder joints of a thermocouple exchange heat with the surrounding air at the rate $P_{e}=\kappa \Delta T$, where $\Delta T$ is the difference between the temperature of a solder joint and that of the air; the constant $\kappa=30 \mu \mathrm{~W} / \mathrm{K}$. The thermocouple sensitivity $\alpha$ is defined as the proportionality coefficient between the thermocouple voltage and the temperature difference between the solder joints; in this case, $\alpha=50 \mu \mathrm{~V} / \mathrm{K}$. The thermocouple is fed with electric current $I=10 \mathrm{~mA}$; find the temperature of the colder solder joint if the air temperature $t_{a}=20^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$. Assume the energy transfer processes in the thermocouple to be reversible.
