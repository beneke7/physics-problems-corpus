The length of an AA rechargeable battery is 5 cm, and its diameter is 1.4 cm.
 $a)$ How much energy is stored in the battery rated at 1.2 V and 2800 mAh?
 $b)$ To what speed could such a battery speed up, if all of its stored energy was converted to its kinetic energy? The mass of the battery is 17 g.
 $c)$ By what factor would the energy needed to heat the same volume of water from $20\;{}^\circ$C to $100\;{}^\circ$C be less than the above calculated energy?
 $d)$ How much energy is there in the same volume of granulated sugar of density of approximately 0.77 g/cm${}^3$? The energy content of sugar is 1680 kJ/100 grams.
 (4 pont)
