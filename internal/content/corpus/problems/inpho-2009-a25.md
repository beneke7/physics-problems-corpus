25. A long straight wire is carrying current $I_{1}$ in $+z$ direction. The $x-y$ plane contains a closed circular loop carrying current $I_{2}$ and not encircling the straight wire. The force on the loop will be
(a) $\mu_{0} I_{1} I_{2} / 2 \pi$.
(b) $\mu_{0} I_{1} I_{2} / 4 \pi$.
(c) zero.
(d) depends on the distance of the centre of the loop from the wire.
