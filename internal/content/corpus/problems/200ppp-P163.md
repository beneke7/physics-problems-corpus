You are given a large complex electrical circuit containing a lot of resistors and other passive elements and wish to determine the resistance of
a particular resistor in the circuit without unsoldering it (i.e. without taking it out of the circuit). A battery, an ammeter and a voltmeter, all of high quality, are provided. How would you carry out the measurement?
