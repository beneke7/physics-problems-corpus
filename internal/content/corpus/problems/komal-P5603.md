We are observing a small starfish lying on a sandy beach, standing on all fours, from exactly above, at a height of $50~\mathrm{cm}$. The tide slowly causes the water level to rise, and $40~\mathrm{cm}$ of water covers the animal.
 a) How many times larger, i.e., at how many times greater angle do we see the starfish than at the beginning if we do not change our body position?
 b) How many times larger do we see the starfish if at high tide we wear glasses which have a power of $0.5$ dioptres? Where is the image formed by the lens in this case?
 Ignore the distance between the glasses and your eyes.
 (4 pont)
