On a planet of a distant binary star intelligent creatures are living. Their astronomers have found that the distance between the two stars is constant in time, so they have chosen this distance as the ``astronomical unit'' (AU). Their space scientists have launched a sensitive space telescope to the Lagrangian point of the binary system $\text{L}_2$, which is located at a distance of $\tfrac{1}{2}~\text{AU}$ from the lower-mass star, on the opposite side from the other star. What is the ratio of the masses of the two stars?
