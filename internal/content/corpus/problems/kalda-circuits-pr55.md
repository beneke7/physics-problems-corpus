$m$ identical resistors of resitance $R$ are connected in an arbitrary way; though, none of the resistors is shortcircuited (there is no direct wire connection between the two leads of a resistor), and all the resistors are connected together (the resistance between any pair of nodes is finite). Overall, this resistor network has $n$ nodes For each resistor, the resistance between the adjacent nodes (to which it is connected) is determined, and the results are added up. Show that this sum of $m$ resistances equals always to $(n-1) R .^{23}$
