7. A ship in a calm port nearing a shore radio station received a 200 MHz signal from the station's antenna. Both station's antenna and the ship's receiving antenna are located 20 m above the sea surface. A succession of maxima and minima are heard in the signal received at the ship. How far is the ship from the station the first time the signal passes through a minimum? How fast is the ship moving if the time between the first minimum and the next one is 50 s? [10 marks]
