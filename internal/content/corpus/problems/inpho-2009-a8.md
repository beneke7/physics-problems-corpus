8. Consider a circle of radius $R$. A point charge lies at a distance $a$ from its center and on its axis such that $R=a \sqrt{3}$. If electric flux passing through the circle is $\phi$ then the magnitude of the point charge is
(a) $\sqrt{3} \epsilon_{0} \phi$
(b) $2 \epsilon_{0} \phi$
(c) $4 \epsilon_{0} \phi / \sqrt{3}$
(d) $4 \epsilon_{0} \phi$
