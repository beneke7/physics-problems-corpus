## Question 4

If Lachlan, in the previous question, doubles the constant horizontal force that he exerts on the chair to push it on the same horizontal floor, the chair then moves:
a. with a constant speed that is double the speed $v_{0}$ in the previous question.
b. with a constant speed that is greater than the speed $v_{0}$ in the previous question, but not necessarily twice as great.
c. for a while with a speed that is constant and greater than the speed $v_{0}$ in the previous question, then with a speed that increases thereafter.
d. for a while with an increasing speed, then with a constant speed thereafter.
e. with a continuously increasing speed.
