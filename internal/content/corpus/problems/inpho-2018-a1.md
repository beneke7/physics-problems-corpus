1. In a nucleus, the attractive central potential which binds the proton and the neutron is called the Yukawa potential. The associated potential energy $U(r)$ is

$$
U(r)=-\alpha \frac{e^{-r / \lambda}}{r}
$$

Here $\lambda=1.431 \mathrm{fm}\left(\mathrm{fm}=10^{-15} \mathrm{~m}\right), r$ is the distance between nucleons, and $\alpha=86.55 \mathrm{MeV} \cdot \mathrm{fm}$ is the nuclear force constant $\left(1 \mathrm{MeV}=1.60 \times 10^{-13} \mathrm{~J}\right)$.
