A spherical balloon is filled with some gas, whose density is smaller than that of the air. In order to make the balloon float at a certain height in the room, paper clips are hung on the free end of the rope, which is tied to the balloon. The floating was not achieved because the balloon rose if 7 paper clips were hung on the end of the rope, but descended if 8 were hung on the end of the rope. What is the density of the gas in the balloon? The density of the air in the room is $1.20~\tfrac{\mathrm{kg}}{\mathrm{m}^3}$. The balloon has a diameter of $26~\mathrm{cm}$ and a mass of $3~\mathrm{g}$ (before it was inflated). The mass of the rope is $2~\mathrm{g}$, the mass of one paper clip is $0.6~\mathrm{g}$. The thickness of the material of the balloon, the volume of the rope and the paper clips can be ignored. 
 (4 pont)
