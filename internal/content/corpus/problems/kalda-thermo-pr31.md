Due to instability (know as the Plateau-Rayleigh instability), a tap water stream breaks into droplets at a certain height. This process can be modelled by the instability of a long water cylinder in weightlessness. Let the diameter of the cylinder be $d=1 \mathrm{~mm}$; estimate the time period $T$ during which the amplitude of the most unstable perturbations will increase by a factor of $e \approx 2.718$. The surface-tension of water $\gamma=72 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{s}^{2}$, and the density $\rho=1 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{cm}^{3}$.
