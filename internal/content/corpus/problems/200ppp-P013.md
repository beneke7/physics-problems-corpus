A small hamster is put into a circular wheel-cage, which has a frictionless central pivot. A horizontal platform is fixed to the wheel below the pivot. Initially, the hamster is at rest at one end of the platform.
![](../../../../../sources/200ppp/figures/200ppp-002.jpg)

When the platform is released the hamster starts running, but, because of the hamster's motion, the platform and wheel remain stationary. Determine how the hamster moves.
