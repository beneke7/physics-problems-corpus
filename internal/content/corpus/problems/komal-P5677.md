To the terminals of a battery of $1.5~\mathrm{V}$, a resistor of $1~\mathrm{k}\Omega$ is connected with two copper wires of length $20~\mathrm{cm}$, and of radius $1~\mathrm{mm}$. The copper has one conduction electron per atom.
 a) Calculate the drift speed of the conduction electrons in the wire.
 b) How long would it take for an electron to travel at this speed from the battery to the resistor?
 (5 pont)
