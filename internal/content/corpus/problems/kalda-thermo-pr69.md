During a day time, air masses in atmosphere are usually in a turbulent (random) motion, up and down, which leads to what is referred to as adiabatic atmosphere: when a parcel of air (a fictitious volume) is raising up, it is expanding and cooling adiabatically; in adiabatic atmosphere, the surrounding air is in mechanical and thermodynamical equilibrium with the raising air parcel (has exactly the same pressure and temperature). Find, how depends air temperature $T$ on height $h$ in adiabatic atmosphere. At the ground level ( $h=0$ ), $T=T_{0}$; express your answer in terms of the adiabatic index of air $\gamma$, molar mass $\mu$, universal gas constant $R$, and free fall acceleration $g$.
