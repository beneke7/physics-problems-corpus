A diffraction grating, with period $d$, consists of slits, of equal width, Figure 5.1.
Monochromatic light, wavelength $\lambda$, incident normally on the diffraction grating, is diffracted by an angle $\theta$.


    (a) Determine the condition for:
        (i) constructive interference
        (ii) destructive interference.

(b) When monochromatic red and monochromatic violet light are incident on the diffraction grating, it is found that the fourth line observed, not counting the undiffracted zero order line, is a superposition of red and violet light. Explain this observation and specify the first four lines.
[4]

(c) If the grating has 500 lines per mm, and the diffraction angle for the composite line is 43.6°, determine the wavelengths of the red and violet lines.

[6]

(d) What is the fifth line in the spectrum and its associated diffraction angle?
[4]
(e) Indicate, graphically, how the intensity of the lines varies with order $n$.
[3]
