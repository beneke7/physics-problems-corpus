## 5. Catching the wave

The speed of of a travelling wave $v$ along a string is a function of its tension $T$ and its linear mass density $\rho$ in the form $v=T^{\alpha} \rho^{\beta}$.
(a) Find $\alpha$ and $\beta$.
(b) A uniform, almost inextensible string of length $l$ and total mass $M$ is suspended vertically and tapped at the top end so that a transverse impulse runs down it. At the same moment, a body is released from rest and falls freely from the top of the string. How far from the bottom does the body pass the impulse?
