A small bob joins two light unstretched, identical springs, anchored at their far ends and arranged along a straight line, as shown in the figure.
![](../../../../../sources/200ppp/figures/200ppp-031.jpg)

The bob is displaced in a direction perpendicular to the line of the springs by 1 cm and then released. The period of the ensuing vibration of the bob is Is. Find the period of the vibration if the bob were displaced by 2 cm before dease. The unstretched length of the springs is $\ell_{0} \gg 1 \mathrm{~cm}$, and gravity is be ignored.
