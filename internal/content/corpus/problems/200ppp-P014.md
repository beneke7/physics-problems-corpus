A bicycle is supported so that it is prevented from falling sideways but can move forwards or backwards; its pedals are in their highest and lowest positions. A student crouches beside the bicycle and applies a horizontal force, directed towards the back wheel, to the lower pedal.
(i) Which way does the bicycle move?
(ii) Does the chain-wheel rotate in the same or opposite sense as the rear wheel?
(iii) Which way does the lower pedal move relative to the ground?
