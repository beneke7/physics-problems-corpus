2. A particle of mass 100 g is dropped from a great height and falls vertically downwards. The force due to air resistance is $-k v$ where $v$ is the speed of the particle and $k$ is a constant having the value $1.09 \times 10^{-2} \mathrm{~kg} \mathrm{~s}^{-1}$.
    (a) What is the terminal velocity of the particle during the fall?

[1 mark]


(b) How much time does it take for the particle to achieve 99\% of the terminal velocity?

[4 marks]

(c) How far has the particle fallen when its velocity achieves 99\% of the terminal velocity?
