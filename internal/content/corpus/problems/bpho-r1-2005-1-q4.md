Q4.

![](bpho-r1-2005-1-q4-fig01.png)
Figure 4.1

Two identical balls, A and B, each of mass $m$ are joined by a massless spring, spring constant $k$, Figure 4.1. They are at rest on a smooth horizontal surface. A ball C , mass $M$ and velocity $V_{o}$, strikes B. All balls are constrained to move along a straight line.
(a)
(i) Write down the energy and momentum equations for the collision in which, immediately after impact, the velocities of the balls are $V_{A}, V_{B}$ and $V_{C}$ in the direction of $V_{o}$.
(ii) Verify, by substituting into the equations, that there are two solutions:

$$
V_{C}=V_{o}, \quad V_{B}=0, \quad V_{A}=0
$$

and

$$
V_{C}=\frac{M-m}{M+m} V_{0}, \quad V_{B}=\frac{2 M}{M+m} V_{0}, \quad V_{A}=0
$$

(iii) Why must the first solution be rejected? Explain why $V_{A}=0$
(iv) Deduce the velocity, after the collision, of the centre of mass of the system consisting of A and B.
(b) Consider the subsequent motion of A and B in their centre of mass system. Assume that their displacements, in their centre of mass system, about their initial positions following the impact are given by:

$$
x=A \sin \omega t,
$$

where $x$ is measured in the direction of the centre of mass, $A$ is the amplitude, $\omega$ is the angular frequency and $t$ is the time measured from the instant of collision.
Determine:
(i) $\omega$
(ii) $A$
(iii) Write down the position of $\mathrm{B}, x_{B}$, in the laboratory system of coordinates, measured from the instant of collision.
