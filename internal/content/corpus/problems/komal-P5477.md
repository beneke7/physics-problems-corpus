An ideal battery of emf $U=24$ V, resistors of resistance values $R_1=500~\Omega$ and $R_2=300~\Omega$, a switch and an ideal transformer were used to construct the circuit shown in the figure. The primary coil of the transformer has a number of turns of $N_1=800$ and the secondary coil has $N_2=1000$ turns. The switch, which had been open for a long time, was once closed. What were the values of the current in the primary and secondary coils immediately after the switch was closed?
