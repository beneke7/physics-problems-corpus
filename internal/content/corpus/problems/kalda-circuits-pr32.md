For an overcurrent protection, there are two fuses connected in parallel: fuse $A$ has resistance $R_{A}=1 \Omega$ and maximal current (by which it melts) $I_{A \max }=1 \mathrm{~A}$; fuse $B$ has resistance $R_{B}=2 \Omega$ and maximal current (by which it melts) $I_{B \max }=1.2 \mathrm{~A}$. What is the maximal total current for such a system of fuses? What is the total current when the fuse $B$ is substituted with a fuse $C$ which has $R_{C}=2 \Omega$ and $I_{C \text { max }}=1.7 \mathrm{~A}$ ?
