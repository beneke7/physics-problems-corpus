One end of a horizontal track of gauge $\ell$ and negligible resistance, is connected to a capacitor of capacitance $C$ charged to voltage $V_{0}$. The inductance of the assembly is negligible. The system is placed in a homogeneous, vertical magnetic field of induction $B$, as shown in the figure.
![](../../sources/200ppp/figures/200ppp-074.jpg)

A frictionless conducting rod of mass $m$ and resistance $R$ is placed perpendicularly onto the track. The polarity of the capacitor is such that the rod is repelled from the capacitor when the switch is turned over.
(i) What is the maximum velocity of the rod?
(ii) Under what conditions is the efficiency of this 'electromagnetic gun' maximal?
