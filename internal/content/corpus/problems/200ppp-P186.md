In a vacuum chamber a current of 10 A is flowing in a long, straight wire, which has a very high conductivity. Electrons with an initial velocity $v_{0}$ start moving perpendicularly towards the wire from a point which is a radial distance $r_{0}$ away from the wire. Given that they cannot approach any closer to the wire than $r_{0} / 2$, determine $v_{0}$. Ignore the effect of the Earth's magnetic field.
