The circuit – containing three identical resistors and two identical coils – shown in the figure has been connected to a DC source for a relatively long time. (The resistance of the coils is negligible.)
 $a)$ What will be the current flowing through the resistors immediately after switch K is turned off?
 $b)$ What is the value of the induced electromotive force in each coil, right after switch K is turned off?
 $c)$ How do the sum and difference of the currents in the coils vary in time?
 $d)$ How long does it take for the current in one coil and the current in the other coil to drop to half of the current measured very soon after switch K was turned off?
 Data: $U_0=1~\mathrm{V}$, $L=1~\mathrm{H}$, $R=1\,\Omega$.

 (6 pont)
