Point-masses of mass $m$ are at rest at the corners of a regular $n$-gon, as illustrated in the figure for $n=6$.
![](../../../../../sources/200ppp/figures/200ppp-037.jpg)

How does the system move if only gravitation acts between the bodies? How much time elapses before the bodies collide if $n=2,3$ and 10 ? Examine the limiting case when $n \gg 1$ and $m=M_{0} / n$, where $M_{0}$ is a given total mass.
