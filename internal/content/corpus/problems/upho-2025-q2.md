The oscillating circuit shown in the figure consists of two capacitors $\mathrm{C}=1000\ \mu\mathrm{F}$, an inductor $\mathrm{L}=0.1\ \mathrm{H}$, and a key. Initially, the key was open, and the charge on each capacitor was equal to $q$.

![First circuit: two capacitors C in parallel with inductor L and a key](../../sources/upho/figures/upho-2025-q2-fig1.png)

2.1 Find the maximum current through the coil $I_{\max}$ after the key is closed.

Let's change the circuit. Connect a resistor to each element of the oscillating circuit with a resistance of $\mathrm{R}=10\ \mathrm{Ohm}$ (see figure). At a certain point in time, the following conditions are met: $\frac{dI_L}{dt}=0$, $I_1 = 10\,A$, $I_2 = 12\,A$.

![Second circuit: three branches C–R ($I_1$), L–R, C–R ($I_2$)](../../sources/upho/figures/upho-2025-q2-fig2.png)

2.2 Find the maximum amount of heat $Q$ that can be released in a system from this point in time.

Let's further modify the circuit. Replace the resistors with a resistance of $\mathrm{R}=10\ \mathrm{Ohm}$ with resistors of different resistance and connect the circuit to an alternating voltage source with known amplitude value $U_0$ and cyclic frequency $\omega$ (see the third figure). It is known that the parameters of the system are related to each other by the relation $\frac{1}{C_1\omega R_1}=\frac{\omega L}{R_3}=\sqrt{3}$.

![Third circuit: branches C1–R1 (node B), C2–R2 (node D), L–R3 (node C), source U(t) at nodes A](../../sources/upho/figures/upho-2025-q2-fig3.png)
