A jet of water strikes a horizontal gutter of semicircular crosssection obliquely, as shown in the figure. The jet lies in the vertical plane that contains the centre-line of the gutter.
![](../../sources/200ppp/figures/200ppp-028.jpg)

Calculate the ratio of the quantities of water flowing out at the two ends of the gutter as a function of the angle of incidence $\alpha$ of the jet.
