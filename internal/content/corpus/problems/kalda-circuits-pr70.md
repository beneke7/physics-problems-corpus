An electrical transformer is connected as shown in the circuit below. Both windings of the transformer have the same number of loops and the self-inductance of the both coils is equal to $L$; there is no leakage of the magnetic field lines from the core so that the mutual inductance is also equal to $L$.
(a) Find the current in the both loops immediately after the switch is closed.
(b) Find the currents as a function of time.
