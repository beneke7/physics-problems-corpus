There is a parabolic-shaped bridge across a river of width 100 m . The highest point of the bridge is 5 m above the level of the banks. A car of mass 1000 kg is crossing the bridge at a constant speed of $20 \mathrm{~m} \mathrm{~s}^{-1}$.
![](../../sources/200ppp/figures/200ppp-035.jpg)

Using the notation indicated in the figure, find the force exerted on the bridge by the car when it is:
(i) at the highest point of the bridge,
(ii) three-quarters of the way across.
(Ignore air resistance and take $g$ as $10 \mathrm{~m} \mathrm{~s}^{-2}$.)
