A closed body, whose surface $F$ is made of metal foil, has an electrical capacitance $C$ with respect to an 'infinitely distant' point. The foil is now dented in such a way that the new surface $F^{*}$ is entirely inside or on the original surface, as shown in the figure.
![](../../../../../sources/200ppp/figures/200ppp-066.jpg)

Prove that the capacitance of the deformed body is less than $C$.
