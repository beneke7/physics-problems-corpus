A thermocouple is being used as a battery: one of its solder joints is at the room temperature $T_{1}=20^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$, the other is kept inside a glass of water with ice, at temperature $T_{2}=0^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$. The output leads are connected to a resistor $R=10 \Omega$, connected in series with an ammeter which shows that current $I=10 \mathrm{~mA}$. How much ice is melted during $t=10 \mathrm{~h}$ if the glass with ice has very good thermal insulation and room temperature remains constant? Latent heat of melting for ice $\lambda=330 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{kg}$. Assume the energy transfer processes in the thermocouple to be reversible.
