On a given day, the air is dry and has a density $\rho=1.250 \mathrm{~kg} / \mathrm{m}^{3}$. The next day the humidity has increased and the air is $2 \%$ by mass water vapour. The pressure and temperature are the same as the day before. What is the air density $\rho^{\prime}$ now? Mean molar mass of dry air: $M_{a}=28.8 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{mol}$; molar mass of water: $M_{w}=18 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{mol}$. Assume ideal-gas behaviour.
