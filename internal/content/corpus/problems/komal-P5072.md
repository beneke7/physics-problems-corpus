The diameter of a neon atom is 0.32 nm. Estimate that when the temperature of the neon gas is $0~{}^\circ$C and its pressure is 1 atm 
 $a)$ by what factor of the average distance between the atoms of the gas is greater than the diameter of a single neon atom? 
 $b)$ by what factor of the average thermal speed of the atoms is greater than the speed of sound in the gas?
