a) The charges $2 q$ and $-2 q$ are separated by a distance $d$ and located at points A and B , Figure 6.1. $d=\mathrm{AB}=\mathrm{AD}=\mathrm{DB}=\mathrm{AO}=\mathrm{AC}=\mathrm{CB}$, where O is the mid-point of GE .

Determine:
(i) the vector forces $\boldsymbol{F}_{\mathbf{c}}$ at C and $\boldsymbol{F}_{\mathbf{0}}$ at O on a unit positive charge
(ii) the potential $V_{\mathrm{c}}$ at C and $V_{\mathrm{o}}$ at O
(iii) the work done in taking the charge $2 q$ along the path ACGOED to D.
b) Figure 6.2 shows the plates, length $L$, of a CRO with a constant electric field $\boldsymbol{E}$ and a constant magnetic field of flux density $\boldsymbol{B}$ parallel to the plates and perpendicular to $\boldsymbol{E}$. A charge $q$, mass $m$, travelling horizontally with velocity $v$ enters the region between the plates.
(i) If $\mathbf{B}=0$, show that on emerging it will be deflected vertically by a distance $y_{1}$ given by

$$
y_{1}=\frac{q E L^{2}}{2 m v^{2}} .
$$

(ii) If $\boldsymbol{E}=0$ and $\boldsymbol{B}$ is finite, show that the charge will emerge with a vertical deflection, $y_{2}$, in the opposite direction to $y_{1}$, given by

$$
y_{2}=R-\sqrt{R^{2}-L^{2}}, \quad \text { where } R=\frac{m v}{B q} .
$$

(iii) Show that if, during the motion through the plates, the fields are adjusted so that the forces on $q$ balance,

$$
\frac{q}{m}=\frac{2 y_{1} E}{B^{2} L^{2}} .
$$
