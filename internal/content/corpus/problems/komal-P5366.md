The ratio of the specific heat capacity of an ideal gas at constant pressure to that of at constant volume is $\kappa$. 
 $a)$ The gas expands adiabatically. What is the ratio of the work done by the gas to the change in its internal energy? 
 $b)$ The gas is compressed isothermally. What is the ratio of the work done by the gas to the absorbed heat by the gas? 
 $c)$ The gas is heated and it undergoes isobaric expansion. What is the ratio of the work done by the gas to the heat absorbed by the gas? 
 (4 pont)
