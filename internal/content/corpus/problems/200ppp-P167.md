A homogeneous field of magnetic induction $\mathbf{B}$ is perpendicular to a track of gauge $\ell$ which is inclined at an angle $\alpha$ to the horizontal. A frictionless conducting rod of mass $m$ straddles the two rails of the track as shown in the figure.
![](../../../../../sources/200ppp/figures/200ppp-073.jpg)

How does the rod move, after being released from rest, if the circuit formed by the rod and the track is closed by:
(i) a resistor of resistance $R$,
(ii) a capacitor of capacitance $C$, or
(iii) a coil of inductance $L$ ?
