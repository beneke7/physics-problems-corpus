2. Consider the following situations:
    I. You are standing in a lift and accelerating upwards
    II. You are standing in a lift and moving upwards at constant speed
    III. You are standing in a lift which has experienced a cable failure, and is freely falling downwards

In which of these situations is your weight substantially different to when you are standing at rest on the surface of the Earth? Select one:


A. None of the above
B. I only
C. I and III
D. III only
E. All of the above
