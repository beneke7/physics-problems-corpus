Drill (or in any other way make) a small hole to the bottom of a 1.5 litre plastic bottle, and fill the bottle with water to a height of  H . Measure how the time T during which the water flows out of the water depends on the diameter of the hole and on the height of the water  H . According to theoretical reasons  T is proportional to some power of  H . According to the measured data what is this exponent? 
 (6 pont)
