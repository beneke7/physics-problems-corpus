What can the maximum angle between a ray of light and the axis of the optical fibre be in order to produce total reflection inside the fibre? A light ray enters from air into the glass fibre at the centre of the cross sectional plane of the fibre, which is perpendicular to the symmetry axis of the fibre. What is the maximum angle of incidence of the light ray?
