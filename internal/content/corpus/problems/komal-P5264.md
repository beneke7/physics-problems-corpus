A racing car starts from rest and goes along a circular race track of radius 60 m. Its tangential acceleration is constant in the first four seconds of its motion, its magnitude is $6~\mathrm{m/s}^2$.
 $a)$ Determine the angular speed at which the acceleration vector rotates with respect to the direction of the motion of the car. Sketch this angular speed as a function of the time.
 $b)$ How much time elapses until this angular speed becomes the greatest? What is this greatest angular speed?
 (5 pont)
