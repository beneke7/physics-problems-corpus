``Intensive meat production'' is often cited as one of the causes of climate change. A single cow emits 160-320 litres of methane per day. There are one billion cattle in global livestock production. Estimate the thickness of the methane layer that would be formed on the surface of the Earth. (Consider the Earth as a sphere of radius 6370 km.)
