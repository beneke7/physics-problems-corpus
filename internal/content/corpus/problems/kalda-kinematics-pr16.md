An ant is moving along a rubber band at velocity $v=1 \mathrm{~cm} / \mathrm{s}$. One end of the rubber band (the one from which the ant started) is fixed to a wall, the other (initially at distance $L=1 \mathrm{~m}$ from the wall) is pulled at $u=1 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$. Will the ant reach the other end of the band? If yes then when will it happen?
