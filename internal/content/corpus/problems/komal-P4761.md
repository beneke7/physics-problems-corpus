The acceleration of a strange car is inversely proportional to its speed in the speed interval of $(v_1,v_2)$, so $a=A/v$ ($A$  is a positive constant).
 $a)$ How long does it take for the car to speed up from the speed $v_1$ to the speed $v_2$?
 $b)$ What is the power of the car during this motion?
