## Problem 2.

Brass weights are used to weigh an aluminum-made sample on an analytical balance. The weighing is ones in dry air and another time in humid air with the water vapor pressure $P_{h}=2 \cdot 10^{3} \mathrm{~Pa}$. The total atmospheric pressure ( $P=10^{5} \mathrm{~Pa}$ ) and the temperature ( $t=20^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ ) are the same in both cases.

What should the mass of the sample be to be able to tell the difference in the balance readings provided their sensitivity is $m_{0}=0.1 \mathrm{mg}$ ?
Aluminum density $\rho_{1}=2700 \mathrm{~kg} / \mathrm{m}^{3}$, brass density $\rho_{2}=.8500 \mathrm{~kg} / \mathrm{m}^{3}$.
