A wedge with mass $M$ and acute angles $\alpha_{1}$ and $\alpha_{2}$ lies on a horizontal surface. A string has been drawn across a pulley situated at the top of the wedge, its ends are tied to blocks with masses $m_{1}$ and $m_{2}$. What will be the acceleration of the wedge? There is no friction anywhere.
