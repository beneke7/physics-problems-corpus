1. A box has mass $m$, and is of uniform density. It has height $h$ when it stands upright on a table. The coefficient of friction between the surfaces is $\mu$, and the acceleration of free fall is $g$.
(a) How would you gently push the box to get it moving without toppling?
    (b) Determine, in terms of the symbols introduced, the position $y$ above the table surface at which to apply a force on the box, such that it remains upright and has an acceleration $a$.
