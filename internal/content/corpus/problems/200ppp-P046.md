An executive toy consists of three suspended steel balls of masses $M, \mu$ and $m$ arranged in that order with their centres in a horizontal line. The ball of mass $M$ is drawn aside in their common plane until its centre has been raised by $h$ and is then released. If $M \neq m$ and all collisions are elastic, how must $\mu$ be chosen so that the ball of mass $m$ rises to the greatest possible height? What is this height? (Neglect multiple collisions.)
