An electrical transformer is connected as shown in the circuit below. Both windings of the transformer have the same number of loops and the self-inductance of the both coils is equal to $L$; there is no leakage of the magnetic field lines from the core so that the mutual inductance is also equal to $L$.
(a) Consider the case when the coil windings are oriented so that if the both coils have current flowing from left to right then the magnetic fields in the transformer core add up destructively Find the currents in the resistors immediately after the switch is closed.
(b) Under the assumption of the task (a), find the current in the left resistor as a function of time.
(c) Now, let one of the coils have a reversed direction of winding; find the current in the right resistor as a function of time.
