6. The device shown schematically below consists of two identical interconnected units, with potential $V_{0}$ applied relative to the ground as shown.
Each unit contains a long coil of resistance of $R=2 \Omega$, wound with $n=10^{4}$ turns per metre, and along the axis of which runs a superconducting wire. Each superconducting wire (labelled $X$ and $Y$ ) has zero resistance when the magnetic flux density is below $B_{0}=10^{-2} \mathrm{~T}$, but has a resistance of $S=10 \Omega$ when driven into the normal (non-superconducting) state by a sufficiently large magnetic field.
![](spot-2018-q4-fig01.png)
    (a) Describe and explain how the device can act as a bistable logic element.
    (b) Calculate the limits for $V_{0}$.
