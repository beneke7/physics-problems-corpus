In a weather forecast not only the temperature but the ``feels like'' temperature is often given, which might be lower or higher than the temperature measured by the thermometer. When we have just finished a shower in the shower enclosure, then we feel the temperature warmer than the measured temperature in the bathroom, but if we open the door of the enclosure, because we left the towel outside, then our ``feels like'' temperature is much lower than the real temperature in the bathroom. Explain what is the reason for the fact that despite the temperature in the bathroom is nearly constant, our sense of temperature changes so abruptly. 
 (3 pont)
