A tram, filled with stones, moves along a on a horizontal straight track. Its total mass with the stones is M . How much work is done while a stone of mass m is thrown out of the tram with a horizontal speed of v with respect to the tram if it is thrown backwards or if it is thrown forward. Find the kinetic energy of the stone in both cases.
 (5 pont)
