Two fixed, point-like charges of charge $q$ are found at the endpoints of a horizontal line segment of length $2d$. A ball of mass $m$ and of charge $Q$ is dropped from point $P_1$ which is on the vertical, perpendicular bisector of the line segment. The ball stops exactly at point $P_2$. 

 $a)$ What is the mass of the ball? 
 $b)$ What is the acceleration of the ball at point $P_2$? 
 Data: $q=2\cdot 10^{-6}$ C, $Q=-6\cdot 10^{-6}$ C, $d=12$ cm, $OP_1=5$ cm, $OP_2=10$ cm. 
 (4 pont)
