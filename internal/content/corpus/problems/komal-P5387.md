Different resistors of resistances $R$ are connected to a battery of electromotive force $U_0$ and of internal resistance $R_{\mathrm{b}}$.
 $a)$ What is the maximum ``useful'' power (dissipated in the external resistor) that this battery can deliver? At what external resistance value $R$ can we achieve this maximum power $P_\text{max}$?
 $b)$ Show that for any other power $P$ which is smaller than $P_\text{max}$, there are two external resistors of resistances $R_1\ne R_2$ at which the dissipated power is $P$. What is the arithmetic and the geometric mean of $R_1$ and $R_2$?
 $c)$ What is the sum of the terminal voltages across the battery in the above two cases?
 $d)$ What is the sum of the currents through $R_1$ and $R_2$?
 $e)$ The efficiency of the delivered energy is defined as the ratio of the useful power to the total power delivered by the battery. What is the sum of the efficiencies in the above two cases?
