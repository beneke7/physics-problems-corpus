Different amount of samples of water of different temperature values are mixed. The corresponding volume and temperature data are the following: 1 litre water at $10\;{}^\circ$C, 2 litres water at $20\;{}^\circ$C, 3 litres water at $30\;{}^\circ$C, 4 litres water at $40\;{}^\circ$C, 5 litres water at $50\;{}^\circ$C, 6 litres water at $60\;{}^\circ$C, 7 litres water at $70\;{}^\circ$C, 8 litres water at $80\;{}^\circ$C and 9 litres water at $90\;{}^\circ$C. What is the common temperature of the mixture if heat losses are negligible?
 (3 pont)
