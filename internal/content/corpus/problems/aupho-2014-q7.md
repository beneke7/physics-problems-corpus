Question 7
The donkey of Question 6 now pulls harder and doubles the magnitude of the force $P$. Which of the following options must be true?

a. $F_{g}$ and $N$ remain constant but $f$ increases to counter the increase in $P$.
b. $F_{g}, N$ and $f$ all remain constant despite the increase in $P$.
c. $F_{g}$ and $f$ remain constant but $N$ decreases due to the increase in $P$.
d. $F_{g}$ remains constant but $N$ and hence $f$ decrease due to the increase in $P$.
e. none of these.
