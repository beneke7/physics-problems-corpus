A thin light ray enters into a prism of refractive index $n_1=1.8$. The cross section of the prism is an equilateral triangle and the path of the light ray in the prism is symmetrical about the bisecting plane.
 $a)$ What is the angle of incidence of the incident light ray?
 $b)$ What is the angle between the incident ray and the light ray which emerges from the prism, the so-called angle of deviation ?
 $c)$ Then the light source and prism system is fixed and submerged into some liquid of refractive index of $n_2=1.5$. What is the angle of deviation of the light ray in this case?
 (5 pont)
