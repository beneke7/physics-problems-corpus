Two positrons are at opposite corners of a square of side $a=1 \mathrm{~cm}$. The other two corners of the square are each occupied by a proton, as shown in the figure.
![](../../../../../sources/200ppp/figures/200ppp-090.jpg)

Initially the particles are held in these positions, but all four are released at the same time. What will their speeds be when they are a significant distance apart? The particles can be considered as classical point masses moving in each other's electric fields. Gravity can be ignored.
