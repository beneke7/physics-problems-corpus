**10. GLASS PLATE (10 points)**

**Equipment:** laser ($\lambda=650 \mathrm{~nm}$), thin glass plate, lens, ruler.

**Note:** The glass plate is fixed to a stand; avoid touching the glass itself
(because its edges are sharp and it can break easily).

Find the thickness of the plate and estimate the uncertainty of your result. Draw a
sketch of your experimental setup.
