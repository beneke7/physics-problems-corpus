A2. A hot air balloon with average density $1.20 \mathrm{~kg} / \mathrm{m}^{3}$ is in equilibrium at a height of 1.00 km above sea level. A gust of wind pushes the balloon to a height of 1.10 km above sea level and releases it at rest.
(20) a. How long does it take for the balloon to return to its equilibrium position?
(5) b. How fast is it going when it passes through equilibrium?

Assume that: The atmospheric density is a linear function of height.
The balloon volume and average density are constant.
Variations in the gravitational field with height are negligible.
The density of air at sea level is $1.29 \mathrm{~kg} / \mathrm{m}^{3}$.
Drag is negligible.
