If one hits something rigid - e.g. a lamppost with a bat, the hand holding the bat may get stung (hurt) as long as the impact misses the so-called centre of percussion of the bat (and hits either below or above such a centre). Determine the position of the centre of percussion for a bat of uniform density. You may assume that during an impact the bat is rotating around its holding hand.
