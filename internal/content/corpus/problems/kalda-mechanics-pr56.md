A straight homogeneous rod is being externally supported against a vertical wall such that the angle between the wall and the rod is $\alpha<90^{\circ}$. For which values of $\alpha$ can the rod remain stationary when thus supported? Consider two scenarios: a) the wall is slippery and the floor is rough with the friction coefficient $\mu ; \mathrm{b})$ the floor is slippery and the wall is rough with the friction coefficient $\mu$.
