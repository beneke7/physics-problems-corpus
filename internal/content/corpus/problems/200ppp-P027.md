A circular hole of radius $r$ at the bottom of an initially full water container is sealed by a ball of mass $m$ and radius $R(>r)$. The depth of the water is now slowly reduced, and when it reaches a certain value, $h_{0}$, the ball rises out of the hole. Find $h_{0}$.
