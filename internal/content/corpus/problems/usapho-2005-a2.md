A2. (25) A sample of water of mass 0.0360 kg and temperature $100^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ is added to an enclosed container of volume $2.00 \mathrm{~m}^{3}$ that contains air at a pressure of $1.00 \times 10^{5} \mathrm{~Pa}$ and a temperature of 500 K Assume both air and steam can be treated as ideal gases and that all of the water vaporizes. What is the final temperature and pressure inside the container?

Possibly useful information:

| Molar Heat Capacities | Air | Steam |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| $\mathrm{C}_{\mathrm{V}}$ (at constant volume) | $20.8 \mathrm{~J} /(\mathrm{mol} \cdot \mathrm{K})$ | $27.9 \mathrm{~J} /(\mathrm{mol} \cdot \mathrm{K})$ |
| $\mathrm{C}_{\mathrm{P}}$ (at constant pressure) | $29.1 \mathrm{~J} /(\mathrm{mol} \cdot \mathrm{K})$ | $36.2 \mathrm{~J} /(\mathrm{mol} \cdot \mathrm{K})$ |

$L_{v}($ Latent heat of vaporization $)=2.26 \times 10^{6} \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{kg}$
Molar mass of water $=180 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{mol}$
