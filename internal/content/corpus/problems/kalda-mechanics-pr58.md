A wedge with the angle $\alpha$ at the tip is lying on the horizontal floor. There is a hole with smooth walls in the ceiling. A rod has been inserted snugly into that hole, and it can move up and down without friction, while its axis is fixed to be vertical. The rod is supported against the wedge; the only point with friction is the contact point of the wedge and the rod: the friction coefficient there is $\mu$. For which values of $\mu$ is it possible to push the wedge through, behind the rod, by only applying a sufficiently large horizontal force?
