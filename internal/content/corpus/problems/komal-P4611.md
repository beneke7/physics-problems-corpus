An insulating rubber thread of spring constant  D is attached to the ceiling of a room of height  h . The floor of the room has a big enough area, and it is made of metal, which is earthed. A light small metal sphere is attached to the other end of the insulating rubber thread; the original length of the rubber thread is . If the sphere is charged to Q then the length of the thread increases to a length of 0.75 . Is it possible to increase the charge on the sphere very slowly to 3 Q , such that the sphere does not touch the floor? (The electrostatic effect of the ceiling and the walls are negligible.)
 (5 pont)
