**8. Airplanes (7 points)** — *Janis Huns, Jaan Kalda.*

Two airplanes pass each other while flying at a constant altitudes. While they have identical airspeeds, their ground speeds are $v_{1}$ and $v_{2}$, respectively, and the angle between the velocity vectors is $\alpha$. Based on this knowledge, what is the minimal possible value

**i)** *(1 point)* of the airspeed of the planes;

**ii)** *(3 points)* of the wind speed at the altitudes of the planes?

**iii)** *(3 points)* Answer the question ii) if now $v_{1}=v_{2}=v$, but it is known that the airspeed of one of the planes is two times bigger than that of the other.
