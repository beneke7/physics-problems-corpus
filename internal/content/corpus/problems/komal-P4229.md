The heating system keeps the temperature of a flat at about 20 $^\circ$C. Outside the weather is cold and damp: the temperature is 0 $^\circ$C, and the relative humidity is 95%. What will the relative humidity of the air in the room be after airing the room, when the temperature reaches again the value of 20 $^\circ$C? Approximately what amount of water must be vaporized in a flat of area 50  m $^{2}$ and of height 3 m, in order to reach the 50% relative humidity, pleasant for the human mucous membrane.
 (5 pont)
