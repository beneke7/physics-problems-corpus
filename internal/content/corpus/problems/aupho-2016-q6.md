Question 6
The electrostatic force $F_{e}$ that an object of charge $Q$ exerts on another object of charge $q$ is given by Coulomb's law:

$$
F_{e}=\frac{1}{4 \pi \varepsilon_{0}} \frac{Q q}{r^{2}},
$$

where the charges of the two objects, $Q$ and $q$, are measured in units of coulombs (C), and $r$ is the distance separating the two objects.
What are the units of the constant $\varepsilon_{0}$ ?

a. $\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{m}^{3} \cdot \mathrm{~s}^{-2} \cdot \mathrm{C}^{2}$
b. $\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{m}^{-3} \cdot \mathrm{~s}^{2} \cdot \mathrm{C}^{-2}$
c. $\mathrm{kg} . \mathrm{m} . \mathrm{s}^{2} . \mathrm{C}^{-2}$
d. $\mathrm{s}^{2} . \mathrm{C}^{2} . \mathrm{kg}^{-1} . \mathrm{m}^{-3}$
e. $\mathrm{s}^{2} . \mathrm{C}^{2} . \mathrm{kg}^{-1} . \mathrm{m}^{-1}$
