The most common heating fuel is natural gas. The gas utility company uses the following method to determine the monthly price to be paid, which is shown on the gas bill (in Hungarian): the amount of consumed gas is multiplied by a so-called correction factor, then the heating value of the gas in MJ is calculated, and finally the heating value is used to calculate the amount of money to be paid. To this, a basic monthly household charge is added. Find a recent gas bill and a recent electricity bill and answer the following questions (in your answers always give the gross values!) 
 $a)$ Why is it necessary to use the correction factor? 
 $b)$ What is the price of $1~\mathrm{m}^3$ natural gas at standard conditions? 
 $c)$ How much does 1 MJ of energy cost if it comes into your home as gas or as electricity? 
 (4 pont)
