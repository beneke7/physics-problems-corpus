If a playing card is placed between a small, strong magnet and a horizontal paper clip, then we can lift the paper clip by the card. Measure how many cards you need to stack so that you can no longer lift the paper clip. What is the thickness of these stacked sheets? Connect two magnets of the same size and investigate how many cards are needed so that we can no longer lift the paper clip.
 (6 pont)
