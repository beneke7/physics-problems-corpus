At home make an inclined plane of small and variable angle of inclination. Fix the slope at a certain angle of inclination of $\alpha$. Place a cylinder-shaped pencil on the slope such that the symmetry axis of the pencil makes an angle of $\beta$ with a horizontal line lying in the plane of the slope. Investigate, at a certain fixed angle of $\alpha$, at which angle of $\beta$ will the pencil begin to move along the plane such that it
 $a)$ slides down, but does not roll at all;
 $b)$ rolls down without slipping?
 Investigate the rolling and slipping regions, and plot them in a coordinate system of $(\alpha,\beta)$.
 (6 pont)
