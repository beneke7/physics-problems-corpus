Beads of equal mass are strung at equal distances on a long, horizontal wire. The beads are initially at rest but can move without friction.
![](../../sources/200ppp/figures/200ppp-027.jpg)

One of the beads is continuously accelerated (towards the right) by a constant force $F$. What are the speeds of the accelerated bead and the front of the 'shock wave', after a long time, if the collisions of the beads are:
(i) completely inelastic,
(ii) perfectly elastic?
