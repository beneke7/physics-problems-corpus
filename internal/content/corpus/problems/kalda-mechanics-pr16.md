A hemispherical container is placed upside down on a smooth horizontal surface. Through a small hole at the bottom of the container, water is then poured in. Exactly when the container gets full, water starts leaking from between the table and the edge of the container. Find the mass of the container if water has density $\rho$ and radius of the hemisphere is $R$.
