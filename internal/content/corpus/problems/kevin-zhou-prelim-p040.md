[3] Problem 40. A uniform string of length $L$ and linear density $ρ$ is stretched between two fixed supports. The tension in the string is $T$.

(a) Find the standing wave solutions and angular frequencies for the given boundary conditions.

(b) A very small mass $m$ is now placed a distance $ℓ$ from one end of the string. Find the change in the angular frequencies to first order in $m$, using the fact that the average potential energy and average kinetic energy for each standing wave should remain equal.

(c) How would you go about finding the exact standing wave frequencies for this setup?
