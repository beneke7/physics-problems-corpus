A4. An ideal gas has a heat capacity per unit mole at constant pressure of $C_{\mathrm{P}}=41.9 \mathrm{~J} /(\mathrm{mol} \cdot \mathrm{K})$ and a heat capacity per unit mole at constant volume of $C_{\mathrm{V}}=33.5 \mathrm{~J} /(\mathrm{mol} \cdot \mathrm{K})$. An 0.100-mole sample of this gas goes through the accompanying cycle. Process 1->2 is isobaric (constant pressure), 2->3 is adiabatic (no heat enters or leaves), and 3->1 is isothermal (constant temperature).
$$
\begin{aligned}
T_{1}=300 \mathrm{~K} & T_{2}=400 \mathrm{~K} \\
V_{1}=0.0118 \mathrm{~m}^{3} & V_{2}=0.0157 \mathrm{~m}^{3}
\end{aligned}
$$
(15) a. Find the work done by the gas during each process $\left(W_{1->2}, W_{2->3}\right.$, and $\left.W_{3->1}\right)$.
