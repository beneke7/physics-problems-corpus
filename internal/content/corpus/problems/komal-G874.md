A truck needs to be loaded with $M=3~\mathrm{ton}$ of coal. The coal is at a distance of $L=20~\mathrm{m}$ from the truck. Two conveyor belts of length $L=20~\mathrm{m}$ can be used for loading. One conveyor belt can transport $m_1=10~\mathrm{kg}$ of coal in a second at a speed of $v_1=1~\mathrm{m/s}$, the other can transport $m_2=15~\mathrm{kg}$ of coal in each second at a speed of $v_2=0.5~\mathrm{m/s}$. What is the minimum time to load the coal onto the truck?
 (4 pont)
