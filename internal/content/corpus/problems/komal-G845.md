A car and a lorry leave a crossroad junction at the same time. (The roads are perpendicular to each other.) The car is travelling at a uniform speed of $20~\text{m}/\text{s}$ and the truck at $15~\text{m}/\text{s}$. After 10 minutes, the truck reaches a crossroad, it turns $90^\circ$ and continues travelling at its previous speed on the road. What might the distance between the car and the truck be 20 minutes after they left the junction?
 (3 pont)
