A spring (designed for both compression and extension) of length $\ell$ is compressed to a length of $d<\ell/2$ with a force of $F_1$. Then the spring is halved and the two alike springs are held next to each other and compressed (in parallel) again, to a length of $d$. What is the magnitude of the force $F_2$, which is to be exerted?
 How many equal pieces should a spring of length $\ell=10d$ be cut into, in order to maximize the force $F_n$, which is exerted when all the pieces, held parallel, are compressed to a length of $d$? What is the magnitude of this force?
 (5 pont)
