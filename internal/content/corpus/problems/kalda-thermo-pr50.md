Glycerol is stored in a tightly closed vessel of volume $V=1 \mathrm{l}$; inside glycerol, there is an air bubble of volume $w=1 \mathrm{ml}$. At temperature $T_{0}=20^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$, the pressure inside the vessel is $p_{0}=1 \mathrm{~atm}$. The linear expansion coefficient of the vessel material is very small and the vessel walls are thick. The volumetric expansion coefficient of the glycerol $\alpha=5.1 \times 10^{-4} \mathrm{~K}^{-1}$. Air can be treated as an ideal gas.
a) Find the dependence of the pressure inside the bottle as a function of temperature.
b) At which temperature and for which physical reason the obtained equality will no longer be valid?
