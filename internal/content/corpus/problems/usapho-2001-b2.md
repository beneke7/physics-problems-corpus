B2. (Cylindrical coordinates are used in this problem and $\hat{\mathrm{u}}_{r}, \hat{\mathrm{u}}_{\theta}$, and $\hat{\mathrm{u}}_{z}$ represent unit vectors in the $r, \theta$, and $z$ directions, respectively. The coordinates form a right-handed orthogonal system, e.g., $\hat{u}_{r} \times \hat{u}_{\theta}=\hat{u}_{z}$.) A particle with charge $q$ and mass $m$ is constrained to move in the $x y$-plane with velocity that can be expressed as $\bar{v}=v_{r} \hat{u}_{r}+r \omega \tilde{u}_{\theta}$. The particle is initially at rest and a distance $r_{u}$ from the origin. Two forces act on the particle. One is due to the presence of a uniform magnetic field given by $\vec{B}=B_{o} \hat{\mathrm{u}}_{z}$. The other force and its corresponding potential energy are:

$$
\vec{F}=\frac{-2 k}{r^{3}} \hat{\mathrm{u}}_{r} \quad \text { and } \quad U(r)=-\frac{k}{r^{2}}
$$

where $k$ is a constant and $r$ is the distance from the origin. The particle executes a trajectory that periodically varies between its initial radius $r_{0}$ and a minimum radius, $\mathrm{r}_{\text {min- }}$ Express your answers in terms of $q, m, v_{r}, r, \omega, B_{0}, k, r_{0}, r_{\text {min }}$, and the unit vectors.
