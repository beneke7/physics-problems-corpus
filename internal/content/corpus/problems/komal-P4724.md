The working temperature of the tungsten filament of an incandescent lamp rated at 230 V and 100 W is $2400~{}^\circ$C. In an experiment the voltage applied to the lamp is gradually increased from zero, and it can be seen that the filament begins to glow at 17 V. What is the temperature and the resistance of the filament in this state, and how much power is dissipated in this state of the bulb?
 We can assume that the emitted power is proportional to the fourth power of the temperature measured in Kelvins, the fifth power of the resistance is proportional to the sixth power of the absolute temperature and heat conduction is negligible.
 (5 pont)
