Let us consider the electric circuit in the figure, for which $L_{1}=10 \mathrm{mH}, L_{2}=20 \mathrm{mH}, C_{1}=10 \mathrm{nF}$, $C_{2}=5 \mathrm{nF}, R=100 \mathrm{k} \Omega$. The switch $K$ being closed, the circuit is coupled with a source of alternating current. The current furnished by the source has constant intensity while the frequency of the current may be varied.
(a) Find the ratio of frequency $\nu_{M} / \Delta \nu$, where $\nu_{m}$ is the frequency for which the active power in circuit has the maximum value $P_{m}$, and the frequency difference $\Delta \nu=\nu_{+}-\nu_{-}$, where $\nu_{+}$and $\nu_{-}$are the frequencies for which the active power in the circuit is half of the maximum power $P=\frac{1}{2} P_{m}$.
