10 cm$^{3}$ water is poured into a container of volume 400 cm$^{3}$ at normal atmospheric pressure and at room temperature (20 $^\circ$C). Then the container is closed hermetically and heated to a temperature of 150 $^\circ$C. By what amount will the volume of the water change? (Do not consider the change in the volume of the container.)
