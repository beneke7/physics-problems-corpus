A piston with a mass of $0.5~\mathrm{kg}$ divides the air in a fixed horizontal cylindrical tank into two parts. The walls of the tank and the piston have good thermal insulation, and the piston can move frictionlessly (see the figure ). Initially, the piston is fixed in such a position that the volume of air in the left part is $3~\mathrm{dm}^3$ and the pressure is $100~\mathrm{kPa}$, while in the other part the volume is $2~\mathrm{dm}^3$ and the pressure is $200~\mathrm{kPa}$. At this state the temperature of the air in both parts is $300~\mathrm{K}$. 

 The piston is released from its clamping. 
 a) After releasing the piston from the clamping, what is the maximum speed to which the piston accelerates? 
 b) What will be the maximum temperature of the air column on the left?
