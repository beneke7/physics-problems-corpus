10. A photon of frequency $\nu$ is scattered, after colliding with an electron initially at rest through an angle of 90°. Show that its frequency $\nu^{\prime}$ after being scattered is given by
$$
\nu^{\prime}=\frac{m_{e} c^{2}}{h \nu+m_{e} c^{2}} \nu .
$$
Determine the kinetic energy of the recoiling electron after the scattering if the X-ray photon has initial energy 50.0 keV. [8 marks]
