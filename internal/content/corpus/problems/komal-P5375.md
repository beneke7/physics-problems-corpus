One end of a thin, uniform-density stick lying in a frictionless horizontal plane is suddenly struck so that the the velocity of the end point is perpendicular to the stick and has a magnitude of $v$.
 $a)$ Which part of the stick will have zero initial speed?
 $b)$ What is the speed and direction of the motion of the other end of the stick, at the moment when it starts to move?
 (5 pont)
