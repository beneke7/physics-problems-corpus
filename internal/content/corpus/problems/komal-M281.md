According to a housewife if water at a temperature of 100 $^\circ$C, is poured from the kettle to the cup from a height of 15-20 cm, than the water in the cup will be colder than if the water is poured at the height of the rim of the cup.
 Check with measurement whether she is right or wrong.
 (6 pont)
