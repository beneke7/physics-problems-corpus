A trolley of mass $m$ can move freely in a box of mass $m$. The box can move on the tabletop which is covered by a thin layer of oil, the frictional force depends on only the speed of the box: $\boldsymbol F=-k\boldsymbol v$. Initially the box stays at rest, and the trolley starts from the wall on the left-hand side at a speed of $v_0$ and moves towards the wall on the right. How many times will the trolley of length $\ell$ collide elastically with the box of length $L$? (The elastic collision is ensured by the small springs attached to the trolley, their length is much smaller than $\ell$.) 

 (5 pont)
