The nominal voltage of a light bulb is $\overline{V_{0}}=26 \mathrm{~V}$ and nominal current $I_{0}=0.12 \mathrm{~A}$. Cold tungsten filament of this light bulb has resistance $R_{0}=24 \Omega$. Estimate the length $l$ and the diameter $d$ of this filament. Also, at which temperature $T$ is this filament supposed to emit light (when working in nominal regime)? The resistivity of tungsten at room temperature $\rho_{0}=5.3 \times 10^{-8} \Omega \cdot \mathrm{~m}$. For metals, the resistivity can be taken to be proportional to the temperature in Kelvins. Treat tungsten as a perfectly grey body which absorbs $k=0.3$ of the incident radiation at any wavelength. Stefan-Boltzmann constant $\sigma=5.67 \times 10^{-8} \mathrm{~W} /\left(\mathrm{m}^{2} \cdot \mathrm{~K}^{4}\right)$. Neglect thermal expansion of the filament.
