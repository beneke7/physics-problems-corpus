An electron-positron pair is produced from a photon of energy $2~\text{MeV}$, when it passes next to a nucleus, which has a high atomic number. (The heavy nucleus gains only momentum, and absorbs almost no energy.) In the Wilson chamber, placed into magnetic field, both particles travel in the same plane, along circular arcs of radius $5~\text{cm}$. What is the magnitude of the magnetic induction vector?
