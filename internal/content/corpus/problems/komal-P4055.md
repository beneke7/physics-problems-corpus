One end of a long, thin elastic sheet of negligible mass is attached to the rim of a horizontal table, and a 3-kg object is fixed to its other end. At the equilibrium position the end of the sheet descends by 6 cm below the horizontal. Then the object is raised by 4 cm above the horizontal.
 How much work is done while the object is raised?
 (4 pont)
