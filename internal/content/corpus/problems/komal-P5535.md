The surface of a $2~\text{m}^2$ shade sail is nearly horizontal. Suddenly, it starts raining at a steady pace. There is a small hole in the middle of the sail through which the rainwater flows off. A cylindrical container, which has a hole of cross section $5~\text{mm}^2$ at its bottom, is placed under the jet of water. The rise of the water level in the container stops at $20~\text{cm}$. Determine how many mm of precipitation fell during the 10 minutes of rain.
