A small body of mass $m$ is placed (but not fixed) onto the top of a thin cylindrical ring of radius $R$ and of negligible mass being at rest on the horizontal ground. The system is displaced from its unstable equilibrium position. As the ring rolls faster and faster, the small body flies off somewhere. 
 $a)$ What is the least value of the coefficient of static friction between the surfaces in contact if neither the small body on the ring nor the ring on the ground is skidding during the motion? 
 $b)$ Where will the small object hit the ground? 
 (6 pont)
