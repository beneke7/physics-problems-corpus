A $0.6-\mathrm{kg}$ block of ice at $-10^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ is placed into a closed empty $1 \mathrm{~m}^{3}$ container, also at a temperature of $-10^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$. The temperature of the container is then increased to $100^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$. How much greater is the heat required than that necessary to raise the empty container alone to that temperature?
