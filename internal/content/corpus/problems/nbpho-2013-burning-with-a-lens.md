**7. BURNING WITH A LENS (7 points)**

Sunrays are focused with a lens of diameter $d=10 \mathrm{~cm}$ and focal length of $f=7 \mathrm{~cm}$ to a black thin plate. Behind the plate is a mirror. Angular diameter of the Sun is $\alpha=32^{\prime}$ and its intensity on the surface of the Earth is $I=1000 \mathrm{~W} / \mathrm{m}^{2}$, Stefan-Boltzmann constant $\sigma=5.670 \times 10^{-8} \mathrm{~W} /\left(\mathrm{m}^{2} \mathrm{~K}^{4}\right)$.

i) (4 points) Find the temperature of the heated point of the plate.

ii) (3 points) Using thermodynamic arguments, estimate the maximal diameter of the lens for which this model can be used.
