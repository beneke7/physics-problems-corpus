Q3.

(a) What is an electrostatic (i) field line and (ii) equipotential surface?
(b) An electrostatic field is produced by a charge $+4 Q$ at A and $-Q$ at B . The distance $\mathrm{AB}=a$. Determine the position, C , of the neutral point/s, where the electrostatic field is zero.
(c) Write down, in its simplest form, an equation for a point on the equipotential surface, with potential $V$, in terms of the distances, $r_{A}$ and $r_{B}$, respective from $A$ and B.
(d) Sketch, in a plane containing AB, (i) the field lines and (ii) the equipotentials.
(e) Three equal charges, each of charge $+Q$, are situated at the corners of a right angled triangle , with sides of length $3 a, 4 a$ and $5 a$.
Determine the potential and electric field vector at the mid-point, M, of the side of length $3 a$.
