A pencil, which can be regarded as a uniform rod, is placed on a table top and it rests on its end with an eraser. A thread is attached to the tip of the pencil, perpendicular to the pencil, to prevent it from falling.
 a) What is at least the coefficient of static friction between the eraser and the table top if the pencil does not slip when it makes an angle of $15^\circ$ with the vertical?
 b) At what coefficient of friction can the pencil be ``laid down'' slowly on the table without the bottom end slipping, if the thread is perpendicular to the pencil during the whole movement?
 (5 pont)
