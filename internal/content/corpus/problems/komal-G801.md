Two men, each having a mass of 72 kg, are standing on skis on the horizontal ground. The pressure exerted on the snow by the first man is 0.02 bar, and the pressure exerted by the second one is 0.05 bar. The second man is also carrying a 5 kg backpack on his back.
 $a)$ What is the surface area of the skis, which is in contact with the snow?
 $b)$ What will the pressure exerted by the second man be if he drops his backpack and stands on one leg only?
