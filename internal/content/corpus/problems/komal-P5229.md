Two point-like charges, each having a charge of $Q$, are fixed at a distance of $2L$ from each other in weightlessness. Between these charges another point like charge of mass $m$ and of charge $q$ ($q$ and $Q$ are opposite charges) is revolving about the symmetry axis of the charges along a circular path of radius $R$. The plane of this circle is the perpendicular bisector of the line segment that joins the two fixed charges. 
 $a)$ Determine the period of the circular motion as a function of the radius of the path. 
 $b)$ Evaluate the limiting cases when $R\ll L$ and when $R\gg L$. 
 $c)$ Determine which is greater: the period of the motion of charge $q$ along the circular path, or the period of the same charge when it undergoes simple harmonic motion along one of the diameters of the circle with amplitude $R$? 
 (Neglect the deceleration of the charge due to the radiation of the accelerating charge and due to air resistance.) 
 (6 pont)
