In a science fiction novel, the following situation is described. There is an emergency on a spaceship, and an astronaut got by an accident to a distance of $L=100 \mathrm{~m}$ from the spaceship. He has a cup with solidified water (ice) and uses the sublimation (evaporation) of ice to return to the spaceship. Estimate, how realistic is this method. You may assume that the sublimation takes place at a constant temperature $T=272 \mathrm{~K}$, by which the pressure of saturated vapours is $P=550 \mathrm{~Pa}$. Estimate the dimensions of the cup and the mass of the water by yourself.
