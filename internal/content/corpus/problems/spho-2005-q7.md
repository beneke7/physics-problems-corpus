7. Two particles with rest masses $m_{1}$ and $m_{2}$ move collinearly in some inertial frame with uniform velocities $u_{1}$ and $u_{2}$ respectively. They collide and form a single particle with rest mass $m$ moving at velocity $u$. Prove that
$$
m^{2}=m_{1}^{2}+m_{2}^{2}+2 m_{1} m_{2} \gamma\left(u_{1}\right) \gamma\left(u_{2}\right)\left(1-\frac{u_{1} u_{2}}{c^{2}}\right),
$$
where $\gamma(u)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-u^{2} / c^{2}}}$. Find an expression for $u$. [14]
