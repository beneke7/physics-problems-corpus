The root-mean-square (r.m.s.) value of the voltage of the 50 Hz mains is 230 V. The r.m.s. value of the voltage across a condenser of a series RLC circuit is 100 V, and that of across the ohmic resistor is 200 V. The r.m.s. value of the current in the circuit is 200 mA. What is the relative permeability constant of the iron core of the coil, if the length of the coil is 8 cm, its cross section is 4 cm$^{2}$ and its number of turns is 300?
