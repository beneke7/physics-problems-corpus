An electron is shot into uniform magnetic field of magnitude B =10$^{-3}$ T the width of which is d =2 cm. The velocity of the electron is perpendicular to the magnetic field lines, the angle between the velocity of the electron and the normal of the boundary is =30$^\circ$. 
 a ) What is the least velocity of the electron if it goes through the region of magnetic field of width  d ? 
 b ) What is the speed below which the electron cannot go through the magnetic field of width  d ?
