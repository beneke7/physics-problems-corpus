A table of height 1 m has a hole in the middle of its surface. A thin, golden chain necklace, of length 1 m , is placed loosely coiled close to the hole, as shown in the figure.
![](../../sources/200ppp/figures/200ppp-045.jpg)

One end of the chain is pulled a little way through the hole and then released. Friction is negligible, and, as a result, the chain runs smoothly through the hole with increasing speed. After what times will the two ends of the chain reach the floor?
