What is the temperature at the top of a hill of height $\bar{H}=200 \mathrm{~m}$ if at the bottom of the valley $t_{0}=20^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ ? You can assume that when air masses move along the slope of the hill, air expansion and/or contraction can be considered to be adiabatic. The air pressure at the valley level $p_{0}=10^{5} \mathrm{~Pa}$, air density $\rho=1,29 \mathrm{~kg} / \mathrm{m}^{3}$ and adiabatic index $\gamma=1,4$.
