Measuring cylinder has volume $V=$ 100 ml and height $h=20 \mathrm{~cm}$. There are marker lines drawn on the cylinder after each 1 ml . Suppose the cylinder is used for measuring water volume, and the reading is taken according to the lowest point of the curved water surface. How large mistake is made due to the fact that the water surface is curved, and not flat? Surface tension coefficient for water $\sigma=0.073 \mathrm{~N} / \mathrm{m}$; Assume that water wets perfectly the walls of the cylinder.
