Two nails at a distance of $L$ are sticking out of a vertical wall at the same height. A piece of rope is placed to the nails such that it sags to a depth of $H$. Estimate the length of the rope if it is known that 
 $a)$ $H\ll L$; 
 $b)$ $L \ll H.$ 

 Friction is negligible everywhere. 
 (5 pont)
