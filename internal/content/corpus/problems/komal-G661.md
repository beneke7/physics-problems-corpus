Three types of liquid, which do not mix with each other, are poured into a graduated cylinder: 100 g water of density 1000 kg/m${}^3$, 200 g oil of density 0.8 g/cm${}^3$, and mercury, such that the graduated cylinder of volume 400 cm${}^3$ and of height 40 cm is fully filled. How many grams of mercury was poured into the cylinder? At what height, measured from the bottom of the cylinder, are the boundary layers which separate the different liquids? (The density of mercury is $13\,600$ kg/m${}^3$.) 
 (3 pont)
