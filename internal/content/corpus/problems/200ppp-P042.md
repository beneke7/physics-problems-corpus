A uniform rod of mass $m$ and length $\ell$ is supported horizontally
at its ends by my two forefingers. Whilst I am slowly bringing my fingers together to meet under the centre of the rod, it slides on either one or other of them. How much work do I have to do during the process if the coefficient of static friction is $\mu_{\text {stat }}$, and that of kinetic friction is $\mu_{\text {kin }}\left(\mu_{\text {kin }} \leq \mu_{\text {stat }}\right)$ ?
