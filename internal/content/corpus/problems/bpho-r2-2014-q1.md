1. Explain or comment on the following. More marks will be awarded for numerate or algebraic solutions where possible. Estimate quantities that you may need if these are not given.
    i) Light hitting a single small hole will spread out.
    ii) Why do you think that stars appear to twinkle to the naked eye when planets do not?
    iii) Figures 1.1 and 1.2 show a comet with its tail. On the original photo the tail is so transparent that stars can be seen through the tail. Why does the comet's tail always point away from the sun? Why are there two tails, as seen in Figure 1.2? Explain what orbit the comet might follow.

iv) It seems odd that the planets' orbits round the Sun are nearly circular. Why do you think this is?
