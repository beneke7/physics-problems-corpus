[Est-Fin-2013] An inductance $L$ and a capacitor $C$ are connected in series with a switch. Initially the switch is open and the capacitor is given a charge $q_{0}$. Now the switch is closed.
(a) What are the charge $q$ on the capacitor and the current $I$ in the circuit as functions of time? Draw the phase diagram of the system - the evolution of the system on a $I-q$ graph - and note the curve's parameters. Note the direction of the system's evolution with arrow(s).
