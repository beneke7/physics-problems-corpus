**4. MAGNETIC FIELD (6 points)**

A magnetic field with flux density $B$ (parallel to the $z$-axis) fills the region
$x^{2}+y^{2}<R^{2}$. Consider an electron with velocity
$v=R B e / m$, where $e$ is its charge and $m$ is its mass.

1) Sketch the trajectory of the particle if, initially, it moves along the line
$y=0$ towards the region filled with the magnetic field.

2) How much time does the electron spend inside the magnetic field?

3) Now consider the situation in which the electron initially moves along the line
$y=a$ ($a<R$). Find the angle $\alpha$ through which the electron is deflected
after passing through the magnetic field.
