By Jaan Kalda  (TalTech).

A spherical ball of radius $R=1$cm is made from a NdFeB alloy. The figure below shows the left upper quadrant ($H<0$, $B>0$) of the hysteresis loop of this alloy for two temperatures, $T_1= 20^\circ$C,  and  $T_2= 120^\circ$C . Initially, the ball has been magnetized by putting it into a very strong homogeneous magnetic field (with   $B\approx 3$T). Then, the external magnetic field is removed while the temperature of the ball is kept equal to $T_1= 20^\circ$C . After that, the ball is heated up to the temperature   $T_2= 120^\circ$C . By how much did the magnetic dipole moment of the ball change due to heating?
