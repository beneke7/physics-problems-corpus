Frictionless movable pistons are in a horizontal cylinder, closed at both ends, and confine samples of diatomic, ideal gas of the same type and of the same volume of $V_0=2~\rm dm^3$ at a pressure of $p_0=10^4$ Pa, and at the same temperature. The pistons are connected by a spring the length of which when it is not compressed or stretched is the same as the length of the cylinder. Between the pistons there is vacuum. 

 $a)$ What is the energy stored in the spring in its compressed position described above? 
 $b)$ The gas samples are heated slowly in the same way, and their absolute temperature is tripled. What will the pressure of the gas samples be at the end of the heating process? 
 $c)$ How much heat was added to the system, if the losses are neglected? 
 (5 pont)
