The electromotive force of a battery is $\mathcal{E}=18$ V. There is a 0.2 A current through one of the two resistors connected in parallel, which has a resistance of $R_x$, whilst the resistance of the other resistor is $R=8~\Omega$. If this resistor is disconnected from the circuit by switching off switch K, then the current through the resistor $R_x$ increases to 0.3 A. What is the resistance of the resistor, and what is the internal resistance of the battery?
