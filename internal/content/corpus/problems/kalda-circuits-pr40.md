In the circuit below, the "device" takes the reading of the ammeter and adjusts the resistance of the rheostat so that the ammeter reading becomes zero. Find the voltage on the resistance $R_{3}$. It is known that $V=5 \mathrm{~V}$, $R_{1}=10 \Omega, R_{2}=1 \mathrm{k} \Omega, R_{3}=100 \mathrm{k} \Omega, R_{4}=4.99 \mathrm{k} \Omega$.
