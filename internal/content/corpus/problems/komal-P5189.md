There is a small air bubble at the middle of a long cylinder-shaped tube, filled with glycerine. When the tube is held vertically, the air bubble is raising at a constant speed of 1 cm/s. Where will the air bubble stop in the tube when it is held horizontally and is accelerated along its symmetry axis to a final speed of 20 m/s? Where will the bubble move if the tube's speed is uniformly increased to 30 m/s, and where will it stop if the tube is ceased to move? (The short time intervals when the bubble starts and stops, and the retarding effect of the wall of the tube are negligible.) 
 (5 pont)
