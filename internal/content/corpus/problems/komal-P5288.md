The walls of an aquarium are made of $d=12$ mm thick glass of refractive index $n_\text{g}= 3/2$. There is a fish swimming in the water. The refractive index of water is $n_\text{w}= 4/3$. When the fish is observed from outside in the direction which is perpendicular to the wall of the aquarium, at what distance does that point of the fish seem to be which in reality is at a distance of exactly 20 cm from the external surface of the wall?
