The 7.3 km M4 metro line connects Kelenföld railway station and Keleti railway station, and between them it has 8 other stops. Trains leave each station (or stop) with a constant acceleration of 1.0 m/s${}^2$ and and stop with the same deceleration when braking. The maximum speed between stations is 80 km/h. At the stations the average time to change passengers is 0.5 minutes. 
 $a)$ How long does it take for the train to reach its cruising speed when it leaves the station? How far does it travel during this time? 
 $b)$ During one journey, how long does the train travel at the speed of 80 km/h? 
 $c)$ What is the journey time of the M4 metro between the two terminals, i.e. how much time elapses between the departure of a train from Kelenföld and its arrival at Keleti railway station? 
 (4 pont)
