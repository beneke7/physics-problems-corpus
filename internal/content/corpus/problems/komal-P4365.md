In a container there is a sample of air at a pressure of 5$^{.}$10$^{5}$ Pa, and at a temperature of 350 K. The air is compressed at constant pressure to 4/5 of its original volume, while a 1000 J work is done.
 a ) What will the temperature of the air be?
 b ) What is the change in the internal energy of the sample of air?
 c ) What is the density of the compressed air?
