**6. TRUCK (7 pts)**

1) A rope is put over a pole so that the plane of the rope is perpendicular to
the axes of the pole, and the length of that segment of the rope, which touches
the pole is $l$, much shorter than the radius of the pole $R$, see figure (a).
To the one end of the rope, a force $T$ is applied; the sliding of the rope can
be prevented by applying a force $T_{1}$ to the other end of the rope. Express
the ratio $T_{1} / T$ via $l, R$, and $\mu$, where $\mu$ is the coefficient of
friction between the rope and the pole.

2) Answer the first question, if $l$ is not small (i.e. without the assumption
$l \ll R$ ).

**Remark:** you may use equality

$$
\lim _{x \rightarrow 0}(1+n x)^{1 / x}=e^{n} .
$$

3) The rope makes exactly $n=2$ winds around the pole. One end of the rope is
attached to a truck standing on a slope (slanting angle $\phi=10^{\circ}$ );
the mass of the truck $m=20 \mathrm{t}$, see figure (b). Find the force $F$,
needed to apply to the other end of the rope, in order to keep the truck at
rest. Use the numerical value $\mu=0,3$. All the other friction forces acting
upon the truck can be neglected.

4) How does the answer change, if the cross-section of the pole is not circular,
but instead, egg-shaped? Motivate your answer.
