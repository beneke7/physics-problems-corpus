There is an octagon all diagonals of which are resistors of equal resistance $R$; the sides of the octagon are made of an insulating material. Find lower and upper bounds for the resistance between two opposing nodes of such an octagon without exactly calculating its value. Verify the result by calculating this resistance also exactly.
