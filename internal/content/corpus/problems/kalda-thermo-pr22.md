One method for finding relative humidity is based on taking the readings of a dry and a wet thermometer. The wet thermometer has a wet piece of cloth wrapped around its sensor. For the method to yield an accurate result, a wind must blow onto the wet sensor (you can create it using a fan). Throughout this problem, you can use the graph of the pressure of saturated water vapours as a function of temperature.
