**9. SOLENOIDS (8 points)** — *S. Ainsaar*

A tightly-wound rigid solenoid coil has been partly inserted into another. They are connected to a constant current source so that the same current $I$ flows through them; both generate a magnetic field in the same direction. The solenoids have $N$ turns both, their length is $l$, their cross-sectional areas are $A_{1}$ and $A_{2}$. It may be assumed that $A_{1}, A_{2} \ll l^{2}$. You may find it useful to know that the magnetic field at the centre of one solenoid taken separately is $B=\mu_{0} I N / l$, where $\mu_{0}$ is the permeability of vacuum.

i) *(2 points)* The centres of the coils are at a distance $x<l$ along their common axis $\left[A_{1}, A_{2} \ll(x-l)^{2}, x^{2}\right]$. Find the total energy $E_{m}$ of the magnetic field in the system.

ii) *(4 points)* Find the electromotive forces $\mathscr{E}_{1}$ and $\mathscr{E}_{2}$ generated on the coils when one is pulled out with a speed $v$.

iii) *(2 points)* Find the force $F$ needed to pull one coil outwards.
