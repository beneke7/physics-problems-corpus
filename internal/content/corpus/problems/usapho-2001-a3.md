## A3. A plane wave traveling in the positive $z$-direction can be described by the following

wavefunction: $\psi(2, t)=A \sin (k z-\omega t+\phi)$. where $A$ (the amplitude) and $\phi$ are constants, $k=\frac{2 \pi}{\lambda}$, and $\omega=2 \pi f$. The factor $(k z-\omega t+\phi)$ is called the phase of the wave.
(5) a. Two sources, $S_{1}$ and $S_{2}$, produce identical plane waves traveling in the same direction. The sources operate in phase and the waves have the same amplitude, frequency, and wavelength. Source $\mathrm{S}_{1}$ is located a distance $z_{1}$ from point P and source $\mathrm{S}_{2}$ is a distance $z_{2}$ from point P . What is the wavefunction of the resultant wave at point $P$ ? The following relation may be useful:

$$
\sin \alpha+\sin \beta=2 \cos \left[\frac{1}{2}(\alpha-\beta)\right] \sin \left[\frac{1}{2}(\alpha+\beta)\right]
$$

(4) b. For what values of $\Delta z=\left|z_{1}-z_{2}\right|$ is the amplitude of the resultant traveling wave zero?
(4) c. For what values of $\Delta z=\left|z_{1}-z_{2}\right|$ is the amplitude of the resultant traveling wave maximum?
