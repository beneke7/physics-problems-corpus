by Jaan Kalda.

Estimate by the order of magnitude the interaction force between a point charge $q$ and a circular metallic disc of radius $r$ if the charge is at the axis of the disc, and the distance between the disc and the charge is $L \gg r$. The total charge of the disc is $0$ and the thickness of the disc is negligibly small.
