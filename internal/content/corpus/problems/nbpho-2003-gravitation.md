**3. GRAVITATION (6 points)**

1) Find the free falling acceleration $g_{0}$ at the surface of such a spherical planet, which has mass $M$ and material density $\rho$ (in what follows, $M$ and $\rho$ are assumed to be constant).

2) Is it possible that at the surface of a nonspherical planet, there is a point with free falling acceleration $g>g_{0}$? Motivate your answer.

3) For which planet shape is the maximum of the free falling acceleration achieved? The answer can be given in polar coordinates; the expression can contain one unspecified constant.
