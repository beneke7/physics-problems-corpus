## Question 8 - Time-of-flight Thermometry [10]

A cloud of rubidium-87 atoms, with atomic mass $m=1.45 \times 10^{-25} \mathrm{~kg}$, is confined in a monochromatic laser trap with angular frequency $\omega$. The temperature of the gas is $T$.
(a) On dimensional grounds, suggest an expression for the mean square radius $\left\langle R^{2}\right\rangle$ of the atomic cloud.
[1 mark]

The trapping lasers are abruptly switched off at time $t=0$.
(b)(i) Explain why $\left\langle R^{2}\right\rangle(t)$ is expected to evolve in a spatially isotropic fashion, even in the presence of gravity in the vertical direction.
[1 mark]
(b)(ii) On dimensional grounds, suggest an expression for the time-evolution of the mean squared radius of the cloud, $\left\langle R^{2}\right\rangle(t)-\left\langle R^{2}\right\rangle(0)$.
[1 mark]

The results of such an experiment are shown in Tab 8.1.

| $t / \mathrm{ms}$ | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 | 9 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| $\sqrt{\left\langle R^{2}\right\rangle} / \mu \mathrm{m}$ | 464 | 528 | 596 | 670 | 785 | 869 | 1010 | 1105 |

Table 8.1: Experimental data from time-of-flight measurements on a rubidium cloud

(c) Plot an appropriate graph and determine the temperature $T$.
[7 marks]
