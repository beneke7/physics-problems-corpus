19. How could you measure the speed of a pulse, keeping the four variables above constant.
- The measurements of you would made.
- A description of how you would use equipment, such as rules, timers and a reference line to make the measurements.
- An explanation of why you would choose to use the equipment in that way.
- How you would calculate the speed of the pulse from your measurements.

Better answers will include explanations of how you would made sure that your value for the speed was the best you could do.
