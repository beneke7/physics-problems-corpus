There is a uniform density right-triangular cross-section prism of mass $M$ on the horizontal ground. The prism lies on its smallest face, and the surface of the ground is rough enough. The acute angle of the triangle at its vertex which is on the ground is $\alpha=60^\circ$. A thread attached to the midpoint of the top edge of the prism, and a small object of mass $m$ is fixed to the other end of the thread. The bob of the simple pendulum is released from the horizontal position as shown in the figure. The prism is observed to tip when the thread makes an angle of $20^\circ$ with the horizontal. 

 $a)$ What is the mass ratio $M/m$? 
 $b)$ To what value should this ratio $M/m$ be increased in order that the moving pendulum should not be able to make the prism tip at all? 
 (5 pont)
