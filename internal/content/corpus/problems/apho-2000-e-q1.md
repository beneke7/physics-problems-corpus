## Problem 1

## Determination of the Density of Oil

Listed below are the only apparatus and materials available for your experiment :

1. Test tube with uniform cross-section over most of its length between its to ends.
2. Vessel
3. Ruler
4. Eye dropper
5. Graph papers
6. Drying cloth/tissue papers
7. Rubber band for level marking
8. Distilled water with density $1.00 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{cm}^{3}$
9. Oil in a plastic cup

In this experiment, you are to determine the density of the oil without measuring the dimensions of the tube. You should not put both oil and water in the tube at the same time.

Include the following in your report:

a. The theoretical basis for the analysis
b. A description of the method and procedure of the experiment
c. Final value for the density of oil
