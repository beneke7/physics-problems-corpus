A boat can travel at a speed of $3 \mathrm{~m} \mathrm{~s}^{-1}$ on still water. A boatman wants to cross a river whilst covering the shortest possible distance. In what direction should he row with respect to the bank if the speed of the water is (i) $2 \mathrm{~m} \mathrm{~s}^{-1}$, (ii) $4 \mathrm{~m} \mathrm{~s}^{-1}$ ? Assume that the speed of the water is the same everywhere.
