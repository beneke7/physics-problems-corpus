Question 6
Someone analysing an electric circuit obtains the equations

$$
\begin{aligned}
10 \mathrm{~V}-I_{1}(15 \Omega)-I_{3}(5 \Omega) & =0 \\
2 \mathrm{~V}-I_{1}(5 \Omega)-I_{3}(7 \Omega) & =0 \\
I_{1}+I_{3} & =I_{2}
\end{aligned}
$$

Note that V is the unit volts, and $\Omega$ is the unit Ohms. How do the magnitudes of the three currents $I_{1}, I_{2}$ and $I_{3}$ compare?

(A) $\left|I_{3}\right|>\left|I_{1}\right|>\left|I_{2}\right|$
(B) $\left|I_{2}\right|>\left|I_{3}\right|>\left|I_{1}\right|$
(C) $\left|I_{2}\right|>\left|I_{1}\right|>\left|I_{3}\right|$
(D) $\left|I_{1}\right|>\left|I_{3}\right|>\left|I_{2}\right|$
(E) $\left|I_{1}\right|>\left|I_{2}\right|>\left|I_{3}\right|$
