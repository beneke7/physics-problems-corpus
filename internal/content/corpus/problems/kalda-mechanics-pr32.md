An inextensible rough thread with mass per unit length $\rho$ and length $L$ is thrown over a pulley such that the length of one hanging end is $l$. The pulley is comprised of a hoop of mass $m$ and radius $R$ attached to a horizontal axle by light spokes. The initially motionless system is let go. Find the force on the axle immediately after the motion begins. The friction between the pulley and the axle is negligible.
