Two small beads slide without friction, one on each of two long, horizontal, parallel, fixed rods set a distance $d$ apart. The masses of the beads are $m$ and $M$, and they carry respective charges of $q$ and $Q$. Initially, the larger mass $M$ is at rest and the other one is far away approaching it at speed $v_{0}$.
![](../../sources/200ppp/figures/200ppp-026.jpg)

Describe the subsequent motion of the beads.
