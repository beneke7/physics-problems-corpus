In a physics stunt, two balls of equal density, and radii $r$ and $R=2 r$, are placed with the centre of the larger one at the middle of a cart of mass $M=6 \mathrm{~kg}$ and length $L=2 \mathrm{~m}$. The mass of the smaller ball is $m=1 \mathrm{~kg}$. The balls are made to roll, without slipping, in such a way that the larger ball rests on the cart, and a straight line connecting their centres remains at a constant angle $\phi=60^{\circ}$ to the horizontal. The cart is pulled by a horizontal force in the direction shown in the figure.
![](../../sources/200ppp/figures/200ppp-041.jpg)
(i) Find the magnitude of the force $F$.
(ii) How much time elapses before the balls fall off the cart?
