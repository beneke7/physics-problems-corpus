A block with mass $M$ lies on a horizontal slippery surface and also touches a vertical wall. In the upper surface of the block, there is a cavity with the shape of a half-cylinder with radius $r$. A small pellet with mass $m$ is released at the upper edge of the cavity, on the side closer to the wall. What is the maximum velocity of the block during its subsequent motion? Friction can be neglected.
