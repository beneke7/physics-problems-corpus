18. Consider a huge charge reservoir at potential $V$. A spherical capacitor $C_{1}$ is brought in contact with the charge reservoir and then removed. Next another spherical capacitor $C_{2}$ is brought in contact with $C_{1}$ and removed. We repeat this process a large number of times. Assume that potential of reservoir does not change during this exercise. Then the charge on $C_{2}$ after a very long time is
    (a) $C_{2} V$
    (b) $C_{1} V$
    (c) $C_{2} C_{1} V /\left(C_{1}+C_{2}\right)$
    (d) $\left(C_{1}+C_{2}\right) V$
