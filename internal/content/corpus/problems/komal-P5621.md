A concave-convex lens has a power of $-1$ dioptre. What is the radius of curvature of the concave side of the lens if, by holding the lens horizontally with the convex side downwards and pouring water into the concave side, we obtain an optical system with a power of $+1$ dioptre? What is the radius of curvature of the convex side if the lens is made of glass which has a refractive index of $1.6$?
