The bob of a simple pendulum of length $\ell$ is displaced horizontally and then released. When the thread reaches the vertical position, it hits a nail and from that moment onward only the lower part of the thread of length $r$ is moving. 
 What is the ratio of $r/\ell$, if the bob leaves the circular orbit somewhere in its upward motion, and then moving freely it exactly bumps into the nail? 

 (6 pont)
