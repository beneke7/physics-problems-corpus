**2. PLANETS (6 points)**

Two planets move along circular orbits around a star of mass
$M=2.0 \cdot 10^{30}\ \mathrm{kg}$; gravitational constant
$G=6.67 \cdot 10^{-11}\ \mathrm{m}^{3}/\mathrm{kg}\cdot\mathrm{s}^{2}$. The dependence
of the angular distance between a planet and the star on time, as seen from the
other planet, is depicted in the figure.

![figure](../../figures/nbpho/figures/nbpho-2004-planets-fig1.png)

**1)** What is the ratio of the radii of the planets $k$? (2 points)

**2)** Determine the value of the unit on the vertical axis (or express it in terms
of $k$, if you were unable to find it) (2 points).

**3)** What are the orbital radii of the planets, if the unit on the horizontal axis
equals one year? (2 points)
