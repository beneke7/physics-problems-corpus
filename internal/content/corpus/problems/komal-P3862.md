A ray of light enters into a spherical water drop. After refraction it is reflected at the internal boundary of the drop, and then it leaves the drop at another point. a ) Prove (numerically) that if the angle of incidence is greater than a certain ``critical'' angle, then there exists another ray parallel to the original ray, and after leaving the drop the two rays remain also parallel. Find this ``critical'' angle. b ) What is the distance between the two parallel incident rays if the refractive index of water is n =4/3, the angle of incidence of one of the rays is =45$^\circ$ and the diameter of the water drop is 1 mm.
 (5 pont)
