Sun-rays are focused with a lens of diameter $d=10 \mathrm{~cm}$ and focal length of $f=7 \mathrm{~cm}$ to the black side of a thin plate. One side of the plate is perfectly black, and the other side is perfectly white. Angular diameter of the Sun is $\alpha=32^{\prime}$ and its intensity on the surface of the Earth is $I=1000 \mathrm{~W} / \mathrm{m}^{2}$, Stefan-Boltzmann constant $\sigma=5.670 \times 10^{-8} \mathrm{~W} /\left(\mathrm{m}^{2} \mathrm{~K}^{4}\right)$. (i) Find the temperature of the heated point of the plate. (ii) Using thermodynamic arguments, estimate the maximal diameter-to-focal-length ratio of a lens.
