Nitrogen-13 is a radioisotope of Nitrogen. Its half life is 10 minutes. The initial number of the nuclei of the isotope ${}^{13}\rm N$ is $N$ . 
 $a)$ How many nuclei decays during 20 minutes if $N=10^{10}$? 
 $b)$ What can be stated about the number of decaying nuclei during 20 minutes if $N=4$? 
 (5 pont)
