The flux linkage of a circular copper loop of radius $R$ and of cross sectional area $A$, is changing in time as $\Phi(t)=\Phi_0-kt$. The loop is in uniform magnetic field and the magnetic induction is perpendicular to the plane of the loop.
 $a)$ What is the tension in the loop at the moment of $t_0$?
 $b)$ What is the value of the current in the loop at the moment when the magnetic induction is zero?
 Data: $R=$10 cm, $A=0.5~\rm mm^2$, $\Phi_0=0.04$ Vs, $k=5$ mV, $t_0=2$ s.
 (5 pont)
