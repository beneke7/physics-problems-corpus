5. (a) An airplane flying horizontally with a constant speed of $540 \mathrm{~km} \mathrm{~h}^{-1}$ at a height of 1200 m fires a projectile horizontally in its direction of motion at a speed of $u \mathrm{~ms}^{-1}$ relative to the plane. At this instant, a vehicle is at a point on the ground $d$ km horizontally from the airplane. The vehicle is moving with a constant speed of $40 \mathrm{~ms}^{-1}$ on a horizontal road in the direction of motion of the plane. What must be the minimum value of $d$ so that the projectile will hit the vehicle? If, at the instant when the projectile is fired, the value of $d$ is 5 times its minimum value, what is the speed of the projectile when it hits the vehicle? [7 marks]

(b) A satellite is orbiting the Earth 400 km above the equator. Its sense of revolution follows the rotation of the Earth. When the satellite is vertically above a point $P$ on the equator, it will take a photo of the region around $P$. How many photos can the satellite take in 24 hours? [5 marks]
