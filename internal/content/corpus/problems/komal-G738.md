When an earthquake occurs several waves are generated from the centre of the quake. The so called primary waves (P-waves) are the fastest, in our case their propagation speed is 5 km/s. The secondary waves (S-waves) are slower, they travel at a speed of 3 km/h. Two seismic observatories are at a distance of 75 km from each other. In one of them 6 seconds were measured between the detection of the P and S waves, whilst in the other 8 seconds elapsed. At what maximum depth could the centre of the earthquake be?
