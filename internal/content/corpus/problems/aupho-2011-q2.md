Question 2
The speed of light in a vacuum, $c$, depends on two fundamental constants; the permeability of free space, $\mu_{0}$, and the permittivity of free space, $\varepsilon_{0}$. The speed of light is related to these constants by

$$
c=\left(\mu_{0} \varepsilon_{0}\right)^{-1 / 2} .
$$

The SI units of $\varepsilon_{0}$ are $\mathrm{N}^{-1} \mathrm{C}^{2} \mathrm{~m}^{-2}$. Recall that a force of 1 N accelerates a mass of 1 kg at $1 \mathrm{~ms}^{-1}$. The units of $\mu_{0}$ are

a. $\mathrm{kg}^{-1} \mathrm{~m}^{-1} \mathrm{C}^{2}$
b. $\mathrm{kg}^{-1} \mathrm{~s}^{-3} \mathrm{C}^{-2}$
c. $\mathrm{kgms}^{-4} \mathrm{C}^{-2}$
d. $\mathrm{kgmC}^{-2}$
e. $\mathrm{kg}^{2} \mathrm{~m}^{2} \mathrm{C}^{-4}$
