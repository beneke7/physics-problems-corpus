8. A certain amount of steam initially at 130°C is used to warm 200 g of water in a 100-g glass container from 20.0°C to $\theta$. Calculate the mass of steam needed if the temperature
    (i) $\theta=100^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$;
    (ii) $\theta=50^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$.
[9]
You may use the following data:

| Specific heat capacity of glass | $837 \mathrm{Jkg}^{-1} \mathrm{~K}^{-1}$, |
| :--- | :--- |
| Specific heat capacity of steam | $2.01 \times 10^{3} \mathrm{Jkg}^{-1} K^{-1}$, |
| Specific heat capacity of water | $4.19 \times 10^{3} \mathrm{Jkg}^{-1} K^{-1}$, |
| Specific latent heat of vaporization of water | $2.26 \times 10^{6} \mathrm{Jkg}^{-1}$. |
