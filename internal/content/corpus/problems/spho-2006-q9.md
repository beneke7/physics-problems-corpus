9. A beam of monochromatic light, whose wavelength and speed in free space is $\lambda$ and $c$ respectively, is split into two separate beams and each of them is the passed through identical troughs of water of length $L$. If the water in one trough is stationary and the water in the other trough is moving with speed $\nu(\ll c)$ in the direction of the light, determine the phase difference in terms of $L, \lambda, \nu, c$ and $n$, where $n$ is the refractive index of the stationary water. [8 marks]
