The focal length of a camera is 50 mm, the diameter of the lens is 20 mm. The lens is adjusted such that it forms the sharp image of an object which is at a distance of 5 m from the lens. What is the smallest and greatest distance of a point-like object from the film, between which the image formed on the film is smaller than a spot of diameter 0.05 mm? How will this interval change if the diameter of the lens is decreased to 10 mm?
