Two motorcycles are travelling behind each other at a speed of $30~\text{m/s}$ on a racetrack, the distance between them is $23~\text{m}$, and the length of the motorcycles is $2~\text{m}$. At some point, the motorcyclist at the back starts overtaking the other, he accelerates at $1~\text{m}/\text{s}^2$ until he is $23~\text{m}$ ahead the other, and then continues at a constant speed. At the moment he finishes overtaking, the other rider starts overtaking him at an acceleration of $1~\text{m}/\text{s}^2$, which he also finishes $23~\text{m}$ ahead the other motorcyclist. What are the speeds of the motorcyclists after this double overtaking?
 (4 pont)
