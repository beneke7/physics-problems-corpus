A 5 dioptre converging lens and a concave mirror of radius 40 cm are at a distance of 40 cm. The two optical devices have the same principal axis. The mirror is facing towards the lens with its reflecting side. There is a small beetle 30 cm away from the lens on the side which is opposite to the mirror.
 What type of image - or images - are formed about the beetle by the optical system? What is the magnification?
 (4 pont)
