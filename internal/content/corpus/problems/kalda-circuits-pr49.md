There is a decagon all sides and all diagonals of which are resistors of equal resistance $R$; let $A$ and $C$ denote the two neighbours of a vertex $B$, and let $D$ be a vertex which is not neighbouring any of the three mentioned vertices. The wires corresponding to the sides $A B$ and $B C$ are cut off. Determine the resistance between $A$ and $D$.
