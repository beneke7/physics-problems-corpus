Q8
    a) A satellite of mass $m=500 \mathrm{~kg}$ is in a circular orbit at an altitude of $l=600 \mathrm{~km}$ above the Earth's surface . The satellite returns to Earth as a result of the frictional forces and impacts with a speed of $v=2.00 \mathrm{kms}^{-1}$. How much energy was absorbed by the atmosphere?
    b) A rocket, mass $M_{R}$, is launched from the Earth. Its motors are used only near the Earth's surface in order to give the rocket just sufficient energy to reach the Moon. Once the fuel is exhausted the rocket has initial velocity $v$. Assume no relative motion of the Earth - Moon system.

Determine:


(i) the distance from the Earth's centre, $d_{E}$, at which the velocity of the rocket is least.
(ii) the initial velocity of the rocket, $v$, justifying any approximations you may make.
