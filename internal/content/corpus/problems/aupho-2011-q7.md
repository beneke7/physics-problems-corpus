Question 7
A spherical drop of mercury with charge $8 Q$ splits into eight identical spherical droplets, each with the same charge and radius. The electrostatic potential energy of a conducting sphere of radius $r$ and charge $q$ is $q^{2} / 8 \pi \varepsilon_{0} r$, where $\varepsilon_{0}$ is a constant. After the droplets are separated far apart so that they no longer interact, the percentage of the initial electrical energy that has been converted into other forms of energy is

a. 0\%
b. 12.5\%
c. 25\%
d. 50\%
e. 75\%
