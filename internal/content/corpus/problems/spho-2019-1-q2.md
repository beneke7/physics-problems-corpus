2. (a) A mass of 0.25 kg is attached to an unstretched spring that has a force constant of $20 \mathrm{Nm}^{-1}$. The mass is released and oscillates with ever decreasing amplitude and eventually comes to rest. The process is irreversible. Assume that the temperature of the surrounding remains constant at 27°C, calculate the entropy change of the surroundings. [4 marks]
