A 'twisted' circular band (called a Moebius strip) is made from a strip of paper of length $L$ and width $d$. A wire running along the edge of the strip is connected to a voltmeter, as shown in the figure.
![](../../../../../sources/200ppp/figures/200ppp-082.jpg)

What does the voltmeter register when the strip is placed in a homogeneous magnetic field which is perpendicular to the plane of the strip and changes uniformly with time, i.e. $B(t)=k t$ ?
