In a thermally insulated cylinder an easily moveable thermally insulated piston separates two samples of air of volumes of 12 l and 8 l at standard condition. There is a heating element in the 8 l volume part, and the temperature of the gas in it is increased to 200 $^\circ$C. 

 a ) What are the volumes and the pressures of the two samples of gas at the end of the process? 
 b ) What will the temperature of the gas in the originally 12 l part be at the end of the process? 
 c ) How much heat is absorbed by the gas which is heated? 
 d ) By what amount do the internal energies of the two samples of gas change? 
 (5 pont)
