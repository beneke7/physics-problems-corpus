A small comet of mass $m$, which can be considered point-like, approaches a spherical planet of mass $M$, and of radius $R$ ($m\ll M$). The speed of the comet very far from the planet is $v_0$, and if the gravitational field of the planet did not exert any force on the comet, it would pass the planet at a distance of $d$ from the centre of the planet ($d>R$). What is the minimum value of $v_0$, at which the comet does not hit the planet? (Apart from the planet and the comet, the gravitational fields of any other celestial objects can be neglected.) 
 (5 pont)
