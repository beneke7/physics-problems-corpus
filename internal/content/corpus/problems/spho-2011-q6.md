6. An aluminum rod 0.500 m in length and with a cross-sectional area of $2.50 \mathrm{~cm}^{2}$ is inserted into a thermally insulated vessel containing liquid helium at 4.20 K . The rod is initially at 300 K .
(i) If half of the rod is inserted into the helium, how many liters of helium boil off by the time the inserted half cools to 4.20 K ? (Assume the upper half does not yet cool.)
(ii) If the upper end of the rod is maintained at 300 K , what is the approximate boil-off rate of liquid helium after the lower half has reached 4.20 K ? (Aluminum has thermal conductivity of $31.0 \mathrm{~J} \mathrm{~s}^{-1} \mathrm{~cm}^{-1} \mathrm{~K}^{-1}$ at 4.2 K . You may ignore its temperature variation. Note that aluminum has a specific heat capacity of $902 \mathrm{~J} \mathrm{~kg}^{-1} \mathrm{~K}^{-1}$ and density of $2700 \mathrm{~kg} \mathrm{~m}^{-3}$. The density of liquid helium is $125 \mathrm{~kg} \mathrm{~m}^{-3}$.)
