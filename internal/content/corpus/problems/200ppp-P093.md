Boron atoms of mass number $A=10$ and a beam of unidentified particles, moving in opposite directions with the same (non-relativistic) speed, are made to collide inside an ion accelerator. The maximum scattering angle of the boron atoms is found to be $30^{\circ}$. What kind of atoms does the particle beam consist of?
