A beam of protons is aimed at a target, which is at rest. If the kinetic energy of the protons in the beam is greater than a critical value of $E_\text{crit}$, then the incident protons may generate positive pions ($\pi^+$) by colliding the protons of the target, which can be considered being at rest, accordingly the following equation: 
 $\mathrm{p}^+ +\mathrm{p}^+ \Rightarrow\mathrm{p}^+ +\mathrm{n}^0 +\pi^+.
$ 
 Determine the value of $E_\text{crit}$ in MeV. 
 Available data: the rest energy of a proton is 938.27 MeV, the rest energy of a neutron is 939.57 MeV and the rest energy of a pion is 139.57 MeV. 
 (5 pont)
