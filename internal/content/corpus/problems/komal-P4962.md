There are two cylinder-shaped wooden billets, each having a mass of mass 45 kg, in a vertical wall sewage, which contains water in it. The two billets have the same size and the same material; they touch each other and the walls of the sewage. One of them is totally under the water, whilst only half of the other one is immersed into the water. Friction is negligible everywhere.

 $a)$ What is the density of the wood?
 $b)$ What are the forces exerted by the billets on the vertical walls?
 (4 pont)
