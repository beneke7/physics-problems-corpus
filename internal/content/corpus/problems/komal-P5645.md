A motorcyclist drives at 36 km/h into a semi-circular ``reverse'' curve. The coefficient of static friction between the asphalt and the wheels is $0.58$. The motorcyclist keeps his vehicle (more precisely, the centre of gravity of the motorcycle and the biker) on a circular arc of radius 40 m, while increasing its speed uniformly.
 a)  At most by how many m/s can the motorcyclist increase his speed?
 b)  To what speed can the rider accelerate at the end of the turn?
 c)  How does the rider's angle with the vertical vary in the semi-circular turn?
 (5 pont)
