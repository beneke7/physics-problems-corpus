There is a plane mirror opposite to a concave mirror of focal length of $f$. The plane mirror is perpendicular to the principal axis of the concave mirror and the two mirrors are at a distance of $d$ from each other (measured along the principal axis). Where should a point-like light source $T$ be placed in order that the light rays emerging from it and reflected in the concave mirror and then in the plane mirror form an image at the position of the light source, and also the rays which are reflected in the plane mirror and then in the concave mirror form an image at the same point? Under what conditions can this happen?
