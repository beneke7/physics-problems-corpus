A block with mass $M$ is on a slippery horizontal surface. A thread extends over one of its corners. The thread is attached to the wall at its one end and to a little block of mass $m$, which is inclined by an angle $\alpha$ with respect to the vertical, at the other. Initially the thread is stretched and the blocks are held in place. Then the blocks are released. For which ratio of masses will the angle $\alpha$ remain unchanged throughout the subsequent motion?
