Certain room is being heated using a heating device the output power $P(T)$ of which depends on the room temperature $T$ as shown in figure. If the outside temperature is $T_{1}$ then the room temperature will reach value $T_{2}$ (see figure). Which room temperature will be reached if the outside temperature is $T_{3}$ ? (find the solution graphically using the figure). The heat exchange loss rate of the room is proportional to the difference of the inside and outside temperatures.
