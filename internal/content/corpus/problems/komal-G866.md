A thin, double-bent tube is made from three long enough pieces. The middle section is horizontal, the first section makes an angle of $30^\circ$ with the horizontal, and the third section makes an angle of $45^\circ$ with the horizontal. The sections lie in the same vertical plane, with short, smooth bends connecting each section. At the left end of the tube, a tap seals off enough air, such that there is a mercury thread, of length $L$, at rest in the bottom of the left section of the tube (as shown in the figure ). The tap is suddenly opened, causing the mercury thread to move without friction. Initially, the bottom of the mercury thread is at the junction of the first and second tube sections.
 What is the maximum fraction of the mercury thread that enters the third section of the tube?
