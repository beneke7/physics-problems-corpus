Pistons encloses samples of nitrogen gas in a vertical, insulated cylinder sealed at its bottom. Initially the samples have the same volume and the same temperature of $T_1=300~\mathrm{K}$. The upper piston can move frictionlessly, has a mass of ${m=40}~\mathrm{kg}$, and is thermally insulated, the lower one is made of good thermal conducting material and is fixed. The external air pressure is $10^5~\mathrm{Pa}$, the pressure of the gas at the bottom part is $1.2\cdot 10^5~\mathrm{Pa}$. The area of the cross section of the cylinder is ${A=1}~\mathrm{dm^2}$, $h=0.5~\mathrm{m}$. 

 $a)$ What are the masses of the samples of nitrogen in the bottom and in the top parts of the cylinder? 
 An electric heating element gives $Q=1580~\mathrm{J}$ thermal energy to the system. 
 $b)$ By how much does the internal energy of the gases in the top and bottom parts change? 
 $c)$ How much does the volume of the upper gas change?
