## Question 8.

Lara has her baby weighed at an early childhood centre every four weeks. The uncertainty in the measurement is 50g. At four weeks old the baby's weight is measured as 3.20 kg. At eight weeks old the baby's weight is measured as 4.05 kg. The baby's weight gain in this four week period is therefore:

A) $0.85 \mathrm{~kg} \pm 0.025 \mathrm{~kg}$
B) $0.85 \mathrm{~kg} \pm 0.05 \mathrm{~kg}$
C) $0.85 \mathrm{~kg} \pm 0.1 \mathrm{~kg}$
D) $0.9 \mathrm{~kg} \pm 0.1 \mathrm{~kg}$
E) $0.90 \mathrm{~kg} \pm 0.10 \mathrm{~kg}$
