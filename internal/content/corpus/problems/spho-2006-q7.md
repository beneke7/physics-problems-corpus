7. A bicycle dynamo consists of a small permanent magnet which is fixed at its center to the axle of one of the bicycle wheels, which itself has radius $r$. Flux from the magnet links (via an iron yoke) a coil of self-inductance $L$, the coil being connected to a lamp of resistance $R$. The flux $\Phi$ linking the coil can be approximated as a sinusoidally varying quantity of the form $\Phi_{0} \cos (\omega t)$.
    (i) Establish the equations satisfied by $I_{0}$ and $\epsilon$, if the current in the circuit is $I_{0} \cos (\omega t+\epsilon)$, and show that $\tan \epsilon=\frac{R r}{v L}$, where $v$ is the road-speed of the bicycle. [8 marks]
    (ii) Find how the power $P$ delivered to the lamp varies with $v$, and show that it cannot exceed $\frac{R \Phi_{0}^{2}}{2 L^{2}}$. [5 marks]
