Measure how long it takes for a single pulse initiated at one end of a slinky, which is stretched along a horizontal tabletop, to travel back. Examine how the speed of the pulse depends on the previously fixed length of the slinky. Initiate both transverse and longitudinal short pulses.
 (6 pont)
