3. The van der Waals equation for a gas is given by
$$
\left(p+a \frac{N^{2}}{V^{2}}\right)(V-b N)=N k_{B} T
$$
where $p, V, T$ are the pressure, volume, and temperature of the gas respectively. $N$ is the number of molecules of the gas, and $k_{B}$ is Boltzmann's constant. The parameters $a, b$ are allowed to be gas-dependent and assumed to be positive.
    (a) Show that at constant temperature, the condition that $d p / d V$ vanishes gives solutions that satisfy
$$
V-b N=\left(\frac{k_{B} T}{2 N a}\right)^{1 / 2} V^{3 / 2} .
$$
State an assumption made about the solutions.
    (b) By sketching appropriate graphs, show that there are either zero, one, or two solutions to this equation.
(c) Find expressions for $V=V_{0}$ and $T=T_{0}$ in the case that there is only one solution.
(d) Sketch a few isothermal curves on a $p-V$ diagram for temperatures below, at, and above $T_{0}$.
