A room has three doors, and next to each door there is a switch. With each of them the chandelier in the room can be switched on or off separately. In order to build such a circuit, multiway switches, namely 3-way and 4-way switches should be used. How can this circuit be built? Look up in literature how these switches work, and give the diagram of their connections.
 (4 pont)
