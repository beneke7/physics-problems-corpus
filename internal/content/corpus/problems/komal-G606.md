The heat capacity of a calorimeter is to be measured, therefore 65 g water at a temperature of $45~{}^\circ$C is poured into the calorimeter, which has already contained for a long time 150 g water at a temperature of $17~{}^\circ$C. The final temperature of the mixture is $25~{}^\circ$C. What is the heat capacity of the calorimeter?
