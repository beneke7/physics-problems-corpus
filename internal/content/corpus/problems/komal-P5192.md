A mercury column confines an air column of height 40 cm and of temperature 300 K in a U-shaped tube, one end of which is closed, and which has a cross sectional area of $1~\mathrm{cm}^2$, as it is shown in the figure. The external air pressure is the same as the gauge pressure of a 76 cm high mercury column.

 Keeping the temperature of the enclosed gas constant, some mercury of volume $V_0$ is poured into the tube such that the mercury levels in the two arms of the tube will be the same. Then if the enclosed air is heated we can observe that its pressure is proportional to its volume at any temperature. What is the volume $V_0$?
 (5 pont)
