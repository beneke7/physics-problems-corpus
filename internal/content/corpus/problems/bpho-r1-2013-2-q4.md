Q4.
Two identical balls, A and B, each of mass $m$, undergo a collision. Initially B is stationary and A has velocity $u_{0}$. After the collision A has velocity $v_{A}$ and B has velocity $v_{B}$.

(a) For an elastic collision along a straight line, determine the motion of the masses after impact.
(b) In an inelastic, two dimensional, collision, A has an initial kinetic energy of 8.00 J and 2.00 J is converted into heat on collision. After impact the directions of A and B make equal angles, $\theta$, with the direction of $u_{0}$.
Determine:
    (i) the energy conservation equation
    (ii) the momentum conservation equation/s
    (iii) $\theta$
    (iv) the change in momentum of A.
