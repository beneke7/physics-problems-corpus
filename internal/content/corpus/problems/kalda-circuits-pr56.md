Consider a tunnel diode which is connected in series with a resistance $R$ to a battery (for a typical $V-I$-curve of a tunnel diode, see problem 25). Let the parameters of the system be such that there is a stationary state at such diode voltage that diode's differential resistance $R_{\mathrm{diff}} \equiv \frac{\mathrm{d} V}{\mathrm{~d} I}$ is negative. Under which condition will this state be stable?
