On a horizontal frictionless table a stick of mass M , length of L and of homogeneous mass distribution is standing.

 a ) With what velocity had a small body of mass m slid into it, if the stick hit the table after a turn of an angle ?

 b ) How far was the mass center of the stick from the small body at this moment?

 (The collision of the small body and the stick is perfectly elastic.) (Data: M =0.6 kg, m =0.2 kg, L =0.8 m, =60$^\circ$.)

 (5 pont)
