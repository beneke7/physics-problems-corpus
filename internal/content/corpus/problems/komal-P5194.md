Consider two metal spheres of the same size, which is small compared to the 0.2 m distance between them. The two spheres are charged differently and they attract each other with a force of 1.2 N. The spheres are made to touch each other and then placed back to their original position. Now they repel each other, but the magnitude of this repulsive force is the same as the magnitude of the attractive force in the first case. What was the initial charge of the spheres? 
 (4 pont)
