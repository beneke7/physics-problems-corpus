Two small insulating discs of mass m are placed to the horizontal tabletop, such that initially the distance between their centres is x $_{0}$=5 cm. A small metal sphere of charge Q =8$^{.}$10$^{-8}$ C is fixed at the top of each disc. 

 One of the discs if fixed to the tabletop and the other is released, and it slides. The coefficient of friction between the disc and the tabletop is = $_{0}$=0.3. Neglect the electrostatic interaction between the charges and the tabletop. 
 a ) What will the distance  x $_{1}$ be between the centres of the two discs at the moment when the speed of the sliding disc is maximal? 
 b ) What will the distance  x $_{m}$ between the two discs be after the sliding discs stops? 
 c ) What is the relationship between x $_{0}$, x $_{1}$ and x $_{m}$? 
 (5 pont)
