The asymmetrical U-shaped tube shown in the figure has a cross-sectional area of $2~\mathrm{cm}^2$, and it is filled with mercury. The top of the tube at the left is sealed, thus in this arm, there will be an air column of height 10 cm, at a pressure of 1 atm. How many cm${}^3$ of mercury should be filled slowly and carefully into the right arm, in order to decrease the level of mercury in the right arm to half of the original height?
