Points $A, B$ and $C$ lie on a straight line, so that $B$ is situated between $A$ and $C$. Three bodies $a, b$ and $c$ start from these points, moving at constant (but different) velocities. It is known that i) if $c$ was missing, $a$ and $b$ would collide and ii) if $b$ was missing, $a$ and $c$ would collide and it would happen before than in i). Would $b$ and $c$ collide if $a$ was missing?
