Air (a mixture of oxygen and nitrogen) is stored in a closed container equipped with a piston on one end at a temperature of $T=77.4 \mathrm{~K}$. The total amount of the gas mixture occupies volume $V_{0}=1.00 \mathrm{l}$ and its initial pressure is $p_{0}=0.500 \mathrm{~atm}$. With the help of the piston the gas mixture is slowly compressed at constant temperature. Using plausible assumptions, plot the pressure of the system as a function of its volume until one tenth of the initial volume, if the ratio of the number of moles of oxygen to the number of moles of nitrogen is $n_{\mathrm{O} 2}: n_{\mathrm{N} 2}=21: 79$. Find the pressure and volume at distinctive points of these isothermal curves.
