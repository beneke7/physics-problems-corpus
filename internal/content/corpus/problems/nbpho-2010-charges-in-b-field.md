**6. CHARGES IN B (5 points)**

There is a homogeneous, parallel to the $z$-axis magnetic field of inductance $B$ in region $x > 0$. In region $x < 0$, there is no field. There are two particles of mass $m$ and charge $q$. Initially, the particles have coordinates $y = z = 0$, and respectively $x = -L_0$ and $x = -2L_0$ (with $L_0 > 0$). Initial velocity of both particles is $v$, along the $x$-axis, towards the magnetic field. Neglect the electrostatic repulsion force of the two charges.

**i)** *(1.5 pt)* Sketch the trajectory of the first particle, and the dependence of its $y$-coordinate on time.

**ii)** *(3.5 pt)* Sketch the distance $L$ between the particles as a function of time $t$, assuming that $\pi m v / (Bq) > L_0$. What is the minimal distance $L_{\mathrm{min}}$?
