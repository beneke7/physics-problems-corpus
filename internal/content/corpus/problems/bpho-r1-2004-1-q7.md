Q7.
a) The relativistic energy $E$ of an electron with momentum $p$ is given by

$$
E^{2}=p^{2} c^{2}+m_{e}^{2} c^{4}
$$

(i) What is understood by the rest energy of the electron?
(ii) Deduce an expression for the kinetic energy, $T$, of the electron.
b) A photon of frequency $f$ is scattered through an angle of $90^{\circ}$ by an initially stationary electron. After the collision the photon has a frequency $f^{\prime}$. The electron acquires relativistic momentum $p$ directed at an angle $\theta$ to the incident photon.
(i) Write down the conservation equations for this collision.
(ii) Prove that

$$
c^{2} p^{2}=h^{2}\left(f^{2}+f^{\prime 2}\right)
$$

and

$$
f^{\prime}=\left(1+\frac{h f}{m_{e} c^{2}}\right)^{-1} f .
$$

Momentum of a photon, frequency $f$, is $h f / c$.
c) What can be deduced about $f^{\prime}, \theta$ and $p$ when $h f \ll m_{e} c^{2}$ ?

Useful relationship: $\sin ^{2} \theta+\cos ^{2} \theta=1$.
