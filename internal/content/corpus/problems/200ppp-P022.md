Two (non-physics) students, $A$ and $B$, living in neighbouring college rooms, decided to economise by connecting their ceiling lights in series. They agreed that each would install a $100-\mathrm{W}$ bulb in their own rooms and that they would pay equal shares of the electricity bill. However, both decided to try to get better lighting at the other's expense; $A$ installed a $200-\mathrm{W}$ bulb and $B$ installed a $50-\mathrm{W}$ bulb. Which student subsequently failed the end-of-term examinations?
