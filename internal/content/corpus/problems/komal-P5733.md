A boy practices free throws on a basketball court. The basket is located horizontally at a distance of $d=4~\mathrm{m}$ and vertically $H=1~\mathrm{m}$ higher than the release point. The initial velocity of the thrown ball is $v_0=10~\mathrm{m}/\mathrm{s}$. 
 a)  At what angle should the boy throw the ball so that it goes cleanly into the basket? 
 b)  What are the minimum and maximum values of $v_0$ for which the shot is possible, taking into account that the ceiling is located 6 m above the release position of the ball? Assume that the ball and the basket (i.e. the centres of the ball and the rim) are point-like, and neglect air resistance. 
 (5 pont)
