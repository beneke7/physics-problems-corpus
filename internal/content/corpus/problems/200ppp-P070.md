A tanker full of liquid is parked at rest on a horizontal road. The brake has not been applied, and it may be supposed that the tanker can move without friction.
![](../../sources/200ppp/figures/200ppp-025.jpg)

In which direction will the tanker move after the tap on the vertical outlet pipe, which is situated at the rear of the tanker, has been opened? Will the tanker continue to move in this direction?
