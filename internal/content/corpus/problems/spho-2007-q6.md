6. A circular coil has inner radius $\mathrm{R}_{1}$ and an outer radius $\mathrm{R}_{2}$. The length of the coil is L.
    (i) Show that the magnetic induction B at the center when the coil carries a current I is
$$
B=\frac{\mu_{0} n I L}{2} \ln \left[\frac{a+\sqrt{a^{2}+b^{2}}}{1+\sqrt{1+b^{2}}}\right]
$$
where $\mathrm{a}=\mathrm{R}_{2} / \mathrm{R}_{1}, \mathrm{~b}=\mathrm{L} / 2 \mathrm{R}_{1}$ and $n$ is the number of turns per square meter. [10 marks]
    (ii) Show that the length of the wire is $2 \pi n\left(a^{2}-1\right) b R_{1}^{3}$.
