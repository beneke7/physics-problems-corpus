There is a long chute of constant slope angle along which balls can slide frictionlessly (the chute is narrow so that the motion of the balls is essentially one-dimensional). Let there be $N$ identical perfectly elastic balls sliding on that chute. The total number of pair-wise collisions between the balls in the chute depends on their initial velocities and positions. What is the largest possible number of collisions? (If you don't know how will move two absolutely elastic balls after a collision, check the hints section.)
