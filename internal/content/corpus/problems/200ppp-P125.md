The refractive index of the medium within a certain region, $x>0$, $y>0$, changes with $y$. A thin light ray travelling in the $x$-direction strikes the medium at right angles and moves through the medium along a circular arc.
![](../../sources/200ppp/figures/200ppp-059.jpg)

How does the refractive index depend on $y$ ? What is the maximum possible angular size of the arc?
