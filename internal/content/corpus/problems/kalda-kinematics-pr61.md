A motorboat approaches a straight coastline perpendicularly, and at a distance $L$ starts turning back by drawing a half-circle of radius $R$; later it departs perpendicularly to the coastline. The speed of the boat is constant and equal to $v$, the water wave speed can be assumed to be constant and equal to $u$ (with $u<v$ ). How long will it take for the waves of the wake behind the boat to reach the coast (as measured from the moment when the boat starts turning)?
