Thermal atmospheric escape is a process in which small gas molecules reach speeds high enough to escape the gravitational field of the Earth and reach outer space. This process, known as Jeans escape, is believed to have been responsible for the loss of water from Venus and Mars atmospheres, due to their lower escape velocity.
