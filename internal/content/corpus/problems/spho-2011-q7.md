7. A soap film ( $n=1.33$ ) is contained within a rectangular wire frame. The frame is held vertically so that the film drains downward and forms a wedge with flat faces. The thickness of the film at the top is essentially zero. The film is viewed in reflected white light with near-normal incidence, and the first violet (the wavelength is 420 nm ) interference band is observed 3.00 cm from the top edge of the film.
(i) Locate the first red ( $\lambda=680 \mathrm{~nm}$ ) interference band.
(ii) Determine the film thickness at the positions of the violet and red bands.
(iii) What is the wedge angle of the film?
