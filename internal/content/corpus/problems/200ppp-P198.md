Classically, the Coulomb field of an atomic nucleus could confine an electron to that nucleus. However, the Heisenberg uncertainty principle prescribes such a high kinetic energy for an electron enclosed in such a small space that it would escape from the nucleus in any case. What would be the atomic number of a transuranic element able to confine an electron within its nucleus for a significant time, if only the element itself were sufficiently stable?
