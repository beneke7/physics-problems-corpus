Two boards have been placed at right angles to one another. Their line of touching is horizontal and one of them (A) makes an angle $\alpha$ to the horizontal. An elastic ball is released at a point at distance $a$ from plane $A$ and $b$ from $B$. On the average, how many times does the ball bounce against wall $B$ for each time it bounces against wall $A$ ? Collisions are absolutely elastic.
