4. A man stands on a long plane concrete runway above which a uniform vertical temperature gradient results in a uniform gradient in the refractive index of the air, $n(x, y)=n_{0}(1+\alpha y)$, where $|\alpha|=1.5 \times 10^{-6} \mathrm{~m}^{-1}$. As a result he cannot see the runway $(y=0)$ beyond a certain distance $d$. If his eyes are 1.7 m above the runway, find the value of $d$. Does the temperature rise or fall with increasing height?
