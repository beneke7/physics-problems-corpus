In a swimming pool only the lights built in the vertical walls of the pool are turned on as dusk falls. The lights are 1 metre below the surface of the water. Someone stands 3 metres from the wall in front of a lamp such that his or her eyes are at a height of 30 cm above the water. How far from the wall does he or she observe a light spot on the surface of the water?
