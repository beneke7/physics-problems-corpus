A small charged bead of mass m and of charge q can move on a frictionless horizontal rod. A pointlike charge Q whose charge is opposite the charge on the bead, is fixed at a point which is at a distance of d from the rod. The bead is a bit displaced from its equilibrium position and then released. Find the period of the resulted oscillatory motion.
 Data: Q =10$^{-8}$ C, q =-10$^{-8}$ C, m =1 g, d =10 cm.
 (5 pont)
