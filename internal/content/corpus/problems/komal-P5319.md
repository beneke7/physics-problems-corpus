A uniform-density thin stick of length $\ell$ and of mass $m$ is made slide along a horizontal surface. At a certain moment the velocity of one end of the stick is $\boldsymbol v_1$, whilst that of the other end is $\boldsymbol v_2$. At this moment what is the
 $a)$ linear momentum of the stick;
 $b)$ angular momentum of the stick with respect to its centre of mass;
 $c)$ its total kinetic energy?
