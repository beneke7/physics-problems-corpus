A metallic sphere of radius 2 mm and density $\rho= 3000 \mathrm{~kg} / \mathrm{m}^{3}$ is moving in water, falling freely with the acceleration $a_{0}=0,57 \mathrm{~g}$. The water density is $\rho_{0}=1000 \mathrm{~kg} / \mathrm{m}^{3}$. With what acceleration would a spherical bubble of radius 1 mm rise in the water? Consider the flow to be laminar in both cases; neglect friction.
