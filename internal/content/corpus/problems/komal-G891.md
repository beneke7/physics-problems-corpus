A domino-shaped block is made from some low-conducting material. The size of the block is: $0.5\,\mathrm{cm}\times 3\,\mathrm{cm}\times 6\,\mathrm{cm}$. The resistance of the domino is measured so that current flows between the opposite faces of the domino. The connections are made such that the current density in the domino is uniform. The voltages are chosen so that the magnitude of the current density is the same in all three cases.
 a) What is the ratio of the three measured resistances?
 b) What is the ratio of the applied three voltage values?
 c) What is the ratio of the dissipated energy per second due to Joule heating in the three cases.
 (4 pont)
