A pointlike discharge lamp of power 5 W emits monochromatic light of wavelength 425 nm. The cathode of a photocell is placed 1 m from the light source, perpendicularly to the light rays. The radius of the cathode is 0.5 cm and the work function of its material is 2$^{.}$10$^{-19}$ J. A capacitor of capacitance 2 nF is connected to the anode and the cathode of the photocell. 
 a ) What is the energy of one photon emitted by the discharge lamp? How many photons hits the cathode of the photocell in each second? 
 b ) What is the maximum of the speed of the electrons which leaves the cathode? 
 c ) What will the voltage of the capacitor be? 
 (5 pont)
