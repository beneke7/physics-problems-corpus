## Problem 5. Diffraction grating (7 points)

Determine the pitch (the distance between the neighbouring lines) of the reflecting diffraction grating; estimate the uncertainty of the result. Equipment: a reflecting diffraction grating, green laser $(\lambda=532 \mathrm{~nm})$, ruler, a stand for holding the laser.
