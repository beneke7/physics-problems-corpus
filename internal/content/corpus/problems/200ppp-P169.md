A resistor and an inductor in series are connected to a battery through a switch.

![](../../../../../sources/200ppp/figures/200ppp-075.jpg)
$v$

After the switch has been closed:
(i) What is the magnitude of the current flowing when the rate of the increase of magnetic energy stored in the coil is at a maximum?
(ii) When will the Joule heat dissipated in the resistor change at the fastest rate?
