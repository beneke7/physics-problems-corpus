4. (a) The distance between the objective lens and the eyepiece of an astronomical telescope in its normal adjustment, i.e., the final image is formed at infinity, is 105 cm. When the eyepiece is moved 1.0 cm towards the objective lens, a final virtual image is formed at a point 20 cm from the eyepiece. Calculate the focal lengths of the objective lens and of the eyepiece.
[3 marks]

4. (b) The intensity of solar radiation at the surface of the Earth is $1.38 \mathrm{~kW} \mathrm{~m}^{-2}$. The orbital radii of Earth and Mars are respectively $1.496 \times 10^{8} \mathrm{~km}$ and $2.280 \times 10^{8} \mathrm{~km}$. Estimate the surface temperature of Mars assuming that both planets behave like blackbodies.
[6 marks]
