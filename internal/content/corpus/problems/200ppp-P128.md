A parallel, thin, monochromatic laser beam falls on a diffraction grating at normal incidence. How does the interference pattern it produces on a viewing screen change if the grating is rotated through an angle $\phi<90^{\circ}$ around an axis, which is
(i) parallel to the lines of the grating; or
(ii) perpendicular to the lines of the grating?
