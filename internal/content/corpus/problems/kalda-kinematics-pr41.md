A uniform rope has been manufactured from an explosive material, combustion travels along the rope at velocity $v$. The velocity of a shock wave in the air is $c$, with $c<v$. Along which curve should the rope be laid out to make the shock wave reach a given point at the same time from all points of the rope? (Finding a quantitative formula for the shape requires solving a very simple differential equation.)
