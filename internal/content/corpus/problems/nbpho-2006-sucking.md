**3. SUCKING (7 points)**

Let a large vessel be filled with an incompressible dielectric liquid of mass density $\rho_{m}$ (the relative dielectric permeability $\varepsilon \approx 1$). This liquid carries a homogeneous volume charge density $\rho_{e}$ which is so small that the electric field $E_{0}$ created by it is negligible: $E_{0} \rho_{e} \ll g \rho_{m}$, where $g$ is the free-fall acceleration. Surface tension can also be neglected. All heights will be measured from the unperturbed height of the liquid surface. A point charge $-q$ of opposite sign is brought to height $H$, due to which a kink is formed on the liquid surface.

**1)** Determine the height of the kink $a$.

**2)** If the height of the charge is slowly decreased, at which height $h$ will the liquid start flowing towards the point charge?
