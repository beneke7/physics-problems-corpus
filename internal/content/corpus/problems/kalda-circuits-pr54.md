A wheel circuit is a circuit which can be drawn as a regular $n$-gon so that the rim of the "wheel" is formed by $n$ resitors of resistance $R$ connecting neighboring vertices of the $n$-gon, and the "spokes" of the "wheel" are formed by $n$ resistors of resistance $r$ connecting the centre of the "wheel" with each of the vertices. Let $R_{1}$ be the resistance between two neighbouring vertices of such "wheel", and $R_{2}$ - the resistance between one of the vertices and the centre. Express $R_{1}$ in terms of $R_{2}, R$ and $r$ (without using $n$ ).
