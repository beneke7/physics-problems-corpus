9. Stratostat (5 points)
i) Show that the pressure of an isothermal gas of molar mass $\mu$ follows the law $p=p_{0} e^{-\alpha z}$, where $p_{0}$ is the pressure at the origin, and $z$ is the height. Find the constant $\alpha$. The temperature is $T$, the free fall acceleration is $g$.
ii) Consider a stratostat, the envelope of which (a freely deformable non-elastic sack) is filled at the Earth's surface by helium to the volume fraction of $\beta=10 \%$. At which height $h$ does the helium expand so that it fills the entire volume of the stratostat? The molar masses of the air and helium are $\mu_{a}=$ $29 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{mol}$ and $\mu_{H e}=4 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{mol}$, respectively. You may neglect the temperature variations of the atmosphere, and use the value $T=250 \mathrm{~K}$.
