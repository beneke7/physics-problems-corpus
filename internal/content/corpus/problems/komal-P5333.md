Electric current flows in a long, straight cylindrical piece of wire of radius 2 cm. The magnitude of the magnetic flux density inside the wire at a distance of 1.5 cm from the symmetry axis of the wire is $2\cdot 10^{-4}$ T. What is the magnitude of the magnetic flux density at a distance of 4 cm from the symmetry axis of the wire?
