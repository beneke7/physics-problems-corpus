10. A binary star has a period $(T)$ of 2 earth years while distance $L$ between its components having masses $M_{1}$ and $M_{2}$ is four astronomical units. If $M_{1}=M_{S}$ where $M_{S}$ is the mass of Sun, the mass of other component $M_{2}$ is
(a) $3 M_{S}$
(b) $7 M_{S}$
(c) $15 M_{S}$
(d) $M_{S}$

Note: The earth - sun distance is one astronomical unit.
