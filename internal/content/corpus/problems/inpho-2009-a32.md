32. Three closed vessels $A, B$ and $C$ are at the same temperature $(T)$ and contain gases which obey the Maxwellian distribution of velocities. The vessel $A$ contains only $O_{2}, B$ only $N_{2}$ and $C$ a mixture of equal quantities of $O_{2}$ and $N_{2}$. If the average speed of the $N_{2}$ molecules in vessel $B$ is $V_{2}$ and that of oxygen molecules in $A$ is $V_{1}$, the average speed of $N_{2}$ molecules in $C$ is
(a) $\left(V_{1}+V_{2}\right) / 2$
(b) $\left(V_{1}-V_{2}\right) / 2$
(c) $V_{2}$
(d) $\sqrt{\left(V_{1} V_{2}\right)}$
