**8. ELECTRON (5 points)**

An electron is at rest at the origin. At time $t=0$, an electric field is switched
on: its magnitude is constant and equal to $E_{0}$, but its direction rotates with
constant angular frequency $\omega$ in the $x-y$ plane. At time $t=0$, it is
directed along the $x$-axis.

1) Find the average velocity of the electron over a long time interval for $t>0$.

2) Sketch the trajectory of the electron and calculate its geometrical
characteristics.
