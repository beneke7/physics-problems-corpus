A small steel ball $B$ is at rest on the edge of a table of height 1 m . Another steel ball $A$, used as the bob of a metre-long simple pendulum, is released from rest with the pendulum suspension horizontal, and swings against $B$ as shown in the figure. The masses of the balls are identical and the collision is elastic.
![](../../../../../sources/200ppp/figures/200ppp-011.jpg)

Considering the motion of $B$ only up until the moment it first hits the ground:
(i) Which ball is in motion for the longer time?
(ii) Which ball covers the greater distance?
