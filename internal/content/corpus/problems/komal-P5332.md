A small ball of mass $m$ and of charge $Q=1~\mu$C hangs on a piece of insulating thread of length $L = 0.2$ m. At a distance of $2L$ below the suspension there is another small fixed ball of charge $Q$.
 $a)$ How does the angle between the thread and the vertical depend on the mass $m$?
 $b)$ What should the least value of $m$ be in order to have a distance of $L$ between the two balls?
 $c)$ What should the maximum value of $m$ be in order to have a distance of $3L$ between the two balls?
 (5 pont)
