Rings $O$ and $O^{\prime}$ are slipping freely along vertical fixed rails $A B$ and $A^{\prime} B^{\prime}$ (see the figure); the distance between the rails is $b$. Some unstretchable rope has been tied to ring $O$ and pulled through ring $O^{\prime}$. The other end of the rope is fixed to point $A^{\prime}$. At the moment when $\angle A O O^{\prime}=\alpha$, the ring $O^{\prime}$ is moving downwards at a constant velocity $v$. Find the velocity and acceleration of the ring $O$ at the same moment.
