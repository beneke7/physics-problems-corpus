Two balls, made of soft copper alloy of specific heat capacity 400 J/(kg$^{.}$K), collide head on. The initial speeds of the two balls, A and B , were the same and oppositely directed. After the collision ball A stops and ball B bounces back with the same speed as it had before the collision. Because of the collision the temperature of the balls increased with C. At least what was the speed of the balls before the collision? 
 (4 pont)
