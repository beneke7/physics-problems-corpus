2. Consider a ball which is projected horizontally with speed $u$ from the edge of a cliff of height $H$ as shown in the Fig. (1). There is air resistance proportional to the velocity in both $x$ and $y$ direction i.e. the motion in the $x(y)$ direction has air resistance given by the $c v_{x}\left(c v_{y}\right)$ where $c$ is the proportionality constant and $v_{x}\left(v_{y}\right)$ is the velocity in the $x(y)$ directions. Take the downward direction to be negative. The acceleration due to gravity is $g$. Take the origin of the system to be at the bottom of the cliff as shown in Fig. (1).
    (a) Obtain expression for $x(t)$ and $y(t)$.
    (b) Obtain the expression for the equation of trajectory.
