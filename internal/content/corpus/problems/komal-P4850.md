The mass of a small ball is 5 g, and its diameter is 4 cm. The ball is hit upward at a speed of $10~\frac{\rm m}{\rm s}$ in the air which has a temperature of $20~{}^\circ$C and a pressure of 1 bar. Calculate with approximations the followings:
 $a)$ the height to which the ball ascends;
 $b)$ the time during which the ball arrives back to its initial position;
 $c)$ the speed of the ball when it gets back to its initial position.
 (6 pont)
