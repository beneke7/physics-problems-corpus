A small bug starts crawling from the vertex $P$ of a wooden cube of edges 10 cm. At least how long does it take for the bug to reach the furthest vertex $Q$ of the cube, if the speed of the bug is 1 cm/s? In how many different paths can the bug move in order to reach $Q$ in the shortest time? 
 (4 pont)
