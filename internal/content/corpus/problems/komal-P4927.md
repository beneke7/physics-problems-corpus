When a battery of unknown internal resistance and electromotive force is loaded with a resistor of resistance 10 ohm the terminal voltage across the battery is 6 V and the energy stored in the battery decreases by 7.2 J in each second.
 $a)$ What is the internal resistance of the battery?
 $b)$ What is its electromotive force?
 $c)$ What is the terminal voltage when the resistance of the load is increased to 20 ohms?
