A plane divides space into two halves. One half is filled with a homogeneous conducting medium and physicists work in the other. They mark the outline of a square of side $a$ on the plane and let a current $I_{0}$ in and out at two of its neighbouring corners using fine electrodes. Meanwhile, they measure the p.d. $V$ between the two other corners. This is illustrated in the figure.
![](../../sources/200ppp/figures/200ppp-071.jpg)

How can they calculate the resistivity of the homogeneous medium using this data?
