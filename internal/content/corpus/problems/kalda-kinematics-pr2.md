Two planes fly at the same height with speeds $v_{1}= 800 \mathrm{~km} / \mathrm{h}$ and $v_{2}=600 \mathrm{~km} / \mathrm{h}$, respectively. The planes approach each other; at a certain moment of time, the plane trajectories are perpendicular to each other and both planes are at the distance $a=20 \mathrm{~km}$ from the intersection points of their trajectories. Find the minimal distance between the planes during their flight assuming their velocities will remain constant.
