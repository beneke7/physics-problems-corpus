**III. BALLISTIC ROCKET (8 points)**

A rocket is launched from a pole of the Earth with the first cosmic velocity (near-Earth orbital velocity) in such a way that it lands at the Equator. The radius of the Earth $R=6400 \mathrm{~km}$.

1) Find the longer semi-axes $a$ of the rocket's orbit (1.5 pts).
2) What is the maximal height of the rocket's orbit $h$ (from the Earth's surface; 3.5 pts)?
3) What is the rocket's flight time $\tau$ (3 p)?

Remark: The mechanical energy of a planet rotating around a star $E=-G M m / 2 a$, where $G$ is the gravitation constant, $M$ - the star mass, $m$ - the planet mass, and $a$ - the orbit's longer semi-axes (zero potential energy corresponds to an infinite departure). The surface area of an ellipse $S=\pi a b$, where $b$ is the shorter semi-axes.
