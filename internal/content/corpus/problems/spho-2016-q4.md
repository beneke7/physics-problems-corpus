4. An experiment was performed to determine the value of the gravitational acceleration $g$ on Earth. Two equal masses of mass $M$ hang at rest from the ends of a string on each side of a light pulley (see figure below). A mass $m=0.01 M$ is placed on the right-hand-side mass. After the heavier side has moved down from rest by $h=1 \mathrm{~m}$, the small mass $m$ is removed. The system continues to move for the next 1s, covering a distance of $H=0.312 \mathrm{~m}$. Find the value of $g$.
