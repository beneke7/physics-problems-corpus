4. In a cylinder of an automobile engine just after combustion, the gas is confined to volume of $50.0 \mathrm{~cm}^{3}$ and has an initial pressure of $3.00 \times 10^{6} \mathrm{~Pa}$. The piston moves outwards to a final volume of $300 \mathrm{~cm}^{3}$ and the gas expands without energy loss by heat.
    (i) If the adiabatic constant $\gamma=1.40$ for the gas, what is the final pressure? [5]
    (ii) Determine the amount of work done by the gas during the expansion. [5]
