A thermally insulated cylinder of cross section 1 dm${}^2$, containing some air, is attached to the ceiling. Initially the volume of the air is 3 litres, and its temperature is 300 K. The piston which confines the gas into the cylinder is also thermally insulated and has a mass of 10 kg. Another 10-kg object is attached with a rope to the piston and hangs 10 cm above the ground. The air is slowly heated until its volume reaches the value of 4.4 litres. 

 $a)$ What is the temperature of the gas at the final state? 
 $b)$ How much heat should be added to the gas? 
 (5 pont)
