**7. FIRE (6 points)**

Wet wood is burning in a fireplace at ground level. Seven metres above the ground,
the smoke has temperature $t_{7}=40^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$. Disregard heat exchange
with the surrounding air and assume that the atmospheric pressure at ground level
is constant in time and equal to $p_{0}=1000 \mathrm{hPa}$; the air temperature
$t_{0}=20^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ is independent of height. Assume that the smoke is an
ideal gas with molar mass $\mu=29 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{mol}$ (equal to the molar
mass of air) and molar specific heat at constant volume $C_{V}=2.5 R$; the
universal gas constant is $R=8.31 \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{(mol\,K)}$. How high will the
smoke column rise?
