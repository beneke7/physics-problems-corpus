Question 10
Which of the following is the best estimate of the volume of an orange.
a. 3×10−5 m3
b. 3×10−4 m3
c. 3×10−3 m3
d. 3×10−2 m3
e. 3×10−1 m3
Solution: b. An orange has a volume of around 300 mL = 3×10−4 m3. This can be seen by comparing
the volume of an orange to e.g., a can of soft drink.
