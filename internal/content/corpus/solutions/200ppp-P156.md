## Hint

Remember that the charge is unchanged when the oil is removed.

## Solution

The stored energy is $Q^{2} / 2 C$ in both cases and $C_{\text {oil }}=\varepsilon C_{\text {air }}$. Thus the energy is increased by a factor of $\varepsilon$ when the oil is poured out. The catch is that the oil is attracted to where the field is strongest and work has to be done to extract it from there; the work needed is at least as great as the increase in stored electrical energy.
