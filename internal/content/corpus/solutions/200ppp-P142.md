## Hint

Consider how $T$ could be incorporated in a formula for the amplitude.

## Solution

Only the energy of the air molecules can be relevant and so $T$ appears in the combination $k T$. Dimensional analysis using $\ell, F, m$ and $k T$ shows that the amplitude must depend on combinations of variables of the form $(k T / F)^{q} \ell^{1-q}$, where $q$ can take any value; $m$ does not appear and so the amplitude is independent of the mass of the cobweb.
