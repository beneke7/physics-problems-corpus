Question 2
Which of the following is the best estimate of the speed of a parrot flying?
a. 3×10−3 ms−1
b. 1×10−1 ms−1
c. 3×100 ms−1
d. 1×102 ms−1
e. 3×103 ms−1
Solution: c. parrots fly much faster than people walk, which rules out a. and b. which are both less than
1 ms−1. However, they do not fly as fast as 100 m, which is a typical length of an oval, in 1 s, which
rules out d. and e.
