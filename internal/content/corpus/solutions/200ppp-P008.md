## Hint

Determine how much additional volume is submerged if the berg is depressed by a small distance $x$. Use the flotation condition to relate the mass $M$ of the berg to its overall dimensions.

## Solution

If the berg has base area $A$ and height $H$, then $M=\frac{1}{3} A H \rho_{\text {ice }}$. If the height showing above the surface is $h$, the flotation condition gives $\left(H^{3}-h^{3}\right) \rho_{\text {water }}=H^{3} \rho_{\text {ice }}$. When the berg is depressed by a small amount $x$ the additional submerged volume is $x A(h / H)^{2}$ and the upthrust is this multiplied by $\rho_{\text {water }} g$. This gives that the angular frequency of oscillation $\omega$ is determined by

$$
\omega^{2}=\frac{3 h^{2} \rho_{\mathrm{water}} g}{\rho_{\mathrm{ice}} H^{3}}
$$

and, on substituting numerical values, that the period of oscillation is about 11 s .
