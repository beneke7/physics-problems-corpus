Question 7
Ali has spent too much time deciding what to do, and only takes one pair of measurements. He measures
the mass of the weight to be (100±5) g and the stretch, s, when he hangs it on the spring to be
(5.0±0.5) cm. Based on these data, the spring constant is:
a. (20.0±0.2) N/m
b. (20±3) N/m
c. (20±6) N/m
d. (200±20) N/m
e. (200±30) N/m
Solution: b. k =
Fapplied
s = mg
s = 0.100 kg×10 m,s−2
0.05 m = 20.0 N/m. When multiplying or dividing numbers
the relative uncertainties add. Here the relative uncertainty in s is ∆s
s = 0.5
5.0 = 0.1 and the relative
uncertainty in m is ∆m/m = 5/100 = 0.05. Hence, ∆k/k = ∆s/s+∆m/m= 0.1+0.05 = 0.15, and
∆k = 0.15×20.0 = 3 N/m.
