C.7: In the absence of magnetic fields, when T > Tc,
there is only one solution at s̄ = 0, which means that
the system cannot maintain a net polarization. How-
ever, applying a magnetic field leads to a net magneti-
zation, which is a characteristic of paramagnets. On
the other hand, when T < Tc, the system can support
a net magnetization even in the absence of magnetic
fields, which is the characteristic of ferromagnets.
