Question 3
A book lies at rest on a table. The table is at rest on the surface of the Earth. By Newton’s Third Law, the
reaction force to the weight of the book is:
a. the gravitational force of the Earth on the book.
b. the normal force exerted by the table on the book.
c. the gravitational force exerted by the Earth on the table.
d. the normal force exerted by the Earth on the table.
e. the gravitational force of the book on the Earth.
Solution: e. The weight of the book is the gravitational force exerted on the book by the Earth. A
Newton’s Third Law reaction force must be of the same physical type, i.e. gravitational, and must be
exerted by the body feeling the first force and act on the body causing the first force. Hence, the reaction
to the weight of the book is the gravitational force exerted by the book on the Earth.
