(b) Since $V_{0}=\frac{-\Delta y}{\Delta t}$ (probe going down, y points up) 2/0,

$$
\begin{aligned}
& \Delta P=-\rho g \Delta y \quad \text { gives } \\
& \frac{\Delta P}{\Delta t}=\rho g v_{0}
\end{aligned}
$$

Need $\rho$ : From ideal gas eq. of state $P V=n R T$,

$$
\begin{aligned}
P=\frac{n}{V} R T \quad \text { where } \begin{aligned}
\frac{n}{V} & =\frac{\# \text { moles }}{\text { vol. }} \\
& =\frac{\# \text { moles }}{\text { mass }} \frac{\text { mass }}{\text { vol. }} \\
\frac{n}{V} & =\frac{1}{M} \rho \\
\Rightarrow P=\frac{\rho}{M} R T & \\
\rho=\frac{P M}{R T} \quad \ldots(2) &
\end{aligned}
\end{aligned}
$$

Using $\rho$ from ( 2 ) in ( 1 ),

$$
\begin{aligned}
& \frac{\Delta P}{\Delta t}=\frac{P M}{R T} g v_{0} \\
& v_{0}=\left(\frac{\Delta P}{\Delta t}\right) \frac{R T}{P M g}
\end{aligned}
$$
