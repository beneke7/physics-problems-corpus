When light passes through a very narrow slit it diffracts. This means that the light spreads out and forms

Solution: The width of the central bright region of pattern (b) is 1.5cm, and that for pattern (a) is
0.5cm, hence, pattern (b) is 1.5/0.5 = 3 times wider than pattern (a).
(i) The width of the central bright region is inversely proportional to slit width, so slit width
= 3.0×10−4 m/3 = 1.0×10−4 m.
(ii) The width of the central bright region is proportional to wavelength, so wavelength
= 650 nm×3 = 1950 nm. (Note that this is an infrared wavelength so we are unlikely to
observe it directly!)
(iii) The width of the central bright region is proportional to distance from the slit to the screen,
so distance to screen = 1.20 m×3 = 3.60 m.

Light comes from a slit at S, and the rays SR and ST represent the light at the edges of the central
bright region. The points Q and P lie on one possible screen, a distance d from the slit, and the
