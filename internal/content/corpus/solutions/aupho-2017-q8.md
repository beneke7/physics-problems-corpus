Question 8
A block of mass 5 kg lies at rest on a horizontal surface. An upwards force of 20 N is applied to the
block, as shown. Assuming g = 10 ms−2, what is the weight of the block?
20 N
5 kg
a. 3 kg
b. 5 kg
c. 30 N
d. 50 N
e. 5 kg−20 N
Solution: d. The weight is the gravitational force on the block. This is
W = mg = 5 kg×10 ms−2 = 50 N.
