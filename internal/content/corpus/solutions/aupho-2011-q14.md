Question 14
Argon atoms are special because they stay in the atmosphere for a very long time. They are not recycled
like oxygen and nitrogen. The aim of this problem is to estimate how many of the argon atoms in each of
your breaths were once in Galileo Galilei’s lungs.
An average breath inhales around 0.5 L of air and people breath on average around once every five
seconds. Air is about 1% argon and has density 1.2 kgm−3
. Assume all air particles have a mass of
approximately 5×10−26 kg. Take the atmosphere to have constant density and be around 20 km thick.
The radius of the Earth is RE = 6.4×106 m.
Note: 1000 L = 1 m3
