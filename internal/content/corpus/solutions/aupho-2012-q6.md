Question 6
Twins Anna and Tom measure each other’s heights each birthday. When they were 12 Anna’s height was
measured as 151.5 cm and Tom’s was measured as 151 cm. On their 16th birthday Anna’s height is
measured as 167.5 cm and Tom’s is measured as 168.5 cm. The uncertainty in the height measurements
is 5 mm. Between the ages of 12 and 16 Anna grew:
a. 16 cm ± 5 cm.
b. 16 cm ± 1 cm.
c. 16 cm ± 0.5 cm.
d. 16.0 cm ± 0.5 cm.
e. 16.0 cm ± 0.25 cm.
Solution: b. — The uncertainty of 5 mm in each measurement must be added to find the uncertainty in
Anna’s height increase.
