Question 5.
A woman is standing on a set of bathroom scales, measuring her weight, in an elevator. Her
weight as shown on the scales is W before the elevator starts to move. She presses the down
button. As the elevator starts to go down, the weight shown on the scales is:
(A) More than W
(B) Equal to W
(C)Less than W
(D)You can’t tell without knowing the velocity the lift is moving at
(E) You can’t tell without knowing the acceleration of the lift
This question requires an understanding of the normal force. This question should be
easy if students take the time to draw a free body diagram and consider the nett
force and acceleration, and most students (72%) did answer this question correctly.
