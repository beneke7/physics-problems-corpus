## Hint

Consider the heat balance at the base of the layer when the layer thickness is $x$.

## Solution

The heat conducted away in a given time must equal (minus) the latent heat of fusion of the additional ice formed in that time. Thus for an area $A$ of the lake

$$
\lambda_{\mathrm{i}} A \frac{\Delta T}{x}=L_{\mathrm{i}} \frac{\mathrm{~d}}{\mathrm{~d} t}\left(\rho_{\mathrm{i}} A x\right) .
$$

Simple integration gives that $t=\frac{1}{2} x^{2} B$, where $B=\left(\rho_{\mathrm{i}} L_{\mathrm{i}}\right) /\left(\lambda_{\mathrm{i}} \Delta T\right)$. Inserting the relevant numerical values gives the time as about 90 hours.
