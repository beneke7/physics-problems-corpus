## Hint

Let the current through the voltmeter be $I$ and that through the major arc of the ring be $i$. Then, using consistent conventions for current directions and circuit traversal, apply Kirchhoff's laws to two different closed circuits.

## Solution

With $I$ and $i$ as defined in the hint, the required voltmeter reading
is given by $R I$. In both cases, applying Kirchhoff's laws yields the equations:

$$
\begin{aligned}
R I+\frac{\theta}{2 \pi} r(I+i) & =\frac{1}{2} a^{2} \dot{B} \lambda, \\
r i+\frac{\theta}{2 \pi} r I & =\pi a^{2} \dot{B} .
\end{aligned}
$$

In case $(a), \lambda=\theta$ and solution of the simultaneous equations shows that $I$, and hence the voltmeter reading, is zero.

In case $(b), \lambda=\theta-\sin \theta$ and straightforward but slightly lengthy algebra shows that the voltmeter reading will be

$$
|V|=\frac{2 \pi^{2} R a^{2} \dot{B} \sin \theta}{4 \pi^{2} R+r \theta(2 \pi-\theta)}
$$
