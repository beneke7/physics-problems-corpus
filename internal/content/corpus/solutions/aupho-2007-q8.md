Question 8.
A boy is swinging a ball attached to a string around in a horizontal circle with constant speed.
Consider the following forces:
I. a gravitational force downwards
II. a force directed towards the boy at the centre of the circle
III. a force in the direction of motion of the ball
IV. a force directed outwards away from the boy at the centre of the circle
Which of the above forces does work on the ball?
(A) I and II.
(B) II only.
(C) I, II, and III.
(D) I, III, and IV.
(E) none of these forces does work on the ball.
This question looks at the relationship between force and work. Many students do
not recognise that work is only done by the component of a force in the direction of
displacement. In this case only two of the forces listed actually exist, and the other
two are both perpendicular to the displacement at all times. The most common
answer was e (29%), however b and d were only slightly less popular choices.
