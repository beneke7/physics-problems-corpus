Question 7.
A boy is swinging a ball attached to a string around in a horizontal circle with constant speed.
Consider the following forces:
I. a gravitational force downwards
II. a force directed towards the boy at the centre of the circle
III. a force in the direction of motion of the ball
IV. a force directed outwards away from the boy at the centre of the circle
Which of the above forces is (are) acting on the ball?
(A) I only.
(B) I and II.
(C) I and III.
(D) I, II, and III.
(E) I, III, and IV.
Australian Science Olympiads 2007 Physics National Qualifying Examination
Page 4
This question looks for two misconceptions – that there is always a force in the
direction of motion and that there is an outwards acting (centrifugal)force acting on
objects in circular motion. The most common answer was e (37% of students), the
next most common was d and only 20% of students chose the correct answer.
