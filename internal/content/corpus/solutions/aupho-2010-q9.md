Question 9
Someone analysing an electric circuit obtains the equations
I1(3Ω) − I2(6Ω) = 7V ,
I1(2Ω) + I3(3Ω) = 28V ,
I2(2Ω) + I3(1Ω) = 17V .
Note that V is the unit volts, and Ω is the unit Ohms. I1, I2 and I3 are currents. Which of the
following statements is true?
(A) I1, I3 > 0 , I2 < 0
(B) I1, I3 < 0 , I2 > 0
(C) I1, I2, I3 > 0
(D) I3 > 0 , I1, I2 < 0
(E) I3 < 0 , I1, I2 > 0
Solution: (E) — I1 = 30 A, I2 = 83/6 A and I3 = −32/3 A.
