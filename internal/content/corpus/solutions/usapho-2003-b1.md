B1. a. Squaring the relativistic expressions for $E$ and $p$,

$$
E^{2}=\frac{m^{2} c^{4}}{1-(v / c)^{2}} \quad p^{2}=\frac{m^{2} v^{2}}{1-(v / c)^{2}}
$$

Multiplying the $p^{2}$ equation by $c^{2}$ and subtracting from $E^{2}$

$$
\begin{gathered}
E^{2}-c^{2} p^{2}=\frac{m^{2} c^{4}}{1-(v / c)^{2}}-\frac{m^{2} v^{2} c^{2}}{1-(v / c)^{2}}=\frac{m^{2} c^{4}\left(1-(v / c)^{2}\right)}{1-(v / c)^{2}}=m^{2} c^{4} \\
E^{2}=(c p)^{2}+\left(m c^{2}\right)^{2}
\end{gathered}
$$

b. Since A is at rest, $E_{A}=m_{A} c^{2}=1000 \mathrm{MeV}$ and $p_{A}=0$;
and

$$
c p_{B}=\sqrt{E_{B}^{2}-m_{B}^{2}{ }^{2} c^{4}}=\sqrt{(700 \mathrm{MeV})^{2}-(500 \mathrm{MeV})^{2}}=490 \mathrm{MeV}
$$

By conservation of energy and momentum,

Thus

$$
\begin{gathered}
E_{C^{*}}=E_{A}+E_{B}=1000 \mathrm{MeV}+700 \mathrm{MeV}=1700 \mathrm{MeV} \\
c p_{C^{*}}=c p_{A}+c p_{B}=0+490 \mathrm{MeV}=490 \mathrm{MeV} \\
m_{C^{*} C^{2}}=\sqrt{E_{C^{*}}^{2}-\left(c p_{C^{*}}\right)^{2}}=\sqrt{(1700 \mathrm{MeV})^{2}-(490 \mathrm{MeV})^{2}}=1628 \mathrm{MeV}
\end{gathered}
$$

c. Again, using conservation of energy and momentum,

$$
\begin{aligned}
& E_{C}+E_{\gamma}=E_{C} * \\
& c p_{C}+c p_{\gamma}=c p_{C^{*}}
\end{aligned}
$$

Since the gamma is massless, $E_{\gamma}{ }^{2}=\left(c p_{\gamma}\right)^{2}$. It is emitted along the direction of travel of $c^{*}$, so we select the positive root, $c p_{\gamma}=E_{\gamma}$. Thus, rearranging.

$$
\begin{aligned}
& E_{C}=E_{C^{*}}-E_{\gamma} \\
& c P_{C}=c P_{C^{*}}-E_{\gamma}
\end{aligned}
$$

We need a fourth relationship,

$$
E_{C}^{2}-\left(c p_{C}\right)^{2}=\left(m_{C} c^{2}\right)^{2}
$$

$$
\begin{gathered}
\left(E_{C^{*}}-E_{\gamma}\right)^{2}-\left(c p_{C^{*}}-E_{\gamma}\right)^{2}=\left(m_{C^{*}} c^{2}\right)^{2} \\
E_{C^{*}}{ }^{2}-2 E_{C^{*}} E_{\gamma}+E_{\gamma}^{2}-\left(c p_{C^{*}}\right)^{2}+2 c p_{C^{*}} E_{\gamma}-E_{\gamma}^{2}=\left(m_{C^{*}} c^{2}\right)^{2} \\
E_{C^{*}}^{2}-\left(c p_{C^{*}}\right)^{2}+2 c p_{C^{*}} E_{\gamma}-2 E_{C^{*}} E_{\gamma}=\left(m_{C^{C^{2}}}{ }^{2}\right)^{2}
\end{gathered}
$$

Since

$$
E_{C}^{2}-\left(c p_{C}\right)^{2}=\left(m_{C} \cdot c^{2}\right)^{2}
$$

the prior equation becomes, $\quad\left(m_{c} c^{2}\right)^{2}-2 E_{\gamma}\left(E_{c \cdot}-c p_{c \cdot}\right)=\left(m_{c} c^{2}\right)^{2}$

$$
E_{r}=\frac{\left(m_{c} c^{2}\right)^{2}-\left(m_{c} c^{2}\right)^{2}}{2\left(E_{c^{*}}-c{P_{c}}^{*}\right)}=\frac{(1628 \mathrm{MeV})^{2}-(1300 \mathrm{MeV})^{2}}{2(1700 \mathrm{MeV}-490 \mathrm{MeV})}=397 \mathrm{MeV}
$$

d. Let the gamma be emitted at an angle $\theta_{r}$ to the axis, and C at an angle $\theta_{G}$. We measure the two angles on opposite sides of the axis so that both are in the interval $[0, \pi]$.
From conservation of energy,
thus

$$
E_{C}-E_{C^{*}}-E_{\gamma}=1700 \mathrm{MeV}-300 \mathrm{MeV}=1400 \mathrm{MeV} ;
$$

And again.

$$
c p_{\gamma}=E_{\gamma}=300 \mathrm{MeV} .
$$

Then from conservation of momentum,

$$
\begin{aligned}
& p_{\gamma} \sin \theta_{\gamma}-p_{C} \sin \theta_{C}=0 \\
& p_{\gamma} \cos \theta_{\gamma}+p_{C} \cos \theta_{C}=p_{C} .
\end{aligned}
$$

Rearranging,

$$
\begin{gathered}
\left(p_{C} \sin \theta_{C}\right)^{2}+\left(p_{C} \cos \theta_{C}\right)^{2}=\left(p_{\gamma} \sin \theta_{\gamma}\right)^{2}+\left(p_{C^{*}}-p_{\gamma} \cos \theta_{\gamma}\right)^{2} \\
p_{C}{ }^{2}=p_{C^{*}}{ }^{2}+p_{\gamma}{ }^{2}-2 p_{C} * p_{\gamma} \cos \theta_{\gamma} \\
\cos \theta_{\gamma}=\frac{p_{C^{*}}{ }^{2}+p_{\gamma}{ }^{2}-p_{C}{ }^{2}}{2 p_{C} * p_{\gamma}}=\frac{(490 \mathrm{MeV} / \mathrm{c})^{2}+(300 \mathrm{MeV} / \mathrm{c})^{2}-(520 \mathrm{MeV} / \mathrm{c})^{2}}{2(490 \mathrm{MeV} / \mathrm{c})(300 \mathrm{MeV} / \mathrm{c})}=0.204 \\
\theta_{\gamma}=\cos ^{-1} 0.204=1.365 \mathrm{rad}
\end{gathered}
$$

Then,

$$
\sin \theta_{C}=\frac{p_{\gamma}}{p_{C}} \sin \theta_{\gamma}=\frac{300 \mathrm{MeV} / \mathrm{c}}{520 \mathrm{MeV} / \mathrm{c}} \sin 1.365=0.565
$$

Thus,

$$
\theta_{C}=0.601 \mathrm{rad} .
$$
