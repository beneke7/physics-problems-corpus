A4. The only torque acting on the system is due to the gravitaqtional force.

For small angles

$$
\begin{aligned}
& I \alpha=\sum \tau=(M+m) g R \sin \theta \\
& \alpha \equiv\left(\frac{(M+m) g R}{l}\right) \theta=\omega^{2} \theta
\end{aligned}
$$

We will use the parallel axis theorem to find the moment of inertia of the sytem. Swinging in the plane of the bob, pivot on rim

$$
\begin{aligned}
I_{t o t} & =I_{r i n g c m}+M R^{2}+I_{r o d c m}+m R^{2} \\
& =M R^{2}+M R^{2}+1 / 12 m(2 R)^{2}+m R^{2}=(2 M+4 / 3 m) R^{2}
\end{aligned}
$$

Swinging perpendicular to the bob, we expect the extreme cases to be rod horizontal and rod vertical

$$
\text { vertical: } I_{\text {rod }}=4 / 3 m R^{2} \quad \text { horizontal: } I_{\text {rod }}=m R^{2}
$$

For the ring:

$$
I_{\text {ring }}=I_{\text {rngcm }}+M R^{2}=1 / 2 M R^{2}+M R^{2}=3 / 2 M R^{2}
$$

vertical:

$$
I_{v \text { lot }}=3 / 2 M R^{2}+4 / 3 m R^{2}=(3 / 2 M+4 / 3 m) R^{2}
$$

horizontal:

$$
I_{\text {htot }}=3 / 2 M R^{2}+m R^{2}=(3 / 2 M+m) R^{2}
$$

The maximum period occurs when $\omega$ is smallest or $l$ is largest. Since $\quad I_{\text {tot }} \geq I_{\text {vtot }} \geq I_{\text {htot }}$ the maximum period occurs when it swings in the plane of the bob. The minimum period occurs when it swings perpendicular to the plane of the bob and the wire is horizontal.

$$
\frac{T_{\max }}{T_{\min }}=\sqrt{\frac{I_{\max }}{I_{\min }}}=\sqrt{\frac{2 M+4 / 3 m}{3 / 2 M+m}}=\sqrt{4 / 3}
$$
