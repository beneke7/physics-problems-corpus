## Hint

Find the net force (the sum of the magnetic force, the weight and the tension in the string) exerted on magnet $A$ as a function $F(x)$ of the distance $x$ apart of the magnets. Use $F(x)$ to determine the conditions for equilibrium and stability.

## Solution

(i) The horizontal force (measured in the direction of increasing $x$, i.e. to the left in the figure in the problem) exerted on magnet $A$ is

$$
F(x)=-\frac{K}{x^{n}}+m g \frac{(d+s-x)}{\ell},
$$

where $d+s$ is the distance from magnet $B$ to the unperturbed position of magnet $A$. The condition of equilibrium is

$$
\left.F(x)\right|_{x=d}=0 .
$$

The stability of equilibrium depends on the behaviour of the function $F(x)$ close to $x=d$. The function can be monotonically increasing or decreasing (Fig. S154.1).

![](../../../../../sources/200ppp/figures/200ppp-229.jpg)
Fig. S154.1

For stable equilibrium, its derivative has to be negative. In the unstable case the derivative is positive, and in the limiting case of neutral equilibrium it is zero, i.e.

$$
\left.F^{\prime}(x)\right|_{x=d}=0 .
$$

Using the expression in (1) for the force $F(x)$, condition (3) can be formulated as

$$
\frac{n K}{d^{n+1}}-\frac{m g}{\ell}=0
$$

But condition (2) can be written in the form

$$
-\frac{K}{d^{n}}+\frac{m g}{\ell} s=0
$$

From these equations we get

$$
n=\frac{d}{s}=4 \quad \text { and } \quad K=m g d^{4} \frac{s}{\ell} .
$$

(ii) In the vertical tube (Fig. S154.2) the repulsive force acting on magnet $A$ is $F_{\text {vert }}(x)=+K / x^{4}-m g$.

![](../../../../../sources/200ppp/figures/200ppp-230.jpg)
Fig. S154.2

Equilibrium levitation occurs with the magnets a distance $h$ apart, where

$$
\left.F_{\text {vert }}(x)\right|_{x=h}=0 .
$$

Taking into account result (6) we get

$$
h=\left(\frac{K}{m g}\right)^{1 / 4}=d\left(\frac{s}{\ell}\right)^{1 / 4}=4 \mathrm{~cm} \times\left(\frac{1}{100}\right)^{1 / 4} \approx 1.3 \mathrm{~cm} .
$$
