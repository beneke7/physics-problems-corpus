Question 4.
A large bus and a small car collide and stick together. Which one undergoes the larger change
in momentum?
(A) The car.
(B) The bus.
(C) The momentum change is the same for both vehicles.
(D) You can’t tell without knowing the final velocity of the combined masses.
(E) The result depends on the energy absorbed by the crumpling bodies of the vehicles
on impact.
This is a conservation of momentum / Newton’s third law question. 33% of students answered correctly, and this
was the most commonly given answer, followed closely by d. and then a.
