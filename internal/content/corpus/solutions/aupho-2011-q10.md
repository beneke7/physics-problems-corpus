The positions of two joggers, Heloise and Abelard, are shown below. The joggers are shown at

Do Heloise and Abelard ever have the same speed?
a. No.
b. Yes, at instant 2.
c. Yes. at instant 5.
d. Yes, at instants 2 and 5.
e. Yes at some time during the interval 3 to 4.
Solution: (e) — Abelard has a constant speed, before 3 Heloise is slower than Abelard, and after 4
Heloise is faster than Abelard. Hence, at some point in the interval 3 to 4 they have the same speed.
