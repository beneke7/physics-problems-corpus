Question 2
The speed of light in a vacuum, c, depends on two fundamental constants; the permeability of free space,
µ0, and the permittivity of free space, ε0. The speed of light is related to these constants by
c = (µ0ε0)−1/2
.
The SI units of ε0 are N−1
C2
m−2
. Recall that a force of 1 N accelerates a mass of 1 kg at 1 ms−2
.
The units of µ0 are
a. kg−1m−1C2
b. kg−1s−3C−2
c. kgms−4
C−2
d. kgmC−2
e. kg2m2 C−4
Solution: (d) — µ0 = 1/(ε0c2) so its SI units must be NC−2 s2 or kgmC−2.
