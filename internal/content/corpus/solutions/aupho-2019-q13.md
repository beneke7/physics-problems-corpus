The pattern of magnets in the fridge magnet should be uniform up and down the magnets, so they slide
easily in that direction, as there are no changes to the magnetic field. There should be a repeating pattern
in the horizontal direction to get the bumpy feeling when they are slid apart.
The diagrams above show a single unit of the same pattern from b), which can be used to generate a
strong field on the black side and a weak field on the picture side. Note that the three diagrams are not all
required, but that where there are multiple diagrams they should be consistent with each other. Any
horizontal offset in the pattern is equally good a solution.
