Question 8
A marauding possum dislodges a tile from the roof of a single–storey house. The tile
a. reaches a maximum speed quite soon after release and then falls at a constant speed the rest of the
way to the ground.
b. speeds up during its entire fall to the ground because the gravitational attraction gets considerably
stronger as the brick gets closer to the Earth.
c. speeds up during its entire fall to the ground because of an almost constant force of gravity acting
on it.
d. falls to the ground because of the natural tendency of all objects to rest on the surface of the Earth.
e. falls to the ground because of the combined effects of the force of gravity pushing it downward
and the force of the air pushing it downward.
Solution: (c) — the gravitational field is approximately constant over the distance the tile falls, so it falls
with almost constant acceleration.
