# Note bundles

Note bundles preserve source-organized teaching material. They are separate
from `corpus/problems/`: a numbered example is added as a problem only after
the source-overlap audit establishes that it is new or a meaningful rework.

English source-oriented notes live under `corpus/notes/<family>/`; Hungarian
mirrors live under `corpus/notes_hu/<family>/` and link back to the English
bundle. Source snapshots and checksums remain under `sources/`.

Kevin Zhou's 103 explicitly marked examples from `phy.pdf` are archived here
as `reference_example` records, separately from competition problems. The
remaining lecture chapters stay source-bound rather than being bulk-ingested.
