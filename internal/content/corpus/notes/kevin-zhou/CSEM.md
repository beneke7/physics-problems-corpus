# Acknowledgments

We would like to thank L. C. McDermott and P. S. Shaffer for allowing us to use their materials. We thank the many professors who administered the CSEM and provided us with data and excellent comments. We also want to thank the many participants of the TYC Physics Workshop Project who provided expert comments at various stages of development of the surveys. We thank Robert Beichner and the other members of the North Carolina State University physics education research group for extensive feedback on a preliminary draft of this article. Finally we thank Bruce Sherwood and Ruth Chabay for allowing us to incorporate into the CSEM questions 3, 4, 5, 31, and 32, which they developed.
