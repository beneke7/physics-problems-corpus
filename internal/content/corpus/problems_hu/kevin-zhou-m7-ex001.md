Example 1

        A large rock is tied to a balloon filled with air. Both are placed in a lake. As the balloon
        sinks, how do the air nyomás in the balloon, the average sűrűség of the balloon, air, and
        rock system, and magnitude of the net erő on the system vary?

        Solution
        For simplicity, we ignore the elastic erő in the balloon itself. Then for the balloon to be in
        egyensúly, its nyomás must match that of the víz nyomás, so the air nyomás in the
        balloon increases. As the balloon sinks, the rock stays the same térfogat but the balloon is
        squeezed smaller, so the sűrűség of the system increases. Finally, since the sűrűség of víz is
        very approximately constant, the felhajtóerő on the system is decreasing since its térfogat is
        decreasing, so the net erő is increasing; the system accelerates downward faster and faster.
