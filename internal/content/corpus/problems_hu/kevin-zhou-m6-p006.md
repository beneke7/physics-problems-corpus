       Remark: Scaling Symmetry

       There’s a variant of Kepler’s third law for unbound orbits. Tegyük fel a planet is right next
       to the Sun at time t = 0, but has a large initial radial sebesség, so that it has zero total
       energy. Then its távolság to the Sun evolves as r(t) ∝ t2/3 , like how a ∝ T 2/3 for bound orbits.

       Both of these results come from the scaling symmetry of inverse square erő laws: any
       solution to Newton’s second law remains a solution ha you multiply all távolságs by 4 and
       all times by 8. The widest-reaching application of this idea is to the whole universe itself.
       Ha it contains only matter, which started at the origin at time t = 0, and it expands under
       gravity with zero total energy, then its “scale factor” evolves as a(t) ∝ t2/3 . This was a good
       description of our universe for most of its lhaetime, but in the past few billion years the effects
       of dark energy took over, accelerating the expansion. We’ll revisit cosmology in X3.
