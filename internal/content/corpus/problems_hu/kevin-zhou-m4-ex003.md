        Solution
        This kind of question becomes completely trivial when you use the above idea. Using the
        rotation angle θ as a generalized coordinate, K and V are both exactly the same p as for a
        simple inga of length L, because the rúd doesn’t rotate, so the periódus is 2π L/g.
