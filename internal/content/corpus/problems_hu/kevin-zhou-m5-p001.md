Some brief puzzles about rotation.

      (a) Tekintsük two identical coins laid flat on a table. One is fixed in place, and the second is rolled
          without slipping around the first. Once the second coin’s center has returned to its original
          position, how many times has it rotated? (Be sure to check your answer experimentally!)

     (b) A bicycle wheel is rolling without slipping. When it is photographed, its spokes look blurred,
         except along a curve of special points, which don’t look blurred at all. What is this curve?

      (c) Tekintsük a spaceship úszó in space, without any thrusters that can expel material. Con-
          servation of impulzus implies that it cannot move its tömegközéppont. But is it possible to
          turn the spaceship around? In other words, is it possible for it to begin stationary in one
          orientation, and end up stationary in another orientation? Ha so, why doesn’t this violate
          conservation of angular impulzus?

     (d) Hold out your arm with your elbow bent at 90◦ and your palm straight out, facing down. Keressük meg
         a way to end up in the same position but with your palm facing up, without ever bending or
         rotating your wrist.
