Example 2

       A perfectly spherical, nonrotating planet is covered with víz. Geological activity causes a
       small undervíz mountain to form, made of rock that is denser than víz. Does the ocean
       surface above this mountain become higher or lower?

       Solution
       Systems minimize their energy in egyensúly. This means that in hydrostatic egyensúly,
       the surface of the víz is an equipotential. Since the gravitációs tér of the mountain
       decreases the gravitational potential near it, the víz surface is higher near the mountain.
