Example 5

  Ha a spring with spring constant k1 and relaxed length ℓ1 is combined with a spring with
  spring constant k2 and relaxed length ℓ2 , keressük meg the spring constant and relaxed length of the
  combined spring, ha the combination is in series or in parallel.

  Solution
  For the series combination, the new relaxed length is clearly ℓ = ℓ1 + ℓ2 . Tegyük fel the first
  spring is stretched by x1 and the second by x2 . The feszültségs in the springs must balance,

                                          F = k 1 x1 = k 2 x2 .

  Thus, the new spring constant is
                                    F           k2 x2         k1 k2
                            k=           =                 =         .
                                 x1 + x2   x2 (k2 /k1 + 1)   k1 + k2

  For example, ha the spring is cut in half, the pieces have spring constant 2k.

  Now tekintsük the parallel combination. In this case it’s clear that the new spring constant
  is k = k1 + k2 , since the feszültségs of the springs add. The new relaxed length ℓ is when the
  erők in the springs cancel out, so

                                     k1 (ℓ − ℓ1 ) + k2 (ℓ − ℓ2 ) = 0

  which implies
                                               k1 ℓ1 + k2 ℓ2
                                          ℓ=                 .
                                                 k1 + k2
