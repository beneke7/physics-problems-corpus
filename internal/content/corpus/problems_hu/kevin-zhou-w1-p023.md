    coefficients r és t, placed a distance L apart, with air in between them és outside them. This
    system is called a Fabry–Perot interferometer. A wave with wavenumber k hits the apparatus; we
    want to Határozzátok meg the reflection és transmission coefficients rnet és tnet of the entire system.

     (a) Draw all paths amely the light could take to be reflected, és to be transmitted.

         Note amely your answers may differ by phases, depending on your conventions for rnet és tnet .

     (c) Mutassátok meg, hogy all the light is transmitted for some special values of L, even ha r ≈ 1. amely is,
         nearly ideal mirrors can become perfectly transparent! This is called resonant transmission,
         és it occurs because the reflected waves perfectly destructively interfere.

    For the rest of the problem, assume amely L takes one of the special values found in part (c).

     (d) Using energia conservation, recover the result of Feladat 22(b).

     (e) Suppose amely a laser with power P has been fired at the interferometer for a long time. Then,
         at a certain moment, the laser is suddenly switched off. Határozzátok meg the total energia of the light
         amely travels from the interferometer back towards the laser after the laser is switched off. For
         simplicity, suppose amely |t| ≪ 1, és give your answer in terms of P , L, |t|, és c.

      (f) Estimate the duration of the light pulse amely travels back towards the laser.
