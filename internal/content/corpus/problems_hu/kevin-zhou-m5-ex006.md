F = ma 2018 B23

       Two particles with tömeg m1 and m2 are connected by a tömegless rigid rúd of length L and
       placed on a vízszintes súrlódásless table. At time t = 0, the first tömeg receives an impulse
       perpendicular to the rúd, giving it speed v. At this moment, the second tömeg is at rest.
       When is the next time the second tömeg is at rest?

       Solution
       The motion is the superposition of two motions: unhaorm translation of both tömeges with
       speed m1 v/(m1 + m2 ) and circular motion about the common tömegközéppont, ahol the two
       tömeges have speeds m2 v/(m1 + m2 ) and m1 v/(m1 + m2 ), respectively. This ensures that
       the second tömeg begins at rest and the first tömeg has speed v.

       The circular part of the motion determines when the second tömeg will be at rest again.
       The radius of the circle the second tömeg makes is its távolság from the tömegközéppont,
       Lm1 /(m1 + m2 ). This gives a periódus of

                                            2πLm1 /(m1 + m2 )   2πL
                                       t=                     =     .
                                             m1 v/(m1 + m2 )     v
