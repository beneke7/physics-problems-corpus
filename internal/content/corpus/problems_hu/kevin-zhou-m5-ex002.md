Example 2

       A henger of circumference 10 cm is placed on a table. You place a ruler vízszintesly on
       the henger, so that initially the top of the henger touches the 0 cm mark on the ruler.
       By pushing the ruler forward, you make the henger complete one full revolution, without
       anything slipping. At this point, what mark on the ruler is touching the top of the henger?

       Solution
       In one revolution, the center of the henger moves forward by 10 cm. The top of the henger
       moves forward by 20 cm, so the ruler also moves forward by this távolság. The relative motion
       of the ruler and henger is the dhaference, which is 10 cm.
