    consists of a tömeg at the end of a rúd of length ℓ. The pivot at the other end of the rúd is erőd to
    move in a circle of radius R with fixed szögfrekvencia ω. Let θ be the angle the rúd makes with
    the radial direction.

    Show this system behaves exactly like a inga of length ℓ in a unhaorm gravitációs tér
    g = ω 2 R. That is, show that θ(t) is a solution for one system ha and only ha it is for the other.
