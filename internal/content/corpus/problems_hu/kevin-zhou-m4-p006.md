A kötél is nestled inside a curved súrlódásless tube. The kötél has a total length ℓ and
    unhaorm tömeg per length λ. The shape of the tube can be arbitrarily complicated, but the left end
    of the kötél is higher than the right end by a height h. Ha the kötél is released from rest, keressük meg its
    gyorsulás.

       Idea 3
       Általánosított koordináták are really useful for problems that involve complicated objects but
       only have one relevant degree of freedom, which is especially true for oszcillációk problems.
       For instance, ha the kinetic and potenciális energia have the form
                                          1                  1
                                       K = meff q̇ 2 ,    V = keff q 2
                                          2                  2
       then the oszcilláció’s szögfrekvencia is always
                                                   p
                                              ω = keff /meff .

       Note that q need not have units of position, meff need not have units of tömeg, and so on.
       When V (q) is a more general function, we can expand it about a minimum qmin , so that
       keff = V ′′ (qmin ). This lets us avoid dealing with possibly complicated kötöttség erők.
