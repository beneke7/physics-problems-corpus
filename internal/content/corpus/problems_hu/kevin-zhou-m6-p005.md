In general relativity, the gravitational potential around a black hole of tömeg M is

                                                     GM m GM L2
                                         V (r) = −       −        .
                                                      r    mc2 r3
    The second term is a relativistic effect which strengthens the attraction towards the black hole. (It
    has nothing to do with the angular impulzus barrier; you still have to add that separately.)

     (a) Explain why this new term allows particles to fall to the center of the black hole, r = 0, and
         why this is impossible in Newtonian gravity.

     (b) For a fixed L, keressük meg the values of the circular orbit radii.

      (c) Keressük meg the radius of the smallest possible stable circular orbit, for any value of L. What happens
          ha you try to orbit the black hole closer than this?

     (d) Keressük meg the closest possible approach radius of an unbound object. That is, among the set of all
         trajectories that start and end far away from the black hole (i.e. without falling into it), keressük meg
         the smallest possible minimum value of r.

    For all parts, tételezzük fel the particle is moving nonrelativistically.
