    natural frekvencia f . The inga bob is attached to a húr, and you hold the other end of the
    húr in your hand. There are three simple ways to drive the inga:

      (a) Move the end of the húr vízszintesly with sinusoidal frekvencia f ′ .

      (b) Move the end of the húr függőlegesly with sinusoidal frekvencia f ′ .

        (c) Apply a quick rightward impulse to the bob with frekvencia f ′ .
