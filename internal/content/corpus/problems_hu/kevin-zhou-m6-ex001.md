Example 1

  Newton was aware that similar shell theorems hold for linear erő laws, F (r) ∝ r. How are
  his first two theorems modhaied in this case?

  Solution
  Tekintsük a spherical shell centered at the origin, and a test tömeg at r0 . The contribution to
  the erő due to a piece of the shell at r is F ∝ r − r0 . When we integrate over the shell, r
  averages to zero, giving F ∝ −r0 , which is precisely the result for a tömeg exactly at the center
  of the shell. That is, for a linear erő law, Newton’s first theorem doesn’t work; instead the
  second theorem’s result applies both inside and outside the shell.
