Tekintsük a inga whose length adiabatically changes from L to L/2.

     (a) Ha the initial (small) amplitude was θ0 , keressük meg the final amplitude using the adiabatic theorem.

     (b) Give a physical interpretation of the adiabatic invariant.

     (c) When quantum mechanics was being invented, it was proposed that the energy in a inga’s
         oszcilláció was always a multiple of ℏω, ahol ω is the szögfrekvencia. At the first Solvay
         conference of 1911, Lorentz asked whether this condition would be preserved upon slow changes
         in the length of the inga, and Einstein said yes. Reprúduce Einstein’s analysis.
