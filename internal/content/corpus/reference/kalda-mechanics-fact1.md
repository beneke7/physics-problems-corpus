(Newton's $1^{\text {st }}$ law.) While the motion of bodies depends on the reference frame (e.g. a body which moves with a constant velocity in one frame moves with an acceleration in another frame if the relative acceleration of the frames is nonzero), there exist so-called inertial reference frames where the facts $2-5$ are valid for all the bodies.
