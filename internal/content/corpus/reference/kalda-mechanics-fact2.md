(Newton's $2^{\text {nd }}$ law.) In an inertial frame of reference, a non-zero acceleration $\vec{a}$ of a body is always caused by an external influence; each body can be characterized by an inertial mass $m$ (in what follows the adjective "inertial" will be dropped), and each influence can be characterized by a vectorial quantity $\vec{F}$, henceforth referred to as the force, so that equality $\vec{F}=m \vec{a}$ is valid for any influence-body pair.
