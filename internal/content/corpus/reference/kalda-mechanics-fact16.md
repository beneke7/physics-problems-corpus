(Amontons' law.) The maximal static friction force at the contact area of two bodies $F_{\max }=\mu_{s} N$, where $N$ is the normal force at that contact area and $\mu_{s}$ is a constant dependent on the two contacting materials, referred to as the static coefficient of friction; it may also depend on the temperature, humidity, etc. Thus, $F_{\max }$ is independent of the contact area.
