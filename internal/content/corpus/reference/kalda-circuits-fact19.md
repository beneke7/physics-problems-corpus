In a closed circuit consisting of a capacitor $C$ and an inductor $L$, current through the inductor and voltage on the capacitor will oscillate sinusoidally with circular frequency $\omega_{0}=1 / \sqrt{L C}$, e.g. $V=V_{0} \sin \left(\omega_{0} t+\varphi\right) .^{29}$
