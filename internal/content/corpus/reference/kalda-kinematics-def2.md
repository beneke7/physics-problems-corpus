Velocity is a vectorial quantity which can be defined by the projections to the axes $\vec{v}=\left(v_{x}, v_{y}, v_{z}\right)$; speed is the modulus of a vector, $v=|\vec{v}|=\sqrt{v_{x}^{2}+v_{y}^{2}+v_{z}^{2}}$. Similarly, displacement is a vector pointing from the starting point of a body to its final position; travelled distance is the sum of the moduli of all the elementary displacements (the curve length).
