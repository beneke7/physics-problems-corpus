section: "Entropy and Carnot cycle"
kind: fact
---

Any reversible heat engine (not necessarily based on Carnot' cycle) which takes heat from a heat bath at a single fixed temperature $T_{1}$, and gives away heat to another heat bath at another fixed temperature $T_{2}$, must have (and does have) exactly the same efficiency as a Carnot' cycle, $\eta_{C}=1-\frac{T_{2}}{T_{1}}$. For non-ideal (non-reversible) heat engines, the efficiency can be only smaller than $\eta_{C}$ and never larger than that.
