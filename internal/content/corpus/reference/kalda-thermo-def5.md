In order to bring a substance from one phase to another (typically from solid phase to liquid or from liquid phase to gaseous phase), certain amount of heat needs to be given to the substance, which is proportional to the mass $m$ of the substance: $Q=\lambda m$. The factor $\lambda$ is referred to as the latent heat (e.g. latent heat of evaporation or latent heat of melting).
