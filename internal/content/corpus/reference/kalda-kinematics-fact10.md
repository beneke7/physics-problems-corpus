If a function $f(x)$ has a minimum or maximum at $x=x_{0}$ then for small departures $\Delta x$ from $x_{0}$, the variation of the function value $\Delta f=f(x+\Delta x)-f(x)$ remains quadratically small ${ }^{8}$ and can be often neglected.
