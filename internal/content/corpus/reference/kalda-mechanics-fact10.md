Potential energy of two gravitationally interacting spherically symmetric bodies is given by Eq. (8); in the case of homogeneous downwards-directed gravity field of strength $g$, the change of potential energy of a body of mass $m$ is $\Delta \Pi=g \Delta h$, where $h$ is the change of height.
