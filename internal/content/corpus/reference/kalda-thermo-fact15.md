section: "Interface between liquid and vapours"
kind: fact
---

At the interface of two liquids, boiling can start at considerably lower temperatures than in both liquids, separately: boiling will start when the condition $p_{1 s}+p_{2 s}>p_{\text {atm }}$ is satisfied.
