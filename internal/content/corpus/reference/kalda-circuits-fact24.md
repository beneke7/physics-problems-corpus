section: "Alternating current"

Suppose at a certain circular frequency $\omega$, impedance is very large, $\frac{1}{Z(\omega)} \approx 0$. Then, a very small current driven to the leads will give rise to a very large voltage $V=I Z$; this phenomenon is called the voltage resonance. Similarly, if the impedance is very small, $Z(\omega) \approx 0$, we have a current resonance: small input voltage will lead to a large current.
