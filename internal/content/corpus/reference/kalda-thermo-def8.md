section: "Adiabatic processes"
kind: def
---

Adiabatic process is a process by which the system parameters change so slowly that the characteristic time of changing is much longer than the period of the slowest mode of natural oscillations; also, there should be no dissipative processes (where mechanical energy is converted to heat), e.g. friction. In the case of gases, this means that the speed of the container walls needs to be much smaller than the speed of sound, and also there should be no external heat supply.
