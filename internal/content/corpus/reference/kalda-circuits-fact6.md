section: "Circuits with resistors, batteries, ammeters and voltmeters"
kind: fact
---

If all the resistors and battery voltages (the electromotive forces) are known, and currents of the wires are considered as unknown variables then the Kirchoff's laws and the Ohm's law form a closed set of linear equations which can be solved to find all the currents and voltages in the circuit (i.e. the solution is unique).
