---

Interfaces between phases carry energy; each interface is characterized by the surface tension coefficient $\sigma$ which gives the energy per unit area. As a direct consequence, $\sigma$ gives also the force per unit length of a fictitious cut of the interface (known as the surface tension or capillary force).
