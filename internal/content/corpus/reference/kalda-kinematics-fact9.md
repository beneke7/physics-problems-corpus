Each parabola has a focus, the properties of which are most easily expressed in terms of geometrical optics: if the parabola reflects light, all those rays which are parallel to the symmetry axis are reflected to the focus (dashed lines in the figure); due to the Fermat' principle, this means also that for each point on the parabola the distance to the focus plus the distance to the infinitely distant light source are equal, $l_{1}+h_{1}+d_{1}=2 h_{0}+d_{0}=\ldots$; since $d_{0}=d_{1}=\ldots$, we have $2 h_{0}=l_{1}+h_{1}=l_{2}=l_{3}+h_{3}$ (see the figure).
