If a rigid body is rotating about a fixed axis then the acceleration of any of its points has two components: centripetal acceleration $\omega^{2} r=v^{2} / r$ directed towards the axis of rotation and a component perpendicular to it, the tangential acceleration $\epsilon r$. NB! the formula cannot be applied if we deal with an instantaneous rotation axis ${ }^{11}$ (more precisely, the formula can be used if the acceleration of the instantaneous rotation axis is zero). The formula $v^{2} / r$ can be also used for the perpendicular to the motion acceleration of a point along a curved trajectory; then, $r$ is the curvature radius of the trajectory.
