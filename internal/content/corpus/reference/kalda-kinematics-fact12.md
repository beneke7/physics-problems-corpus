if a vector $\vec{a}$ rotates with an angular speed $\vec{\omega}$ so that the rotation axis is perpendicular to $\vec{a}$ then the time derivative $\frac{d \vec{a}}{d t}$ is perpendicular to $\vec{a}$ and $\left|\frac{d \vec{a}}{d t}\right|=\omega a$
