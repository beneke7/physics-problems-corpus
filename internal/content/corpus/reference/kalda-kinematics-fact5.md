Let a point $A$ be situated in a medium where the light propagation speed is $v_{1}$, and point $B$ - in a medium where the speed is $v_{2}$. Then, the light propagates from $A$ to $B$ according to the Snell's law: it refracts at the interface so that the angle between the surface normal and the path forms angles $\alpha_{1}$ and $\alpha_{2}$ (see figure) satisfying equality $\sin \alpha_{1} / \sin \alpha_{2}=v_{1} / v_{2}$.
