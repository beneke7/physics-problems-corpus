(Coulomb's friction law.) When two bodies move with respect to each other, the friction force at the contact area of these bodies $F=\mu_{k} N$, where $N$ is the normal force at that contact area and $\mu_{k}$ is a constant dependent on the two contacting materials, referred to as the kinetic coefficient of friction; it does depend slightly on the slipping speed, but this dependence is weak and typically ignored.
